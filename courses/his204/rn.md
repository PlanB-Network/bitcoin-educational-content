# Urugendo mu mateka y'ivy'ubutunzi vy'umwidegemvyo

Iki cigwa citohoza ukuvuka kw'ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi mu Bufaransa bwo mu kinjana ca 18, tubicishije mu ndorerwamo ya laissez-faire. Uzobona ingene abanyavyiyumviro barwanije mercantilism, bavuga ko Leta ikwiye gukingira uburenganzira bwite bw'abantu, ariko ntihindagure amasoko biciye ku mabwirizwa.

Uzokwiga ingene abahinduye ibintu bafise iyerekwa baguye iryo ciyumviro, basaba ubudandaji bwidegemvya n'ugutembera kw'ibicuruzwa ata nzitiro, bashinga imishinge y'ivyiyumviro vya capitalisme ya kino gihe. Mu kwiga abo bahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'ubutsimvyi, uzotahura ingingo ngenderwako zitajajuka zabumbye ubutunzi bwa politike kw'isi yose kandi zibandanya zigira ico zivuze mu mpaka z'ingingo mfatirwako z'uyu munsi. Iyandikishe ubu, umenye neza ivyiyumviro vyubatse ubutunzi bwa kino gihe.

### Kaze

Kaze muri HIS 204! Iki cigwa, citangwa na **[Benoît Malbranque](https://planb.academy/professors/benoit-malbranque)**, umukuru w'Institut Coppet kandi umwe mu bahinga ba mbere mu muco w'umwidegemvyo w'Abafaransa, kitohoza inkomoko y'Abafaransa y'iciyumviro ca *laissez-faire* nk'uko catewe imbere mu kinjana ca 18 biciye ku muco utoshe w'ivyiyumviro.

Imbere y'igihe kirekire ko Adam Smith asohora [*Wealth of Nations*](https://planb.academy/resources/books/the-wealth-of-nations-c3e78eda-cc44-4cae-8460-f962148aa289), abanyavyiyumviro b'Abafaransa bariko bubaka ubutunzi bwa politike bushingiye ku mwidegemvyo mu vy'ubutunzi, ku kwiyubara ukwivanga kwa Leta, n'ukwizera urutonde kamere rufasha iterambere n'ukworoherwa. Mu gukurikirana ivyiyumviro vyabo kuva kuri Vauban na Boisguilbert gushika kuri Turgot na Condorcet, uzomenya umwandu w'ivyiyumviro wibagiwe wabumbye isi ya kino gihe.

### Ivyo uzokwiga

- **Kurikirana ukuvuka kwa laissez-faire** kuva ku babanjirije ba mbere bo mu ngoma ya Louis XIV gushika ku kwiserura kwayo kwuzuye mu ishure ry'ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy.
- **Menya abanyavyiyumviro b'ishimikiro** barwanije mercantilism n'ubutegetsi bw'igitugu bwuzuye: Vauban, Boisguilbert, Cantillon, Quesnay, Turgot, Condillac, n'abandi.
- **Tahura ivyiyumviro birwanya ukwivanga kwa Leta** ivyo abo bahinga mu vy'ubutunzi bateje imbere, kuva ku kunegura amakori gushika ku kuburanira ubudandaji bwidegemvya.
- **Sesengura uburyo bwa Physiocracy** n'iciyumviro cabwo gihindura vyose c'uko uburimyi, atari ubudandaji, ari isoko ry'ubutunzi bw'igihugu.
- **Suzuma ingaruka zamaho z'ivyiyumviro vya laissez-faire** ku butunzi bwa politike kw'isi yose no ku mpaka zibandanya zibumba ingingo mfatirwako z'ubutunzi z'uyu munsi.
- **Menya ukudahuza kuri hagati y'inyigisho n'ingene ishirwa mu ngiro** biciye ku nkuru y'ivyahinduwe na Turgot n'ingene vyanse mu Bufaransa imbere y'Impinduramatwara.

### Urutonde rw'ivyigwa

**Igice ca 2: Ababanjirije.** Turatangura ku mpera z'ikinjana ca 17 n'intango y'ica 18, igihe carangwa n'ingoma y'umwami ifise ubutegetsi bwose n'akamo rya mbere ryasaba uburyo bushingiye ku bwenge mu kuyobora ivy'igihugu. Biciye kuri Vauban, Boisguilbert na Cantillon, turabona ukunegura kwa mbere ukwivanga kwa Leta n'ishusho ya mbere y'ico hanyuma cabaye ubutunzi bwa politike bw'umwidegemvyo.

**Igice ca 3: Abahinduye ibintu n'abanyavyiyumviro bo mu ntango y'ikinjana ca 18.** Iki gice cibanda ku bagerageje guhindura ubutunzi bw'Ubufaransa hisunzwe ingingo ngenderwako nshasha. Abanyavyiyumviro nka Abbé wa Saint-Pierre, Marquis d'Argenson na Gournay basavye ko inzitiro z'amashirahamwe y'abakozi zisenywa, ko ubudandaji ruhabwa umwidegemvyo, kandi ko ihiganwa rishigikirwa nk'urufunguruzo rw'iterambere. Ivyo baciye bavuga badatinya vyabanjirije ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy.

**Igice ca 4: Ishure ry'ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy.** Turatohoza kimwe mu bihe vy'ishimikiro cane mu vyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi vy'Abafaransa. Turihweza inkomoko, imishinge y'inyigisho n'ibikorwa nyamukuru vy'aba Physiocrats, twibanda kuri Quesnay na Dupont de Nemours. Iciyumviro ca laissez-faire caba ng'aho uburyo bwifatanye, bushingiye ku ciyumviro c'urutonde kamere Leta itegerezwa kwumvira.

**Igice ca 5: Ikiringo c'Ubwenge n'ubutunzi bwa politike.** Amaherezo, turabona ingene ivyiyumviro vy'umwidegemvyo mu vy'ubutunzi vyakwiragiye mu muhuriro munini w'Ikiringo c'Ubwenge. Voltaire, Turgot, Condillac na Condorcet baraguye umuco wa laissez-faire, bashinga ikiraro kigana igihe c'Impinduramatwara no kuhagana imbere.

Witeguye kumenya bushasha imizi y'Abafaransa y'umwidegemvyo mu vy'ubutunzi? Tugende!

## Kahise

### Ubufaransa mu ntango y'ikinjana ca 18

Mu ntango y'ikinjana ca 18, Ubufaransa bwari mu kibazo giteye amakenga. Abanyagihugu bo mu makungu ntibashobora kwimbura ivyari bikwiye kubatunga, kandi bariko baciwe amakori menshi.

![Ishusho y'ubuzima bwo mu makungu mu kinjana ca 19, bivugwa ko yakozwe na Louis-Léopold Boilly](assets/en/001.webp)


Abanyabukorikori bo mu bisagara, bapfunzwe mu mashirahamwe y'abakozi atagira uruvugiro, ntibashobora guhingura ibishasha canke mbere gufashanya. Ico gihe nyene, ayandi makungu y'i Buraya ntiyatevye kurengura Ubufaransa mu vyose, arushiriza ibicuruzwa vyabo. Uburoranirwa mu budandaji bw'Ubwongereza n'Ubuholandi bwari mu muzirikanyi wa bose.

Ariko none uwo mugera w'ico gihe wari wotorerwa umuti gute? Ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi ntibwari bukiriho, ni co gituma nta muti wihariye wari wo gukoresha. Ingingo ngenderwako z'ingingo mfatirwako z'ubutunzi zakoreshwa ku bwotoro, zihindukira hagati y'ibihe vy'inzitiro n'ibihe vy'umwidegemvyo. Ni ukuri, twari dufise uburorero, ivyo mu kahise vyo gukurikira. Muri vyo hari *Sully*, umushikiranganji wa Henri IV, yaharanira uburimyi kandi yateza imbere umwidegemvyo mwinshi w'ubudandaji mu Bufaransa.

![Ishusho ya Duc de Sully, Ivyibutswa vya Duc de Sully, Umushikiranganji wa Henri IV](assets/en/002.webp)



Ariko hanyuma haza Colbert, umushikiranganji wa Louis XIV, yajajura hagati y'amabwirizwa n'umwidegemvyo, ariko amaherezo yashimikiye ku mabwirizwa. Ku mpera nyene z'ikinjana ca 17, Colbert yararenguye Sully: abashikiranganji baciye bavuga umwandu wiwe kandi bagerageza gushira mu ngiro ivyo bavuga ko ari impanuro ziwe.

![Jean-Baptiste Colbert, Umugenzuzi Mukuru w'Ivy'Imari mu ngoma ya Louis XIV](assets/en/003.webp)

### Impanuro zine za Colbert

Mu vyiyumviro vy'abatwara igihugu ku mpera z'ikinjana ca 17, impanuro za Colbert zari zine.

(1) **Inganda zitegerezwa kugengwa n'amabwirizwa kandi zipfungirwe mu mashirahamwe y'abakozi**. Ayo mabwirizwa yerekana, nk'akarorero, ingene amashuka n'impuzu bitegerezwa gukorwa, ubunini n'uburemere bwavyo.
Hariho amajana y'ayo mategeko, yakoranijwe mu bitabu vyihariye ku bwoko bwose bw'inganda. Yamara, mu nyonga z'abayoboke ba Colbert, ivyo ntivyari bihagije: inganda, nk'uko biyumvira, zitegerezwa kandi kugenzurwa n'amashirahamwe y'abakozi.

![Uruganda rw'umuhinguzi w'ibikozwe mu moya mu kinjana ca 18](assets/en/004.webp)

Uwushaka gukora umwuga yategerezwa kubanza kumara imyaka myinshi yiga uwo mwuga, hanyuma agakora nk'umukozi asanzwe, imbere yuko agerageza gushika ku rwego rw'umuhinga mukuru mu gukora "igikorwa c'akadasanzwe" no gutanga amahera menshi ku ishirahamwe ry'abakozi. Ihiganwa muri umwuga wose ryari rero rigabanijwe bimwe bikomeye.


(2) **Ubudandaji ni umukino aho ico umwe aronka ari ico uwundi ahomba**. Ku bijanye n'ubudandaji, abayoboke ba Colbert bari bafise ivyiyumviro vy'ubuhumye nk'ivy'abantu b'ubugome bo mu bihe vya kera. Nk'uko umushikiranganji wa Louis XIV abivuga, ubudandaji ni "intambara idahera". Kubera iki? Imvo iroroshe: kuri Colbert n'abamusubiriye, ubutunzi bwose bwiyongereye mu gihugu kimwe vyasobanura ubukene mu kindi gihugu. Mu vyiyumviro vyabo, Abongereza canke Abaholandi ntibari bakwiye kwemererwa gutunga, kuko ivyo vyosobanura ko banyaga ukworoherwa kw'Ubufaransa.

![Ikivuko ca Bordeaux mu kinjana ca 18, casizwe na Vernet](assets/en/005.webp)

Ibicuruzwa vy'ivyo bihugu, ni co gituma, vyategerezwa kubuzwa canke kucibwa amakori menshi, ata kwiyumvira kabiri, kuko ubudandaji ni intambara aho dushobora kwipfuza gusa ko abansi bacu bahonywa.

> Abafaransa ntibashobora kwongereza ubudandaji bwabo atari mu guhonya Abaholandi.
> *Colbert*

(3) **Iyo Leta yabuze amahera, niyongereze amakori**. Colbert n'abayoboke biwe ntibiyumvira namba ko ubutunzi bw'abatanga amakori ari ikintu kigira akagero. Mu vyiyumviro vyabo, ugukoresha amahera ya Leta ntivyoshobora kuba ikibazo, igihe cose amahera akwiye yakoranijwe. Kandi nimba abantu bagararije, vyari kubera gusa ko abashikiranganji batabikoze neza, kuko, nk'uko Colbert yabivuze ku bugombe, "ubuhinga bwo kucisha amakori ni ukwambura inyoni amoya ata kuzitera gutakamba cane".

![Robin Hood wa Leta - amakori canke urupfu](assets/en/006.webp)

(4) **Ubutunzi ni, imbere ya vyose, inzahabu n'ifeza**. Imbere yuko ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi buvuka, abanditsi benshi bakurikira inyigisho imwe yaganza ku bijanye n'ubwoko bw'ubutunzi, iyahavuye yitwa *mercantilism*. Colbert n'abamusubiriye baciye babandanya kuri iyo nzira. Muri make, abakurikira mercantilism biyumvira ko ikimenyetso nyakuri c'ukworoherwa kw'igihugu ari ukwegeranya ubutare bw'agaciro, ifeza n'inzahabu.

![Uncle Scrooge](assets/en/007.webp)


> Ubwinshi bw'amahera muri Leta bwonyene ni bwo butuma iba nini kandi ifise ububasha.
> *Colbert*

Inkurikizi y'ico ciyumviro ni ugushira imbere ibicuruzwa bijana mu mahanga uko byose bishika, kuko vyinjiza inzahabu n'ifeza z'amahanga, no kugabanya bimwe bikomeye ibicuruzwa binjira, kwirinda kurungika izo nzahabu mu mahanga.

### Imishinge y'ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi

Izo ni ingingo ngenderwako zine zayoboye ubutegetsi bw'Ubufaransa mu myaka mirongo, mu gihe igihugu cinjira mu kinjana ca 18. Yamara, ntibitevye ko zihurirwako bimwe bikomeye. Hagati ya 1690 na 1710, abanditsi benshi barakozwe cane ku mutima n'ukuntu Ubufaransa bwari bubi. Mu kurondera imvo zavyo, bashitse ku ciyumviro c'uko impanuro nyene zarazwe na Colbert ari zo zibitera, babona ko ari ivyiyumviro vy'ubuhendanyi gusa. Mu kubigenza gutyo, barashinze imishinge y'ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi.

## Vauban

<chapterId>ee9c1e0e-96cc-5026-a5e2-963d68122786</chapterId>

### Umugaba mukuru yacitse umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi

Uno musi, mu gihe igitutu c'amakori mu gihugu cacu kibandanya cikwiragira, kigira kaze guhagarika ububasha bw'ivy'ubutunzi bw'igihugu, amajambo asaba ihinduka aragenda aravugwa. Baba babizi canke batabizi, ayo makamo asaba ihinduka akenshi asubiramwo ibikorwa vy'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'Abafaransa, abo, kuva mu kinjana ca 17, banegura uburyo bw'amakori bwo mu gihugu babona ko butagira urutonde, bufise igitugu kandi burengeje urugero.

Uwa mbere muri abo bahinduye ivy'amakori, ku bijanye n'igihe no ku bijanye n'ubushobozi, ni umugaba mukuru w'ingabo Sébastien Le Prestre Vauban, uwaronse izina mu kwubaka ibirindiro n'ibigo vy'ingabo.

![Ishusho ya Vauban, Ikigo c'ingabo ca Lille cubatswe na Vauban](assets/en/008.webp)



Naho biteye agahinda ko twavyibagiye, Vauban ntiyari umusirikare gusa; yari kandi umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi. Yaritwararitse **ibituma abarimyi bageramiwe kandi yaciye avuga ivyo guhindura ku vy'amakori atadohotse** mu 1695 (Projet de capitation - "Umugambi w'ikori rimwe ku muntu") no mu 1707 (Projet d'une Dime Royale - "Icumi c'Umwami"): ni ukuvuga gusubiriza amakori menshi yariho n'ikori riharurwa ku kigero c'ivyinjira, ikori ry'ijanisha rimwe imbere y'igihe cavyo.

![Vauban, “Royal tithe project”](assets/en/009.webp)

Vauban yashitse kuri ivyo vyiyumviro biciye ku kwipfuza kumenya. Yari umuntu yitegereza cane, yihatira kwiga ubuzima bw'imibano n'ukuri kw'ivy'ubutunzi mu buryo butoranguye, ashigikira ku bumenyi. Yashimikiye canecane ku kuba hakenewe guharura, biciye ku mabarura y'abantu.

![Ibarura ry'abantu](assets/en/010.webp)

### Ishusho iteye ubwoba y'amagorwa y'Abafaransa

Ubushobozi bwiwe bwa kabiri bukuru bwari insiguro yiwe ikora ku mutima kandi y'ukuri y'amagorwa abantu benshi bashikirwa. Yanditse: "Ntitwihende; umutima w'ubwami warahonye. Vyose birababara, vyose birihanganira, vyose birinuba. Uragira ngo urabe kandi wihweze umutima w'intara, ico uzosanga kirushiriza kuba kibi ku vyo ndiko ndavuga". Kure y'ukurenza urugero, ivyo Vauban yitegereza biteye agahinda vyari ishusho nyayo y'ubuzima bwo mu ntango y'ikinjana ca 18. Alexis de Tocqueville yari azi neza ivyo vyiyumviro, kandi yahavuye avuga ko *Royal Tithe* ya Vauban "iteye ubwoba", kuko yari y'ukuri.

![Tocqueville, “The Old Regime and the Revolution”, Amashami atatu y'igihugu](assets/en/011.webp)

Ikindi Vauban akwiye guhayagizwa nk'umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi ni uko yaciye avuga ihinduka rikomeye ry'amakori rigamije gutsinda, canke n'imiburiburi kugabanya, ububi yabonye kandi yasiguye. Yari afise imvo yo kubigira; ubutunzi bw'Ubufaransa mu ngoma ya Ancien Régime bwari bwapfuye kubera amakori atangana, atarama kandi atumvikana.


Mu nyandiko ziwe zitandukanye z'ivya politike n'ivy'ubutunzi, intumbero nkuru ya Vauban yari kugabanya imibabaro y'ico yita "igice co hasi c'abantu, abo, biciye ku kazi kabo, batunga kandi bashigikira abo hejuru".





Yatahuye ko amakori acinyiza kandi aca intege yari yarenze urugero ku barimyi, iciyumviro yerekanye mu buryo butomoye, ikintu tubandanya tubona neza mu bihe vyacu:

> Umurimyi areka agatongo gatoyi afise gapfa, akarima ata mutima, atinya ko nimba kimbuye ivyo cari gushobora kwimbura hakoreshejwe ifumbire n'uburimyi bukwiye, cocibwa amakori arushirije kuba menshi.

Vauban yabonye ukuri: amakori mu ngoma ya Ancien Régime ntiyari gusa atashingiye ku bwenge, yari kandi akomeye mu buryo bw'ubugarariji. Ni ubwo buryo bw'amakori, butagira ubutungane mu kugabura umutwaro, yagomba gutsinda.

### Ikori ry'ijanisha rimwe imbere y'igihe cavyo

Umuti yaciye avuga, ikori ry'ijanisha rimwe riharurwa ku vyinjira vyose, cotumye umutwaro w'amakori ugaburwa mu butungane ku miryango yose y'abantu. Rishingiye ku nyigisho ya Leta ibona ko ubutegetsi bwa rusangi ari nkenerwa mu kukingira uburenganzira bw'umuntu ku giti ciwe n'umutungo wiwe, ihinduka ry'amakori rya Vauban ryasaba ko abenegihugu bose batanga umuganda ushingiye neza ku vyo bironkera, nk'akarorero, 10% vy'ivyo binjira

Muri The Royal Tithe, igitabu ciwe c'ivy'ubutunzi conyene casohowe akiriho, Vauban yavuze atomora:

> Kubera ko umuntu wese ari muri Leta akenera ukingirwa kwayo kugira abeho, ni vyiza ko bose batanga umuganda, bivanye n'ivyo binjira, mu kuyitunga no mu gukoresha amahera yayo [...]. Nta kintu kirenza ukutagira ubutungane nk'ugusonera abashoboye cane kuriha, no kwimurira umutwaro ku badashoboye, abo bikoroza uburemere; uburemere bwoba butoyi cane iyo bwikorwa n'abantu bose bivanye n'inkomezi za umwumwe. Ni co gituma, ugusonera amakori kwose ni ukudatunganira kutegerezwa kutorerwa umuti.

Imbere gato yuko apfa, iciyumviro ca Vauban carahawe ikibanza n'abashikiranganji ba Louis XIV. Yamara, Vauban yari yasavye ko ikori riharurwa ku kigero c'ivyinjira risubirira amakori yose, canke hafi ya yose, yariho. Aho kubigenza gutyo, nk'ukuntu bikunda kugenda, ikori ryiwe ryarashizwe mu ngiro, mugabo ayandi yose yarabizwe.

## Boisguilbert

<chapterId>200149c6-b5fc-566e-ab0e-bafb1c3fed3c</chapterId>

### Umutsimvyi yibagiwe

Abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'Abafaransa bo mu kahise ni bake bahabwa mu gihugu cabo uno musi iteka ringana n'ivyo bakoze, kandi Boisguilbert ntacitandukanije na bo.

![Pierre Lepesant de Boisguilbert](assets/en/012.webp)

Ata kibanza mu bamusoma bo mu gihe ciwe, akurwa kure y'uduce vy'ubutegetsi kubera kamere yiwe idasanzwe n'umwete wiwe urenze urugero, Boisguilbert yasize ikimenyetso gitoyi ku kinjana ca 18. Yamara, mu ntango y'ikinjana giheruka, ibikorwa vyiwe vyagiye biramenyekana buhorobuhoro bushasha.

Yamara, ukwo kumenyekana bushasha kwerekana ko turiko turinjira mu nzira itagira ikirangiro. Ubushobozi nyakuri bwa Boisguilbert bwarazimiye kubera ko bamwerekana nk'umutsimvyi w'inyigisho nyinshi n'uwabanjirije abanyavyiyumviro benshi. Bavuga ko yatahuye iciyumviro c'ukubura akazi guhagije Keynes yahavuye aburanira, ko yabanjirije itegeko rya Say, ko yugururiye inzira inyigisho ya Walras y'iringanira rusangi, kandi ko mbere yabanjirije isesengura ry'imirwi y'abantu ry'aba Marxists. 'Boisguilbert ntiyoba yabanjirije nde canke iki?' ni co uwutanga iciyumviro yahavuye abaza.


(Pierre Le Pesant de) Boisguilbert yavukiye i Rouen mu 1646. Yize kuri Port-Royal i Paris, Boisguilbert yatanguye umwuga w'ubwanditsi utaroraniye imbere yuko afata amabanga atandukanye, harimwo n'irya Lieutenant-General ya Rouen. Ni muri ico gihe yanditse ibitabu vyinshi vyo kuburanira ivyiyumviro vyiwe, muri vyo "Détail de la France" mu 1695, iyo yasubiye gusohora umwaka ukurikira ku zina rirushirije kuba ritomoye: [*France Ruined under the Reign of Louis XIV, by Whom and How, with the Means to Restore It*](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_0jUaWNbTJa8C/page/n23/mode/2up).

![Boisguilbert, “Ruined France under Louis XIV”, Ikarata ya Port Royal des Champs](assets/en/013.webp)



Kandi ico ni co shimikiro c'ibitabu vya Boisguilbert: ubukene bw'Abafaransa n'imvo zabwo.

Ayo magorwa y'Abafaransa ku mpera z'ikinjana ca 17, Boisguilbert arayasigura mu buryo burambuye, nk'uko Vauban yabigize. Yanditse:

> Amatongo yumye canke arimwe nabi, abonwa na bose, ni umuvyimba w'Ubufaransa.

Yasiguye imirima y'inzabibu yarandurwa, abarimyi baheba amatongo, n'ikigoyi kigaruka. 

### Imvo zibiri z'uguhona kw'Ubufaransa

Boisguilbert aronka imvo zibiri nyamukuru z'ico goryo. Kuko nimba abantu babayeho mu bukene bukabije, ni kubera ko babuzwa gukoresha ivyo bikenewe, kandi uguhona kw'ugukoresha ibintu kufise imvo zibiri.

Ubwa mbere, abantu ntibashobora kuronka ivyo bikenewe imbere ya vyose kubera amakori aciwe ku bwotoro. Taille, ikori ry'umuntu ku giti ciwe ryo mu ico gihe, ryaharurwa ku buhumye ku muntu wese, ryiyongera canke rigabanuka ata mvo. Kubera uburenganzira bwihariye bwinshi, umutwaro waguye ku barimyi bakene, baciye bahona. Kugira ngo ivyo bitorerwe umuti, Boisguilbert yasavye ikori riharurwa ku kigero c'ivyinjira vyose, nk'ico Vauban yavuze.

Imvo ya kabiri y'amagorwa y'Ubufaransa ni uko inzitiro nyinshi zabuza ubudandaji bwidegemvya bw'ibicuruzwa, canecane ibivuye mu burimyi. Hariho amakori yo ku mipaka kandi mbere no mu gihugu imbere, hagati y'intara zitandukanye, ivyo vyapfisha ubudandaji bwose. Izo nzitiro zabuza ko igiciro c'iringanira rishingwa kandi zagabanya uburyo bwo kwidandariza. Ivyo vyatuma abarimyi badashobora kubaho ku vyo bimbuye, kuko ntibashobora kugurira inyungu kandi bagerwako n'ibiciro vy'ibivuye mu burimyi bidatanga inyungu, ikibazo kibandanya kigira intumbero cane uno musi kandi kiri mu mutima w'inyigisho ya Boisguilbert. Ku bijanye n'inzitiro ku budandaji, Boisguilbert yaharaniye ko amabarabara akurwako inzitiro, ni ukuvuga ko ubudandaji bwidegemvya bushingwa.

![Gustave Caillebotte - "Upward Path”](assets/en/014.webp)

### Akamo rya mbere risaba laissez-faire

Kandi umwidegemvyo ni wo iciyumviro ciwe ca nyuma. "Ntibigira ico gukora", avuga, "ariko gusa ni uguheba gukora nk'ukuntu tubigira mu bukazi ku kamere, iyo ihora yerekera ku mwidegemvyo n'ubutunganirizo". Vyose bizogenda neza, asubiramwo atiganwa, "igihe cose tureka kamere ikagenda mu nzira yayo, ni ukuvuga tuyihaye umwidegemvyo wayo, kandi ata muntu yivanga muri ubwo budandaji atari mu gukingira bose no mu kubuza urugomo".

Ico kigabane ca nyuma ni c'ishimikiro. **Boisguilbert ni we wa mbere yasavye atomora ingingo mfatirwako y'ubutunzi ya laissez-faire**, ayigira icizigiro ciwe, kandi yubaka uburyo nyabwo bwifatanye kuri yo. Nk'uko avuga, hariho urutonde kamere rw'ibintu, kandi ntirukwiye guhindurwa, ngo runasenyurwe, n'ukwivanga kwa Leta kutari ku gihe. Leta, mu vyiyumviro vyiwe, ntikwiye kwigira ico ku bijanye n'ivy'ubutunzi, ahubwo ireke ibintu bigende kamere. Bitagenze gutyo, izozana amagorwa.

Boisguilbert yaranenguye mbere "*abantu b'imitima myiza*", nk'uko yabita, abo bagomba iciza mugabo bagira nabi cane. Bipfuza ko abantu baronka umukate ku giciro gito, mugabo mu gukurura hasi ibiciro, barahonya abarimyi, batashobora kubaho kuri iyo nyungu ntoyi. Abo barimyi baciye baheba amatongo yabo, bagwa mu bukene burushirije. Nk'uko twese tuzi, 'inzira igana mu muriro udahera kenshi ibambwa n'ivyipfuzo vyiza'.

## Cantillon

<chapterId>bc206d41-6a64-5688-a489-40fcfa0e5397</chapterId>

### Umunyabanki w'Umunya-Irlande yashinze ivy'ubutunzi vya kino gihe

Umwanditsi w'igitabu "Essay on the Nature of Trade in General" (canditswe ahagana mu 1730, casohowe mu 1755), Richard Cantillon abonwa nk'umwe mu batsimvyi b'ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi bwa kino gihe. Mu gitabu ciwe History of Economic Thought, umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi Murray Rothbard aravuga mbere ko Cantillon ari we yashinze ivy'ubutunzi vya kino gihe.

![Murray Rothbard, “An Austrian perspective on the History of Economic Thought”](assets/en/015.webp)

Yavukiye muri Irlande, Richard Cantillon yaje kuba i Paris akiri umusore kandi yaronse ubwenegihugu bw'Ubufaransa. Yakoze nk'umunyabanki kandi yaronse itunga ryinshi mu gihe ca John Law.

![Richard Cantillon, “Essay on the Nature of Commerce”, John Law](assets/en/016.webp)

Ni na ho nyene yatanguye kwiga inyigisho y'ivy'ubutunzi. Ahagana mu 1730, Cantillon yanditse igitabu ciwe Essay on the Nature of Commerce in General.



Ico gitabu gishobora kubonwa nk'umwe mu bigerageza vya mbere vyo gushinga inyigisho rusangi y'ivy'ubutunzi. Cantillon yitwararika kwerekana ico yita "amategeko rusangi y'ivy'ubutunzi", ayari mu kamere y'ibintu, atari mu bintu vyihariye vy'iki canke ico gihugu. Ubwo buryo bwari buhindura vyose.

### Ibintu bitanu Cantillon yazaniye ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi

Dushobora kwegeranya mu duce dutanu ibihambaye biri mu gitabu Essay ca Cantillon: inyigisho y'ubutunzi, iciyumviro c'umurwizatunga, ukunegura amahera ata gaciro, "Ingaruka za Cantillon", n'ubwa nyuma ukuburanira umwidegemvyo.

Ubwa mbere, **inyigisho yiwe y'ubutunzi ishingiye ku kazi no kuri kamere**.
Bitandukanye n'iciyumviro ca mercantilism caganza ico gihe, Cantillon yashinze isesengura ryiwe ku kwemera ko ubutunzi bugizwe n'ibicuruzwa bikwiye kunezereza umuntu. Ubwo butunzi, nk'uko avuga, buva kuri kamere kandi buhingurwa biciye ku kazi k'abantu. Ivyiyumviro vyiwe ku bijanye n'ubwoko bw'ubutunzi vyagize ingaruka nkomeye kuri Beccaria na Adam Smith, kandi, biciye kuri Smith, ku ishure ryose ry'ivyiyumviro vya kera vy'Abongereza.

Ubwa kabiri, **umurwizatunga nk'umukozi w'ishimikiro mu vy'ubutunzi**.
Naho ntiyabisiguye neza, Cantillon yabona umurwizatunga nk'umukinyi mukuru w'ishimikiro mu bikorwa vy'ubutunzi. Kuri Cantillon, ikimuranga ni uko yemera akaga kandi akora atazi neza ivyo bizoba. Ivyo vyiyumviro Turgot yahavuye avyagura, na canecane Say, gushika aho umurwizatunga ahabwa ikibanza ciwe kidasanzwe mu vy'ubutunzi, ivyo ubu bitandukanye n'ivyo ishure ry'Abongereza ryemeza.

Ubwa gatatu, **akaga k'amahera ata gaciro**.
Mu kwishura ivyabaye ku John Law, Cantillon asigura ivyo biba canke bitegerezwa kuba igihe amahera ata gaciro nyakuri afise.

![Impapuro y'amahera ya banki ya Law](assets/en/017.webp)

Abona ingaruka zibiri nyamukuru zo gusubiriza amahera ata gaciro nyakuri, nk'amahera y'impapuro, ku mahera y'ubutare. Ingaruka ya mbere ni ico yita "ukwanka kw'abantu benshi", ni ukuvuga ukudakwizigira amahera ata gaciro gukwiragira. Ingaruka ya kabiri ni [iyiduga ry'ibiciro](https://planb.academy/resources/glossary/inflation): ukugabanuka kw'agaciro k'amahera gutuma ibicuruzwa bihenda.

Ubwa kane, mu isesengura ryiwe ry'**ingaruka zitangana z'iyiduga ry'ibiciro**, Cantillon yagiye kure kuruta abo bari kumwe. Amaze kwitegereza uguhirika kw'uburyo bwa Law, Cantillon yatahuye ko iyiduga ry'ibiciro ritagirira ingaruka zingana ku bantu bose. Ahubwo riho, ritunga bamwe mugabo rikenesha abandi. Yashitse ku ciyumviro c'uko iyiduga ry'ibiciro rifise ingaruka yo kugabura ubutunzi ukundi: abaronka bwa mbere amahera mashasha yasohotse baronka ubushobozi bwo kugura burushirije, mu gihe abaronka nyuma bagenda barakena kubera amahera mashasha yasohowe biciye ku kuduga kw'ibipimo vy'iyiduga ry'ibiciro.

Ubwa gatanu, naho hasigaye ibimenyetso bike vy'ivyiyumviro vya mercantilism, iciyumviro rusangi ca Cantillon cari **c'umwidegemvyo wuzuye mu kuburanira umutungo bwite**. Yaburaniye umutungo bwite nk'inkingi y'ishimikiro y'imico y'abantu, avuga ko nta mibano ishobora kugenda ata mutungo bwite w'amatongo n'ivyimburwa mu kazi. Yabona kandi ko ukutangana kw'ubutunzi hagati y'abantu ari ikintu ca kamere kandi kidacumuye. Nk'uko Cantillon abivuga, nta kibi kiri mu kuba umukozi w'umwete kandi w'intwari, canke afise impano idasanzwe, aronka amahera kuruta uwutazi akazi canke umunebwe.  Ubwa nyuma, Cantillon yiyumvira ko ibiciro bitegerezwa guhora bishingwa mu mwidegemvyo, biciye ku mukino w'ivyo bitangwa n'ivyo bisabwa, ata kwivanga kw'ubutegetsi bwa rusangi.

### Ingaruka ya Cantillon

Muri ivyo vyiyumviro bitanu bikomeye vyo mu gitabu ciwe Essay, ic'ishimikiro cane ni, ata gukeka, ico ubu cambaye izina ryiwe: **Ingaruka ya Cantillon**.
Biciye kuri iyo nyigisho ku ngaruka z'iyiduga ry'ibiciro, Cantillon aduha inyishu ku ngorane nyinshi z'iki gihe. Idufasha kutahura inkurikizi z'ingingo mfatirwako z'amahera ziheruka zo kwagura amahera no kuduza ibiciro, izo zakenesheje abari mu rugero rwo hagati n'abo mu makungu, mu gihe zatunze abakora ku masoko y'imari na Leta, ibigo vyayo n'abakozi bayo, kubera ko bose bari hafi y'isoko ry'amahera mashasha asohorwa: amabanki nkuru n'amabanki y'ubudandaji.


# Abahinduye ibintu n'abanyavyiyumviro bo mu ntango y'ikinjana ca 18

<partId>1f7b50d4-ce93-5db3-8396-43c1fa5419ff</partId>

## Abbé wa Saint-Pierre

<chapterId>13478fe2-4c12-593c-a410-54c2cfb7ef7f</chapterId>

### Uwuharanira amahoro yanditse cane mu gihe c'intambara

Muri abanditsi bose twatoye kushira mu rutonde rw'abanyavyiyumviro b'Abafaransa ba laissez-faire bo mu kinjana ca 18, Abbé wa Saint-Pierre ni we, ata gukeka, yirengagijwe cane.

![Abbé wa Saint-Pierre](assets/en/018.webp)

Mu kuvugisha ukuri, igice c'ico kosa ni ciwe. Yanditse cane, mugabo uburyo bwiwe bwo kwandika bugoye kusoma kandi bwuzuye ivyisubiramwo. Mbere na Jean-Jacques Rousseau yagerageje kwegeranya ibikorwa vyiwe: yatanguye kubigira, mugabo ntiyatevye kubiheba, kuko yabonye ko birenze inkomezi ziwe. Mu kiringo co hagati mu kinjana ca 19, Gustave de Molinari yamuhaye iteka mu kusohora igitabu kirambuye kimwerekeye, aho yahayagije uwuharanira amahoro n'umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi Abbé wa Saint-Pierre yari. Mugabo ivyo ntivyahagije ngo bimukure mu kwibagirwa, kuko aracaguma muri kwo uno musi.

![Ishusho ya Gustave de Molinari, Abbé wa Saint-Pierre canditswe na Molinari](assets/en/019.webp)



Abbé wa Saint-Pierre yanditse ku vy'ubutunzi, mugabo akenshi abonwa nk'uwuharanira amahoro. Ni umwanditsi w'igitabu Project for Perpetual Peace, cabanjirije ico kizwi cane ca Emmanuel Kant.

![Abbé wa Saint-Pierre, “Project to Restore Peace in Europe”](assets/en/020.webp)

Muri ico gitabu, yavuze ko intambara isenya, atari gusa ku batsinzwe, mugabo no ku batsinze, kandi mbere no ku batayigira, kuko ubudandaji bwabo bwononekara.

Kugira arwanye icago c'intambara, yasavye ko hashingwa nk'Ishirahamwe ry'Amakungu ry'i Buraya. Hari kwubakwa inama y'i Buraya yotorera umuti ingorane z'igihugu cose. Amakungu yari kwitura ubukemurampaka, kugira ngo yirinde kwitura ibirwanisho. Igihugu kitagira ubwenge buhagije ngo cakire amahoro, iyo cageramiye ayandi makungu y'i Buraya, ishirahamwe ry'amakungu y'i Buraya ryari kugira uburyo bwo kwishura. Imbere y'ivyo bintu, hari kwubakwa ingabo z'i Buraya, zigizwe n'abasirikare bavuye mu bihugu bitandukanye.

### Yirukanywe kubera yashize ubutinyuka bwo kunegura Louis XIV

Hari kandi ikintu mu buzima bwa Abbé wa Saint-Pierre cerekana neza kamere yo kunegura iri inyuma y'urunani rw'Abafaransa rwa laissez-faire. Yinjiye mu Académie Française mu 1695, mugabo yirukanywe mu 1718 kubera yashize ubutinyuka bwo kunegura ingoma ya Louis XIV. Muri ivyo, yahuje na Vauban na Boisguilbert, abo na bo nyene bashize ubutinyuka bwo kuvuga ku magorwa yihishe inyuma y'uburangare bw'ingoma y'Umwami w'Izuba.

![Louis XIV, Versailles, Inzu y'Ibirabuza](assets/en/021.webp)

Abbé wa Saint-Pierre yavuze ko ingoma ya Louis XIV, n'urugo rwayo rw'ubutunzi bwinshi n'ukwiyegereza intambara zo kwigarurira ibihugu, ntiyari ikimenyetso c'umwami mwiza. Yanse kwemera ko Louis XIV akwiye izina "**Louis Mukuru**".



"Guhonya ababanyi bawe n'abantu bawe ico gihe kimwe ntiyo ubukuru", yavuze. Ico ciyumviro caratetereje cane Académie Française, iyo yamaze igihe kirekire yiyegereza guhayagiza umwami mu buryo bwose bw'ubwanditsi bushoboka. Ni co gituma batoye hafi ya bose ko yirukanwa.

![Académie Française](assets/en/022.webp)

### Ivyiyumviro vya mbere ku guhanahana no ku kazi

Ku bijanye n'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi, yakoresha imisi yose ingingo ngenderwako y'akamaro, nk'ukwo Jeremy Bentham yahavuye abigira, kandi akenshi yatanga ivyiyumviro bitomoye. Mu kuvugisha ukuri, yari aracakwegwa n'ivyiyumviro vya mercantilism, ivyo ata muntu yari yaravavanuye rwose ico gihe.

Yamara, Abbé wa Saint-Pierre yagize ivyiyumviro bitomoye ku vy'ubutunzi. Mbere imbere ya *Condillac*, uwukunda guhabwa iteka kubera ico ciyumviro, yavuze atomora ko mu guhanahana kwose, impande zibiri zironkeramwo akamaro. Ivyo bisanzwe mu gitabu ciwe co mu 1733 ["Project for Improving France's Trade"](https://www.institutcoppet.org/projet-pour-perfectionner-le-commerce-de-france/), aho yanditse:

> Iyo umuguzi n'umudandaza baguranye, uwudandaza aronkeramwo akamaro, n'uwuguze na we nyene; kuko iyo hataba akamaro nyakuri canke akamaro kabonwa ku mpande zibiri, uwudandaza ntiyodandaza ku giciro nk'ico, kandi uwuguze na we ntiyogura ku giciro nk'ico.

![Agatabu k'Abafaransa ko mu kinjana ca 18 gaciye guhindura ivy'ubudandaji n'ivy'imari, kerekana impaka za mbere ku budandaji no ku ngingo mfatirwako z'ubutunzi.](assets/en/023.webp)

Yashimikiye kandi, imbere ya Vincent de Gournay, ku gaciro k'akazi no ku kuba ari ngombwa gutuma akaba akwegakwega. "Akazi kose kagoye", yanditse. "Kandi iyo umuntu abonye ko akazi kiwe ntikimuriha ikintu, canke ntikimuriha bihagije, aracika umunebwe kandi ntashaka kwiruhisha ku busa". Ico ciyumviro nyene Marquis d'Argenson, Vincent de Gournay n'aba Physiocrats bahavuye bagisubiramwo mu gihe banegura amabwirizwa aremereye n'uburyo bw'amashirahamwe y'abakozi. Bavuga ko izo nzitiro zaca intege abakozi, zizana ingorane z'ubusa, kandi amaherezo zatuma ubunebwe busa n'ukwegakwega kuruta akazi gitanga umwimbu muri nk'izo ngingo.


## Marquis d'Argenson

<chapterId>e9960ab4-72ec-5afd-8e97-bf89c83b62bc</chapterId>

### Uwabanjirije Adam Smith

Marquis d'Argenson ni umwe mu batsimvyi b'inyigisho ya laissez-faire yibagiwe.

![Marquis d'Argenson, Laissez-faire, laissez-passer](assets/en/024.webp)

Yamenyekanye bushasha kubera August Oncken, umwanditsi w'igitabu ku nyigisho ya laissez-faire, laissez-passer, uwashitse ku ciyumviro c'uko d'Argenson yagize uruhara runini mu kuvuka kw'ico ciyumviro.



René-Louis Voyer, Marquis d'Argenson, yavutse mu 1694. Yatanguye umwuga wiwe wa politike nk'umuhanuzi mu parlement, hanyuma yakoreye mu Nama ya Leta.

**Imyaka mirongo itatu imbere ya Adam Smith**, d'Argenson yari asanzwe aburanira akamaro k'ugusaranganya akazi n'ukwihariza umwuga.

![Uruganda rw'inshinge, “Encyclopédie” ya Diderot na d'Alambert](assets/en/025.webp)

Yaneguye bimwe bikomeye amabwirizwa, nk'uko yabibona, yagerageza guhindura ivyo intara zitandukanye zikunda kamere. Nk'akarorero, yatangajwe n'uko ubutegetsi bwagomba ko impuzu n'ubudodo bwa velours bikorwa i Tours, igisagara cari gikene ico gihe, kugira bingane ku bwiza n'ibikorwa i Genoa yaroranirwa, iyo yari izwi ku bicuruzwa vy'agaciro. Yashitse ku ciyumviro:

> Ikibanza cose gitegerezwa kwemererwa kwitorera inganda zaco. Umwidegemvyo! Umwidegemvyo!

### Urutonde ruvuka ku bwarwo n'ukuboko kutaboneka

Yaramenye kandi iyindi ngingo ngenderwako y'ishimikiro ya Smith: iciyumviro c'uko urutonde ruvuka ku bwarwo ruva ku kurondera inyungu bwite. Marquis d'Argenson yiyumvira ko inyungu bwite y'ubu na nyene ari yo yavyura inguvu z'abantu. Yanditse ko ubukorikori bubi n'uburiganya bica izina ry'uwuhingura, mu gihe umwete n'ivyipfuzo vyiza bizana ukworoherwa. Uwuzi neza akamaro k'ikintu, nk'uko avuga, ni umuntu ku giti ciwe, ni abantu bose muri rusangi, abo bagura ibicuruzwa kandi bitwararika kugira isoko ryiza. "Umwe wese arumva inyungu yiwe", yavuze, "umwe wese afata ingingo zimufitiye akamaro, kandi ni muri ubwo bwumvikane rusangi tubona ukuri."


Mbere imbere ya Adam Smith, yatahuye ko inyungu bwite ishikana ku nyungu rusangi biciye ku kwiyubaka kw'urutonde kamere ruvuka ku bwarwo.

![Ishusho ya Adam Smith](assets/en/026.webp)

Yagereranije imibano n'umutiba w'inzuki aho udukoko twose dukurikira kamere yatwo, "Mu bikorwa vyazo", ni ko yavuze neza, "havuye ukwegeranya kwinshi kw'ivyo umuryango mutoyi ukeneye; mugabo ivyo ntivyazanwe n'amabwirizwa, canke n'abagaba b'ingabo, abasaba umuntu wese gukurikira ivyiyumviro vy'umuyobozi wabo". Ivyo birashobora kuba ari ijambo ryegereye cane, mu vyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi vy'Abafaransa, iciyumviro kizwi ca Adam Smith c'"ukuboko kutaboneka".

Marquis d'Argenson yahora ashavuzwa n'ivyiyumviro vy'abashikiranganji bo mu gihe ciwe. Ikibazo conyene bibaza cari: "Twoshira amabwirizwa muri ubu buryo canke muri bwo bundi? Twoyobora ivy'ubutunzi tubijana muri iki canke muri kino?". D'Argenson yishura: "Ntitwobanza kwibaza nimba bibereye kuyobora ikintu na kimwe, canke nimba ibintu bitegerezwa kurekurwa ngo bikore ku bwavyo?"


Mu kuvugisha ukuri, yatangazwa n'uko abantu bagora cane kutahura, canke ahubwo kubona, ingaruka mbi z'amabwirizwa y'ubwoko bwose arengeje urugero ku vy'ubutunzi. Mu vyiyumviro vyiwe, vyari bihagije gusa ko umuntu ahumbura amaso. "Ibintu vyinshi biriko biragenda neza uno musi", yanditse abinuba, "kubera gusa ko vyashoboye guhunga ukuboko kw'itegeko".
Rimwe na rimwe, yataye umutwe kubona ivyiyumviro vyiwe bitatahurwa.

![Marquis d'Argenson, “Diary and Memoirs”](assets/en/027.webp)

### Igituma Leta itegerezwa kuba ntoyi

Ingingo mfatirwako y'ubutunzi yaburaniye yari rero ihushanye n'ivyakundwa mu gihe ciwe. Iciyumviro ciwe cerekana ko uruhara rwa Leta ari, imbere ya vyose, urwo kudakora. "Ico ubudandaji bukeneye ni ugukurwaho kw'inzitiro. Busaba gusa abacamanza beza, ukuhanwa kw'ukwiharira amasoko, ukukingirwa kungana ku benegihugu bose, amahera atajajuka, n'amabarabara n'amayira y'amazi". Iyo yari insobanuro ya Leta ntoyi, iyahavuye iba imwe mu mishinge y'umuco w'Abafaransa mu butunzi bwa politike.

Ico ciyumviro c'uruhara rwa Leta mu bikorwa vy'ubutunzi cerekanwe kamere biciye ku kwiga ibibazo bibiri bikuru vyahagaritse umutima abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi n'abanyavyiyumviro b'imibano bo mu gihe ciwe: amabwirizwa ku nganda n'ubudandaji bw'ingano.

Amabwirizwa ku nganda, imbere ya vyose, yamuteye ishavu ryinshi, kuko yari uburenganzira bwihariye bwa bamwe ku mutwaro w'abandi. "Imvo nyayo y'uguhungabana kw'amahinguriro yacu", yanditse, "ni ukukingirwa kurengeje urugero kwayahawe". Kandi ni n'umwete utagabanuka yerekanye ukunegura kwiwe umwete wo kuyobora vyose w'abatwara igihugu bo mu gihe ciwe:


> Kuyobora inganda ku nguvu ni ukwipfuza ko zihona.

Ku kibazo c'ubudandaji bw'ibifungurwa, d'Argenson ntiyari afise iyindi nyishu atari umwidegemvyo. Nk'uko avuga, ukubura kw'ingano kwavuye ku kwiharira isoko no ku kwiyubara kurengeje urugero kw'ubutegetsi. Ico dutegerezwa gukora conyene cari ukureka ibintu bigende, kandi ntihogira ukubura kw'ingano mu gihugu aho ibivuko vyugururutse. Abanyamahanga, bakwegwa nk'abandi bantu bose n'inyungu, bodushikirije ivyo dukeneye kandi bojana ivyo dusigaje. "Reka bigende", yavuze, "kandi vyose bizogenda neza".

## Vincent de Gournay

<chapterId>e8ae40dc-7450-552f-9ddc-9e02936cf425</chapterId>

### Umudandaza mu nzu z'ubutegetsi

Vincent de Gournay yari umwe mu baserukira ba mbere ba laissez-faire mu Bufaransa, kandi umwe mu bayiharaniye imbere y'abandi mu buyobozi bwa Leta no mu duce vy'abanyabwenge. Ni co gituma akwiye guhabwa iteka rininiya cane mu mateka y'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi kuruta iryo ahabwa. Naragerageje kwerekana ivyo yakoze mu gitabu giherutse.

![Benoît Malbranque, “Vincent de Gournay”](assets/en/028.webp)

Yavukiye mu muryango w'abadandaza, Gournay yacitse umudandaza yaroraniwe na we nyene, yegeranya itunga ryinshi imbere yuko aronka ibanga mu buyobozi bwa Leta y'Ubufaransa. Mu Biro vy'Ubudandaji, yari **uwuharanira n'umwete akazi kidegemvya n'ubudandaji bwidegemvya**.

Naho yari yinjiye neza mu ruhando rw'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi bakomeye bo mu gihe ciwe, Gournay yanditse bike canke, ahubwo, yasohoye bike. Yanditse canecane amakete n'inyandiko z'ubuyobozi, izitasohowe canke zasohowe n'abandi banditsi bazibanje kuzitunganya.

Ivyo dufise ni ibi:

1. [Ivyiyumviro](https://archive.org/details/traitessurlecomm0000chil) ku mpinduzi y'igitabu c'umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi w'Umwongereza Josiah Child;

![A treatise on trade ya Josiah Child, Inyandiko y'igenekerezo rya 26 Ruheshi 1715, yanditswe na M. de Gourgan, umudandaza, Icegeranyo ca Leta ku Compagnie des Indes](assets/en/029.webp)

2. "Observations" ziri mu gitabu Examination of the Advantages and Disadvantages of the Prohibition of Printed Fabrics;



3. "Observations on the East India Company", Abbé Morellet yashize ku gitabu ciwe Memoir on the Current State of the East India Company (1769);



4. Kandi imbere ya vyose, inyandiko zitandukanye zivuye ku ibanga ryiwe ry'Intendant w'Ubudandaji.

### Ingaruka z'amahanga n'ivyiyumviro bibumba

Izo nyandiko zerekana ingaruka z'amahanga zitomoye n'ukuba hariho ivyiyumviro bibumba bikomeye. Ingaruka z'amahanga, kuri Gournay, ni ukwemera ko Ubwongereza n'Ubuholandi bibarushiriza. Gournay yari yemeza ko ayo makungu abiri yatahura ivy'ubutunzi kuruta Ubufaransa cane, kandi ko Ubufaransa butegerezwa gukurikira akarorero kayo. "Ayo makungu abiri ni yo yaroraniwe cane", yavuze, "kandi akurikira uburyo butandukanye rwose n'ubwacu. Turabuza ibicuruzwa vy'amahanga kwinjira, dupfungira ibikorwa vy'ubutunzi mu mabwirizwa akomeye, mu gihe bo bagenza ukundi. Nimba bo bameze neza", Gournay yashitse ku ciyumviro, "ni kubera ko Ubufaransa buyobowe n'ingingo ngenderwako zitagira ishingiro".

Ivyo yaciye avuga vyo guhindura vyari bishingiye ku ngingo nyamukuru nyinshi.

Ubwa mbere, yiyumvira ko akazi gategerezwa kukingirwa no kuremeshwa. Ico gihe, abakozi b'Abafaransa bafatwa nk'abagizi ba nabi, bagenzurwa imisi yose, kandi bagumizwa mu bwoba ko batakurikije rimwe mu mabwirizwa ibihumbi. Ukwo kubabaza kurengeje urugero kwaca intege abantu bo kukora, kubasunikira ahubwo mu bunebwe. Yamara, Gournay avuga, "akazi kagira iteka kandi ni inzira yonyene yo gutungisha igihugu".

Ubwa kabiri, yaraneguye uburyo bw'inzitiro bw'amashirahamwe y'abakozi bwapfungiye abahinguzi. Kwinjira mu mwuga vyafata igihe kinini kandi vyari bihenze, kandi umukozi mushasha wese yategerezwa gukurikira n'ubwitonzi ivyari vyamaze gushingwa n'amategeko y'ishirahamwe ryiwe. Ubwo buryo ntibwasiga ikibanza ku bushobozi budasanzwe, ku guhingura ibishasha, canke ku iterambere.

Ubwa gatatu, ubudandaji mu Bufaransa bwari bugabanijwe n'amategeko y'inzitiro. Mu vyiyumviro vya Gournay, abaguzi bofise akamaro kanini iyo ibivuko bishobora guhiganwa mu mwidegemvyo kandi ibicuruzwa vyose, nk'impeke n'impuzu zicapuwe, byemererwa kwinjira ata nzitiro. Yari umwe mu ba mbere berekanye inkomoko nyayo y'amagendo: yariho gusa kubera ko ubudandaji bufise akamaro kandi bwiza bwari bubujijwe. Yongeyeko iciyumviro gitomoye: amagendo yari umwuga "widegemvya", ata mabwirizwa, ata mashirahamwe y'abakozi, ata makori anyaga. Yamara, amabwirizwa arengeje urugero ya Leta ni yo yasunikira abakozi benshi b'inzirakarengane mu kurenga amategeko.

Ubwa nyuma, Gournay yerekanye ko ibipimo vy'inyungu ku nguzanyo vyari bito mu Bwongereza no mu Buholandi, ibihugu vyaroranirwa kuruta Ubufaransa. Yaharaniye ko ibipimo vy'inyungu bigabanuka no mu Bufaransa, kugira ngo ibikorwa vy'ubutunzi bironke amahera ku ngingo nziza nk'ahandi. Yamara, Gournay ntiyarondera uburyo bw'agahato canke bw'amategeko; ahubwo yashimikiye ko ari ngombwa gutuma ugutiza amahera bimenyekana n'amategeko, kuko Ekleziya Gatolika yaracaguma ibiciraho iteka.

### Ingaruka zamaho kuri Turgot no kuhagana imbere

Kuri izo ngingo zose, Gournay yagize uruhara rw'ishimikiro mu mpaka z'ivyiyumviro zo mu kiringo co hagati mu kinjana ca 18. Ukuburanira umwidegemvyo mu vy'ubutunzi kwiwe kwabanjirije aba Physiocrats imyaka cumi na Adam Smith imyaka mirongo ibiri. Mugabo ingaruka ziwe zamaho cane zabaye kuri Turgot. Gournay yafashe Turgot akiri umusore, aramutunganiriza kandi amwigisha ivyiyumviro vyiwe.

![Ishusho ya Turgot](assets/en/030.webp)

Nyuma y'imyaka, Turgot, uwahavuye aba umushikiranganji mu ngoma ya Louis XVI, yanditse Éloge (ijambo ryo guhayagiza) ryubahisha umugenzi wiwe amaze kwitaba Imana. Kandi nimba Turgot ntiyaremeye rwose Physiocracy ya François Quesnay, ni kubera ko yagumye afatanye n'umwigisha wiwe wa mbere, Vincent de Gournay, ku buryo butavunika.

## Umurwi wa Gournay

<chapterId>b86ba6bd-8f3a-5d8f-b098-4d0413f00deb</chapterId>

### Uruhurirane rw'abahinduzi n'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi

Ku bijanye n'imisi ya mbere y'ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi, amateka akunda kwerekana ishure rya mbere ry'ivyiyumviro: aba Physiocrats, bayobowe na François Quesnay n'abayoboke biwe. Yamara, imbere y'imyaka igera ku cumi imbere y'iterambere ryabo, hariho uwundi mugwi, utari utunganijwe cane mugabo w'ishimikiro kimwe, wari ushingiye ku muhinga mu vy'ubutunzi Vincent de Gournay.

![Umurwi wa Vincent de Gournay](assets/en/031.webp)

Nk'uko twabonye mu gice giheruka, Gournay yakwegwa n'akarorero k'amakungu y'amahanga nk'Ubwongereza n'Ubuholandi. Yashima kandi cane abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi babo (nka Josiah Child, Johan de Witt na David Hume).

![Ishusho ya David Hume](assets/en/032.webp)

Ukwo kubashima kwamushikanye ku guhindura, no ku gutuma abandi bahindura, inyandiko zabo z'ivy'ubutunzi.

Ibanga ryiwe mu buyobozi bukuru ryatumye Gournay ahura n'abahinga bose mu vy'ubutunzi bazwi mu Bufaransa ico gihe. Muri ubwo buryo, yakoranije umugwi w'abahinduzi b'ubushobozi budasanzwe. We nyene yahinduye ibikorwa vya Child na Culpeper. Abbé Le Blanc yahinduye igitabu Political Discourses ca David Hume. Véron de Forbonnais yahinduye umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi w'Umunya-Espagne Geronymo de Uztariz. Turgot yakoreye ku nyandiko za Josiah Tucker, kandi umuhungu wa Montesquieu yahinduye Joshua Gee.

### Ukudurumbanya kw'ibisohokayandikiro vy'ivy'ubutunzi

Kubera ugukorana kwa bamwe mu bagize umurwi wa Gournay, abanditsi benshi barashoboye kusohora ibikorwa vyabo bwite ku mazina yabo. Ivyo bitabu, hamwe n'impinduzi, vyararoraniwe bidasanzwe. Muri vyo, tuvuge nk'ibi:

- Igitabu Essay ca Herbert ku [General Police of Grain](https://archive.org/details/essaisurlapolice00herb/page/n7/mode/2up) (basohoye incuro 6 mu myaka 4)

![Abbé Gabriel-François Coyer, “La Noblesse commerçante”, Essay on the general grain policy (1755)](assets/en/033.webp)

- Igitabu [Noblesse commerçante](https://archive.org/details/lanoblessecommer00coye/page/n3/mode/2up) ca Coyer (basohoye incuro 5 mu myaka 2)



- Igitabu [Remarks on the Advantages and Disadvantages of France and Great Britain](https://archive.org/details/bim_eighteenth-century_remarks-on-the-advantage_plumard-de-dangeul-loui_1754) ca Plumard de Dangeul (basohoye incuro 3 mu mwaka wa mbere)
- Igitabu [Memoir on the Trades](https://www.amazon.com/Memoire-sur-corps-metiers-French/dp/1978196903) ca Cliquot-Blervache na Gournay (basohoye incuro 2 mu 1758)
Uwo mugwi yagize kandi uruhara rw'ishimikiro mu kusohora 
- [Essay on the Nature of Trade in General](https://archive.org/details/essayonnatureofc0000cant) ca Richard Cantillon.

![Richard Cantillon, “Essay on the nature of commerce”](assets/en/034.webp)

Ico gitabu ca nyuma, canditswe ahagana mu 1730, casigaye mu ntoke z'umwanditsi inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe. Gournay, afashijwe n'abagenzi biwe b'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi, yagisohoye mu 1755. Nk'uko Abbé Morellet, umwe mu bagize uwo murwi, abivuga, Gournay yaragihanuye umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi wese yari azi.

Ivyavuye mu bwenge bw'umurwi wa Gournay vyagize ingaruka nkomeye ku mateka y'ivyiyumviro. Muri ubwo buryo, bashobora kubonwa nk'abatsimvyi b'ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi mu Bufaransa. Christine Théré wo muri INED, uwize amateka y'ibisohokayandikiro vy'ivy'ubutunzi, yasanze ko hagati ya 1750 na 1759, ibikorwa bitari munsi ya **349** vy'ivy'ubutunzi vyasohowe, mu gihe vyari **83** gusa mu myaka cumi yabanje yose (1740–1749). Iryo hinduka rikomeye ryo mu myaka ya 1750 riva cane ku murwi wa Gournay.

![Ubushakashatsi ku bisohokayandikiro vy'ivy'ubutunzi mu Bufaransa, 1566-1789.](assets/en/035.webp)

Kugira bakwize ugukunda impaka z'ivy'ubutunzi mu Bafaransa, Gournay n'abagenzi biwe barakoze ngo bibe bitahurwa biciye ku bitabu vy'inkuru z'uruhimbo. Inyuma y'igitabu *Memoir on the Trades*, icanenguye amashirahamwe y'abakozi, Gournay na Cliquot-Blervache barafashije Gabriel-François Coyer kwandika inkuru ngufi y'urwenya yitwa Chinki: [A Cochinchinese Tale Applicable to Other Nations](https://www.institutcoppet.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/Coyer-Chinki-Histoire-cochinchinoise.pdf).

![Abbé Gabriel-François Coyer, “Chinki”](assets/en/036.webp)

Inkuru ikurikira umuntu nyamukuru, Chinki, uwuhatirwa kuva mu gihugu ciwe kubera amakori arengeje urugero kandi agerageza kurondera akazi k'ubukorikori ku bana biwe mu gisagara. Mugabo imyuga yose irabugarira kubera amabwirizwa mabi y'amashirahamwe y'abakozi, ni co gituma inkuru yerekana ukwihebura kwiwe kugenda kwiyongera mu buryo bw'urwenya.

### Ugutuma ivy'ubutunzi biba ivyakundwa na bose

Umurwi wa Gournay ni wo rero wavuyemwo umuraba mwinshi w'ibisohokayandikiro. Naho ivyo bikorwa bikomeye vyibagiwe n'abahinga mu mateka y'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi, vyari bitomoye cane ku bo mu gihe cabo. Aba Physiocrats, abatunganije ishure ryabo mu myaka ya 1760, bahavuye bereka umugwi wa Gournay nk'ababanjirije babo bwa nyene. Mu 1767, umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi Jacques Accarias de Serionne yabivuze arushiriza kutomora mu kubahayagiza. Yanditse: "Abafaransa bake, ari abafilozofe ari abenegihugu, batanguye imyaka mikeyi iheze kwigana abanditsi b'Abongereza. Babanje guhindura abo bigana kandi ntibatevye kubarusha mu bintu vyinshi. Bazanye uburyohe bwose n'ubutunzi bwose bw'ubwanditsi mu kuvuga ku bintu bifise akamaro; baravyuye kandi bakwiza ugukunda ubumenyi bwari bw'ishimikiro cane ku kworoherwa kwa Leta".

Kandi ni ukuri, mu myaka ya 1750, ibibazo vy'ivy'ubutunzi vyabaye ivyakundwa na bose. Voltaire yavuze ijambo rizwi cane ko ahagana mu 1750, Abafaransa baheba ibitabu vy'inkuru z'uruhimbo kugira baganire ku mwidegemvyo w'ubudandaji bw'impeke. Ico kintu carerekanywe kandi n'ikinyamakuru Mercure de France, cavuze mu nomero yo mu 1758, amezi makeyi imbere y'urupfu rwa Gournay: "Ubutunzi bwa politike ubu ni bwo bumenyi bukundwa na bose. Ibitabu bivuga ku burimyi, ku bantu, ku nganda, ku budandaji no ku vy'imari ubu biri mu ntoke z'abantu batabarika, abo, hakiri gato, basoma gusa ibitabu vy'inkuru z'uruhimbo ku rutubi". Ntihoshobora kuboneka ijambo ryiza kuruta iryo ryo guhayagiza Gournay n'ibikorwa vy'umurwi wiwe w'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi.

## Mirabeau

<chapterId>2d2f802b-e3b6-556f-9025-a1b1dc4409ca</chapterId>

### Se w'umuhungu w'izina

Ubufaransa bwamenye abagabo babiri b'izina bitwa Mirabeau, se n'umuhungu, mugabo ni umuhungu yaranze amateka vy'ukuri. Umuvugizi w'Impinduramatwara kandi umwe mu bantu b'ishimikiro mu vyabaye mu Mpinduramatwara y'Ubufaransa, yagumye afise izina.

![Impaka mu Nama Nkuru y'Igihugu, Ubufaransa, 1789](assets/en/037.webp)

Biciye ku mpano yiwe no ku kibanza ciwe mu mateka y'Ubufaransa, yaciye igicucu kuri se, umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi kandi inkingi y'ishure rya François Quesnay, uwari umunywanyi waryo wa mbere kuva mu 1758.

![Ishusho ya Mirabeau, Mirabeau, “L'ami des hommes”](assets/en/038.webp)

### L'Ami des Hommes: igitabu cavuzwe na bose

Marquis de Mirabeau yari, mu vy'ukuri, yamaze kuronka izina rikomeye mbere imbere yuko yemera Physiocracy, kubera igitabu cimwe cari "The Friend of Mankind. Treatise on Population [FR: L'ami des hommes, ou, Traité de la population](https://archive.org/details/lamideshommesou00unkngoog). Naho ubwa mbere casohowe mu 1756, camenyekanye ahagana mu 1757, umwaka umwe imbere yuko ahura na Quesnay.



Ibisohokayandikiro birenga 20 vyasohowe hagati ya 1757 na 1760, ivyo bishobora gutuma kiba igitabu c'ivy'ubutunzi caroraniwe cane mu mateka. Abasoma bamwe biyumvira mbere ko ico gitabu canditswe na Montesquieu kubera ukwiyumvira kwaco kutomoye. Dauphin, se w'Umwami Louis XVI, yavuze mbere ko yakimenye ku mutwe. Mu kiringo kimwe, cari igitabu bose bo i Versailles basoma.

Uno musi ni igitabu kitaba kisomwa, mugabo benshi baracakivuga. Mbere n'aho hashika ikinjana ca 19, Edmond Roussel yavuze:

> L'Ami des Hommes ni kimwe mu bitabu bose bavuga, ico hafi ata muntu azi. Mu bisata vyose vy'abantu, umwenegihugu umwe w'umutima ukomeye yokwiye kugisoma; kugira ngo abandi bose ntibategerezwe kubigira.

Mu ntango y'umwuga wiwe nk'umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi, Mirabeau yakuye ivyiyumviro kuri Richard Cantillon. Yamaze imyaka 15 afise inyandiko y'intoke y'igitabu Essay on the Nature of Commerce in General ca Cantillon kandi yari yayihweje n'ukwihangana kandi ayandikako ivyiyumviro vyiwe.

![Richard Cantillon, “Essay on the nature of commerce”](assets/en/039.webp)

L'Ami des Hommes yatanguriye kwiyumvirwa nk'ivyiyumviro bisanzwe ku gitabu Essay ca Cantillon. Mugabo kubera ko Mirabeau yari afise umuzirikanyi ufatanya ibintu vyinshi bitandukanye, ni ukuvuga, mu kuvugisha ukuri, atari mu bwenge bwiwe bwose, ntiyatevye kuva ku mugambi wiwe wa mbere. Ico gitabu kiravuga gusa ku bibazo vyose vy'ivy'ubutunzi yari azi, rimwe na rimwe kivavanura na Cantillon. Ni igitabu kigoye kusoma, kifise umugambi udasanzwe n'ivyisunzwe mu gice cose. Mirabeau ubwiwe yemeye ko cari akajagari kandi ko uburyo bwiwe bwo kwandika bwari buteye ubwoba.



Naho ari akajagari, ivyiyumviro bimwebimwe birakwiye kwerekanwa:

- Mirabeau arwanya iciyumviro c'ubuhumye ca mercantilism ku bijanye n'ubwoko bw'ubutunzi.
- Arahayagiza uburimyi kandi aranegura ukuhebwa kwabwo.
- Aranuba ingene abantu bameze, canecane abarimyi.
- Ubwa nyuma, aburanira umwidegemvyo w'ubudandaji n'ubuvukanyi bw'amakungu mu mahoro.

![Inyigisho z'ivy'ubutunzi n'iz'imibano, za Marquis de Mirabeau](assets/en/040.webp)

Biragoye kwita ivyiyumviro vya Mirabeau ivy'umwidegemvyo canke ibirwanya umwidegemvyo. Akenshi yajajura hagati y'iciyumviro kimwe n'ikindi, rimwe na rimwe atabizi. Yamara, ivyiyumviro vy'umwidegemvyo vyakunda kuganza mu nyandiko ziwe. Rimwe mu majambo yiwe azwi cane ryari:

> **Ingingo ngenderwako nyakuri kandi yonyene y'ubutunzi bwa politike** ni ukureka vyose bikagira umwidegemvyo.

### Ukwemera Physiocracy

Amaze kuronka uburoranirwa bwiwe bukuru, Mirabeau yaronse abamurondera. François Quesnay, uwari yamaze kwitwararika ivy'ubutunzi, yamutumiye mu cumba ciwe co hagati i Versailles.

![François Quesnay](assets/en/041.webp)

Baragize impaka zikomeye kandi amaherezo, ikintu kitakunda kuba mu mpaka, kirabaye: Mirabeau yemeye atiziganya ko yari yibeshye. Yemeye ivyiyumviro vya Quesnay kandi avuga ko yiteguye kubikwiza.

Bafatanye, baciye baba ishimikiro ry'ico cahavuye kuba ishure ry'ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy, rikomezwa n'abanywanyi bashasha bagiye barinjiza. Bidatevye inyuma y'ihinduka rya Mirabeau, Quesnay yamusavye kuburanira ivyiyumviro vyiwe ku bijanye n'amakori. Ivyo vyavuyemwo igitabu ["Theory of Taxation"](https://archive.org/details/thoriedelimpot00mira), ico gituma Mirabeau yajanwe mu misi mikeyi mu munyororo wa Vincennes hanyuma acibwa bugarariji i Bignon.

![Pierre Samuel Dupont de Nemours, Mirabeau, “Theory of taxation”](assets/en/042.webp)

Mirabeau yagize kandi uruhara rw'ishimikiro mu kwinjiza abanywanyi bashasha mu ishure rya Quesnay. Yaremeje mbere umusore Dupont de Nemours.



Ni no kwa Mirabeau aba Physiocrats bahurira imisi ya kabiri yose. Abantu nka Turgot na Adam Smith basa n'abitavye izo nama incuro imwe canke nyinshi.

Mu buzima bwiwe bwose, Mirabeau yagumye umwanditsi ataruha. Yanditse ibitabu vyinshi vy'ivy'ubutunzi biburanira inyigisho ya Quesnay. Yamara, mu mpera z'ubuzima bwiwe, ububasha bwiwe bwaragabanutse. Uburyo bwiwe bwo kwandika bwarononekaye ku buryo mwene wabo nyene yamwandikiye avuga ko atagishoboye kutahura inyandiko ziwe. Ivyiyumviro vyiwe, vyari vyacitse vy'umwidegemvyo wuzuye, vyahushanye n'ukwishura kw'aba sosiyalisiti canke aba proto-comunisti nka Mably na Rousseau ubwiwe. Yaragowe kuronka abamusoma n'abamusohorera ibitabu, kandi yitavye Imana ata muntu yabimenye mu 1789, mu misi imbere y'ukwigarurirwa kw'ibohero rya Bastille.

## Quesnay

<chapterId>7a35f20b-5ea0-544d-b290-bcd9c6f7f11a</chapterId>

### Kurenza ishusho y'urwenya y'ibitabu vy'ishure

François Quesnay ni **umwe mu bahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'Abafaransa bazwi cane**.

![Ikarata y'ivy'ubutunzi ya François Quesnay, François Quesnay](assets/en/043.webp)

Izina ryiwe riri mu gitabu c'ishure cose c'ivy'ubutunzi n'ic'amateka y'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi. Bavuga ko yaremye "Tableau Économique" kugira yerekane mu buryo bworoshe ingene ivy'ubutunzi bigenda, ko yari umukuru w'ishure ry'ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy, ko yibeshye mu kwiyumvira ko amatongo gusa ni yo yimbura, kandi ubwa nyuma, ko Adam Smith yaje agororora ibintu. Ivyo, mu buryo bumwe canke bundi, ni ko ibitabu vy'ishure bikunda kwegeranya François Quesnay.



Kumugabanura muri ivyo ni agahomerabunwa, kuko Quesnay yari kandi umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi wa mbere yagerageje gushinga ukuburanira umwidegemvyo mu vy'ubutunzi ku ngingo ngenderwako z'ubumenyi. Yari umwe mu bahinga mu vy'ubutunzi bumvirwa cane kandi bafise ububasha cane mu gihe ciwe. Ikirenze ivyo, yashinze Physiocracy, inyigisho itoshe cane kuruta iciyumviro coroshe c'uko kamere yonyene ari yo yimbura ubutunzi, iciyumviro kenshi cerekanwa nabi.

Tuzovuga kuri Physiocracy mu buryo burambuye mu bice bitatu bikurikira. Ubwa mbere, reka twihweze neza François Quesnay ubwiwe.

### Kuva ku muganga abaga gushika ku muganga w'umwami

Yavukiye mu 1694 i Méré mu muryango w'abarimyi utashoboye kwigisha Quesnay gusoma. Yatojwe n'umugabo wo mu kibano ciwe kandi amaherezo yaguma yiga mu ishure rikuru ry'ukubaga hanyuma mu ishure rikuru ry'ubuvuzi. Ku myaka 24, yacitse umuganga abaga i Mantes.

Yaronse izina mu 1730, ku myaka 36, kubera yarwanije ico basanzwe bakora co gukura amaraso ku barwayi, ico yiyumvira ko cashingiye ku nyigisho zitagira ishingiro no ku vyiyumviro vy'ubuhumye. Yashize kandi ubutinyuka bwo guhirikira uburyo bw'amashirahamwe y'abakozi, ubwo bwabwiriza ko abaganga babaga gusa ni bo bashobora kubaga kandi ko abaganga gusa ni bo bashobora kwandika imiti. Ivyo kenshi vyahatira abakene kuriha kabiri no kuzana abantu babiri, ikintu Quesnay yabona ko giteye ishavu.

Mu 1740, yacitse umunyamabanga w'Academy of Surgery. Mu 1748, ku myaka 54, yacitse umuganga bwite wa Madame de Pompadour kandi yimukira i Versailles.

![François Quesnay](assets/en/044.webp)

Yari afitaniye ubugenzi bukomeye na Madame de Pompadour. Quesnay yavuze mbere, mu gihe hamenyekanye ko uwo mukunzi w'umwami atagifise iteka, ko ntashaka kuguma ari umuganga i Versailles atari kumwe na we:
"Nari nifatanije na Madame de Pompadour mu kworoherwa kwiwe; nzoguma ndi ukwo no mu kutagira iteka kwiwe".

Hanyuma yarasohoye ibitabu vyinshi vy'ubuvuzi: Treatise on Suppuration (1749), Treatise on Gangrene (1749), na Treatise on Continuous Fevers (1753), ivyo vyose vyasubiwe gucapurwa incuro nyinshi akiriho. 

Ku myaka 60, amaze kumenyeshwa impaka z'ivy'ubutunzi zakundwa ico gihe, yatanguye kwandika ku vy'ubutunzi. Mu 1755, yanditse ibiganiro "Farmers" na "Grain" bijanye n'*Encyclopédie*.

![Encyclopedia, Dupont de Nemours,, “Theory of taxation”](assets/en/045.webp)

Vyasohowe mu 1757. Uwo mwaka nyene, yahuye na Mirabeau w'izina kandi yashoboye kumuronsa ivyiyumviro vyiwe vy'ivy'ubutunzi: ishimikiro rya Physiocracy ryari rivutse. Quesnay yaciye arema Tableau Économique, iyo yacapuwe mu bicapuro vy'umwami, bivugwa ko yacapuwe umwami ubwiwe ariho i Château de Versailles. Kuva ico gihe, yakira imisi yose abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi n'abafilozofe mu nzu yiwe y'i Versailles, aho baganira mu mwidegemvyo. Quesnay yagize kandi uruhara mu gitabu Theory of Taxation ca Mirabeau, casohowe mu 1759.



Ntibitevye ko ugucungera ibisohokayandikiro kwabaduka. Mirabeau yapfunzwe, kandi Quesnay yakebuwe. Inyuma y'ivyo, Quesnay yatahuye ko atashobora gusohora ibitabu ku mugaragaro kandi ko yokeneye abayoboke bo gukwiza ivyiyumviro vyiwe.

### Abayoboke n'ugukwiragira kw'ivyiyumviro

Ntiyatevye kubaronka: Baudeau, Dupont de Nemours, Le Trosne, Mercier de la Rivière, n'abandi. Abo ni bo bagabo bahavuye bakwiza kandi bamenyekanisha ivyiyumviro vya Quesnay. Yamara, Quesnay, uwakenera abayoboke, ntiyanezerezwa rwose n'uko umugwi wiwe wasa n'idini. Ivyo bishingirwa n'amakete yandikiye Mirabeau, aho amubwira:

> Nimwiyumvire ubwanyu. Nabonye ko inyandiko zanje mbi zibagira abanebwe. Ubu ni umuzo wanyu wo kwiyumvira. Muzi ibingana n'ivyo nzi.

Yamara, abayoboke biwe bari biyeguriye cane kandi baragize uruhara runini mu kumenyekanisha Quesnay. Amaze kwitaba Imana, Mirabeau yavuze ijambo ryo kumuhayagiza, ati: "Twatakaje data, kuko ivyo dufise vyose twabikesha we". Mu vy'ukuri, Quesnay ni we yabikeshe bo, kuko iyo batabaho, yari kuguma apfundikiye i Versailles, aho ivyiyumviro vyiwe, naho vyari bifise ivyo bikwega canke ivyo bihagarika umutima, bishimisha bake.

Kubera ibikorwa vy'abakoranye na we, ivyiyumviro vyiwe vyaronse aho bivugirwa: ubwa mbere biciye ku binyamakuru nk'*Journal of Agriculture* n'*Ephémérides du Citoyen*.

![“Physiocracy”, ikoraniro ry'ibiganiro vya Quesnay, 1768, “Ephémérides du Citoyen”, ikinyamakuru nyamukuru c'aba Physiocrats](assets/en/046.webp)

Hanyuma biciye ku bitabu, atari vy'abayoboke biwe gusa mugabo n'ikoraniro rikomeye ryasohowe mu 1768 na Dupont de Nemours, ryitwa *Physiocracy*.



Ico gitabu caregeranije ivyo Quesnay yazanye bikomeye. Cerekana intumbero y'ivy'ubutunzi y'umukuru w'aba Physiocrats: uburorero bw'ubutunzi bushingiye ku burimyi aho itegeko rihagarikira umuntu wese uburenganzira bwo kugira umutungo n'umwidegemvyo wo kudandaza.

# Ishure ry'ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy

## Amateka y'aba Physiocrats

<chapterId>4236ff8b-b53a-59e7-92c0-f96f9afa1c00</chapterId>

### Kuva kuri Boisguilbert gushika kuri Quesnay

**Physiocracy yabaye ikintu cakundwa mu Bufaransa**, kandi mbere no mu Buraya bwose, mu myaka igera ku cumi gusa. Inyuma y'igihe kirekire c'iterambere, yaronse izina mu kiringo co hagati mu myaka ya 1760. Igihe Turgot afata ubutegetsi mu 1776, urwo runani rwari rwamaze gutakaza ugukundwa kwarwo, kandi umushikiranganji yagumye ahisha cane ukushigikira ishure rya Quesnay.

Inkomoko yayo iri mu kwiyongera kw'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi mu myaka ya 1750. Abanditsi batari bake barafashije kwuzuza icobo kiri hagati ya Boisguilbert n'ico cahavuye kwitwa Physiocracy. Nk'uko twabivuze, Vincent de Gournay n'umurwi wiwe w'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi bari inyuma y'ibisohokayandikiro vyinshi vyamenyesheje abasoma b'Abafaransa ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi vy'amahanga kandi vyabateye umwete ku vy'ubutunzi. Imishinge ya Physiocracy yari isanzwe iri mu bitabu nka "Détail de la France" ca Boisguilbert na ["Essay on the Nature of Trade in General"](https://archive.org/details/essaisurlanature0000cant) ca Cantillon.

![Boisguilbert, “Detail of France”, Cantillon, “Essay on the nature of commerce”](assets/en/047.webp)



Iciyumviro ca laissez-faire cari gifise abakiharanira batari bake kuva kuri Boisguilbert, canecane Vincent de Gournay na Marquis d'Argenson.

Ico casigaye cari ukuvyura ivyo vyiyumviro vyose bibe inyigisho yifatanye kandi yuzuye, kandi Marquis de Mirabeau ni we wa mbere yagerageje. Yimbuye ivyiyumviro ku gitabu Essay ca Cantillon, yatanguye kwandika igitabu kirambuye ku bibazo vy'ivy'ubutunzi cari "L'Ami des Hommes", ico caroraniwe cane.

Gutyo, mu 1756, inkuru ya Physiocracy yashobora gutangura. François Quesnay, ico gihe yari umuganga abaga yacitse umuganga bwite wa Madame de Pompadour, yatumiye Mirabeau i Versailles kugira baganire ku vyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi.

![Quesnay, Ikarata y'ivy'ubutunzi ya Quesnay, Mirabeau, “Theory of Taxation”](assets/en/048.webp)

Mu mpera z'ikiganiro cabo, Mirabeau yemeye ivyiyumviro vya Quesnay. Kuva ico gihe, baranditse: Quesnay yaremye *Tableau économique* (1758) kugira yerekane ingene ubutunzi butemba mu vy'ubutunzi, kandi bafatanye basohoye igitabu Theory of Taxation (1759).






Ivyo bakoze ntivyakiriwe neza. Ku rugo rw'umwami, ico cakunda kuboneka cari ukutabitwararika. Umwami yemeye ko Quesnay akunda inyigisho kandi amwita, mu rukundo, "umunyavyiyumviro wanje". Mugabo uretse iryo jambo ryo kumushemeza, ibikorwa vyabo ntivyavuyemwo ikintu. Mu vy'ukuri, igitabu cabo Theory of Taxation caratetereje abacungera amakori canenguye, ku buryo basavye kandi baronse ko Mirabeau apfungwa. Madame de Pompadour amaherezo yatumye arekurwa, mugabo yabandanije kubaho mu bugarariji ku mutumba wiwe wa Bignon mu ndwi zikeyi.


![Marquise de Pompadour](assets/en/049.webp)

Igice ca mbere c'imyaka ya 1760 caciye rero mu kirundurumbwe. Kubera ibanga ryiwe i Versailles, Quesnay yahatiwe guheba kwandika, canke n'imiburiburi guheba kusohora ikintu ku zina ryiwe. Mirabeau, uwari yamaze kucirwa urubanza rimwe, yari yaburiwe kandi yari azi neza ko umukunzi w'umwami atamukingira ibihe vyose.


### Imyaka cumi y'iteka rya Physiocracy

Inyuma y'ako kirundurumbwe gato, abo bahinga babiri mu vy'ubutunzi batanguye kwinjiza abayoboke: iyo yari inzira yonyene yo kumenyekanisha ivyiyumviro vyabo. Mu 1765, uburoranirwa bwabo bwari butangaje. Dupont de Nemours, Abeille, Mercier de la Rivière, Le Trosne na Baudeau ntibatevye kubinjira. Barashinze ishure: bari bafise ikinyamakuru cabo, *Les Éphémérides du Citoyen*, kandi mbere bahurira imisi ya kabiri yose kwa Mirabeau.

![Ephémérides du Citoyen, ikinyamakuru nyamukuru c'aba Physiocrats](assets/en/050.webp)

Hagati ya 1765 na 1775, umugwi wifatanye w'aba Physiocrats wari ku ntambwe yo hejuru y'izina ryabo. Isi y'ubwanditsi n'iya filozofiya yabona bo gusa, ivyo vyatuma ivyiyumviro vyabo bikwiragira cane kandi ningoga. Abantu babita "abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi", canke, mu buryo bwo kubatwenga, "idini y'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi". Naho vyavugwa gute, izina ryabo ryari rikomeye rwose. Mu 1774, inyuma y'urugendo rwo i Metz, M. de Vaublanc yanditse mu vyibutswa vyiwe ukuntu yatangaye: bose bari bamukikuje bavuga ivy'ubutunzi kandi biyumvira nk'abayoboke ba Quesnay. "Vyari ivyakundwa na bose", yerekanye. "Umuntu wese yari umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi".

### Uguhungabana n'ingaruka zamaho

Yamara, aho hagera mu 1770, ugukundwa kwabo kwatanguye kugabanuka. Uwo mugwi warahuye n'abawuvamwo ba mbere, kandi ubushobozi bwabo bwo kwihanganira ukuneguzwa buragabanuka. Kandi abanegura bari benshi: Condillac, Mably, Voltaire, Galiani, Linguet, Graslin, na mbere Adam Smith muri Écosse, bose barahirikiye ivyiyumviro vyabo.

![Abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi bo mu Kiringo c'Ubwenge](assets/en/051.webp)

Ikinyamakuru cabo ntikiba kisohoka ku bihe vyaco. Ivyo vyaranze iherezo ry'igihe uwo mugwi wakora cane.

Physiocracy yabandanije kugira ububasha gushika ku Mpinduramatwara. Mu Bufaransa, ubwa mbere biciye kuri Turgot, uwari umugenzi w'urugendo kuruta umuyoboke, no biciye ku muserukira wayo Dupont de Nemours, uwo tuzokwiga ubuzima n'ibikorwa vyiwe. Mugabo kandi mu Buraya bwose, aho inyigisho ya Physiocracy yakiriwe n'umwete. Mu Budagi, biciye kuri Margrave wa Baden, no mu Butaliyano, biciye kuri Leopold wa Toscane, inyigisho za Physiocracy zaravyuye mbere amahinduka mu vy'ubutunzi ashigikira umutungo bwite n'umwidegemvyo.


## Imishinge y'inyigisho y'aba Physiocrats

<chapterId>4dbe5436-0578-57c2-b054-03ed00aa091a</chapterId>
Ijambo Physiocracy, risobanura "ubutegetsi bwa kamere", ryahimbwe na Dupont de Nemours kandi rikoreshwa nk'izina ry'igitabu *Physiocracy*, ikoraniro ry'ibiganiro vya Quesnay casohowe mu 1768.


![Physiocracy, ikoraniro ry'ibiganiro vya Quesnay, 1768](assets/en/052.webp)

Ni ijambo ritumvikana neza. Nta mwigishwa wa Quesnay yaduhaye insobanuro yaryo nyakuri. Yamara, uburyo bwabo bwo kwiyumvira ntibwari butumvikana namba. Mu vy'ukuri, bwari bwubatswe ku ngingo ngenderwako nkeyi zitomoye cane, izo tugiye kwerekana ng'aha.


### Ingingo ngenderwako ya mbere: uburimyi bwonyene ni bwo bwimbura


Ico ciyumviro ca mbere ni co cakwegereye abahinga mu mateka. Uno musi, mu bitabu vy'ishure canke mu vyigwa vy'ivy'ubutunzi, aba Physiocrats begeranywa gutyo. Bavuga ko biyumviye nk'abatazi ko uburimyi bwonyene ni bwo bwimbura. Kuri ico shimikiro, inyigisho yabo iciwe iteka ko itagira intumbero, kandi isesengura riraheba rijana ningoga kuri Adam Smith.

Yamara, ntibigira ubutungane kunegura aba Physiocrats kubera ko bahaye uburimyi agaciro karengeje urugero, kuko mu kiringo co hagati mu kinjana ca 18, uburimyi bwatanga akazi ku 90% vy'abantu kandi bwari ishimikiro ry'ubutunzi bw'Ubufaransa.


![Jean-François Millet, “Peasants”](assets/en/053.webp)

Iciyumviro c'aba Physiocrats ni, mu vy'ukuri, gitoranguye. Nk'uko babivuga, hariho itandukaniro hagati y'ugwimbura n'ugwinjiza. Umunyainganda n'umudandaza barashobora kuronka inyungu, mugabo umurimyi gusa ni we yimbura vy'ukuri, kuko ugwimbura ni ukurema ibintu bifise akamaro, atari ukwongera akamaro ku bintu vyari bisanzwe biriho.

Dukwiye kandi kugerageza kutahura igituma banka inganda n'ubukorikori nk'ibidimbura. Ico gihe, iyo myuga yari ipfungiwe mu buryo bw'amashirahamwe y'abakozi, ubwo bwabuza guhingura ibishasha, gushora amahera, n'iterambere.

### Ingingo ngenderwako ya kabiri: ubutegetsi bw'igitugu bushingiye ku mategeko aho kuba demokarasi

Uno musi, kugira dutuke umuntu, tuvuga ko atari uwa demokarasi. Naho abahinga mu mateka baharira aba Physiocrats iciyumviro cabo gikomeye ku gwimbura kw'uburimyi, ntibabaharira ukurwanya demokarasi, canecane ko babayeho mu gihe ivyiyumviro vy'Ikiringo c'Ubwenge vyari hejuru cane. Kuva mu kiringo co hagati mu kinjana ca 18 gushika imbere gato y'Impinduramatwara, aba Physiocrats babonwa nk'abansi b'iterambere.

![Tocqueville, “The Old Regime and the Revolution”](assets/en/054.webp)

Tocqueville yashimikiye kuri ico ciyumviro:

> Aba Physiocrats bari mu vy'ukuri bashigikira cane ubudandaji bwidegemvya bw'ibicuruzwa, laissez-faire canke laissez-passer mu budandaji no mu nganda; mugabo ku bijanye n'umwidegemvyo wa politike ubwawo, ntibawitwararika, kandi mbere n'igihe ivyo vyiyumviro vyabaduka mu muzirikanyi, mu ntango barabyanka.

B'umwidegemvyo mu vy'ubutunzi, aba Physiocrats ntibari rero b'umwidegemvyo ku bijanye na politike. Quesnay yandika mu *maxims* ziwe: "Ubutegetsi bukuru bube bumwe kandi buri hejuru y'abantu bose b'imibano no hejuru y'imigambi yose itagira ubutungane y'inyungu bwite." Kandi hanyuma, muri iyo mpanuro nyene: "Uburyo bw'ugucungerana kw'inzego z'ubutegetsi ni iciyumviro c'ubwicanyi, ico cerekana gusa ukudahuza hagati y'abakomeye n'ukucinyizwa kw'abatoyi".

![Louis XVI](assets/en/055.webp)

Tocqueville yerekanye neza ko aba Physiocrats banse demokarasi bakimenya uburyo bwayo. Bari bakekeranya kuri demokarasi, ivyo vyahavuye biba ibisanzwe mu butunzi bwa politike bw'Abafaransa, kuko demokarasi iri kure y'uburyo butagira agahaze: irashobora gutuma abari bake bacinyizwa n'abari benshi, kandi irashobora kuba icibare c'ukwinyaga, ubutegetsi bw'igitugu n'ukwambura ibintu.

### Ingingo ngenderwako ya gatatu: ukwubaha kwuzuye umutungo bwite

Aba Physiocrats biyumvira ko abantu bakenera kugira kandi kugumana ivyavuye mu kazi kabo. Mu vyiyumviro vyabo, uburenganzira ku mutungo bwari ishimikiro nyene ry'imibano. Biyumvira ko Leta ifise akazi kamwe nyamukuru: gukingira umutungo w'abantu. Ikirenzeko, ku bijanye n'ivy'ubutunzi, aba Physiocrats bemeza ko ukudakorwako kw'umutungo kuremesha akazi n'umwete kandi ko ari ingingo y'iterambere ry'ivy'ubutunzi.

![Millet, “Angelus”](assets/en/056.webp)

Quesnay yabivuze mu buryo bworoshe:

> Ugutunga amatongo n'ubutunzi bushobora kwimurwa bube bwizigiwe ku bafise uburenganzira bwo kubitunga, kuko ukwizigirwa kw'umutungo ni ryo shimikiro ry'ishimikiro ry'urutonde rw'ivy'ubutunzi n'ukwizigirwa kw'imibano; ni ukumenya neza ko uzoguma ufise umutungo ibihe vyose ni ko kuremesha akazi n'ugukoresha ubutunzi mu gutunganya no mu kurima amatongo, no mu migambi y'ubudandaji n'iy'inganda.


### Ingingo ngenderwako ya kane: umwidegemvyo wuzuye w'ubudandaji

Mu *Maxims* ziwe twavuze aho hejuru, Quesnay avuga:

> Ubudandaji bugire umwidegemvyo wuzuye, kuko ibwirizwa ryizigirwa cane, ritomoye kandi rifitiye akamaro igihugu na Leta, ku budandaji bwo mu gihugu n'ubwo mu mahanga, riri mu mwidegemvyo wuzuye w'ihiganwa.


![Ikivuko ca Bordeaux mu kinjana ca 18](assets/en/057.webp)

Aba Physiocrats bari babonye ivyononwa n'ukwivanga kw'ubutegetsi, canecane mu budandaji bw'impeke. Dutegerezwa kwemera, ni ko bavuga, ko ubutegetsi ntibuzokwigera bushobora kuyobora ubudandaji nk'ukwo abantu ku giti cabo babigira, kuko bwokwiye kwishura ku vyo bikenewe vyose no kwitaba impinduka yose y'ivyo bisabwa canke ivyo bitangwa. Ivyo vyose birenze kure ubushobozi bwa mbere n'ubutegetsi bufise ubwenge kuruta ayandi umuntu ashobora kwiyumvira. Ni co gituma, ni ngombwa kureka ibintu bigende kandi kureka bitembe.

Kubera ko bufise akamaro kamere, ubudandaji butegerezwa kugira umwidegemvyo wuzuye kandi utagira agahaze. Umwe muri bo, Le Trosne, yanditse mbere agatabu kitwa [*The Freedom of the Grain Trade: Always Useful, Never Harmful*](https://www.institutcoppet.org/liberte-commerce-grains-toujours-utile-jamais-nuisible/).

### Ingingo ngenderwako ya gatanu: Abantu bose ni bavukanyi

Barwanya bimwe bikomeye ubucakara, aba Physiocrats bari kandi abaharanira amahoro bakomeye. "Ingingo mfatirwako yacu y'ivy'amakungu yitwa amahoro", ni ko Mirabeau yavuze mu buryo bworoshe. Mu 1790, mu Nama Nshingiro, Dupont de Nemours yabandanije ukwo kuharanira amahoro igihe yaciye avuga umugambi w'ibwirizwa ribuza intambara zo gutera abandi.


## Ivyashitseko aba Physiocrats n'ingaruka zabo

<chapterId>5b911105-796a-5e2e-a501-c7a364fc758e</chapterId>

### Ugutuma ivy'ubutunzi bimenyekana mu Bufaransa

Nk'uko twabonye mu gice ca mbere mu bice bitatu vyeguriwe aba Physiocrats, abayoboke ba Quesnay bavuzwe na bose mu Bufaransa mu myaka igera ku cumi. Uwo mwete ku vyiyumviro vyabo waragaragaye mu gihe cabo kandi wabandanije gushika mu mpera z'ikinjana. Ng'aha, turaza kuraba bimwebimwe mu vyashitseko n'ububasha bagize ku babasubiriye mu ndimiro y'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi.

Ico bashitseko kikuru, inyuma y'umugwi wa Gournay, ni ugutuma ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi bimenyekana. Ijambo rizwi rya Voltaire rirazwi neza: ahagana mu 1750, igihugu, camaze kwihaza ku vy'imivugo n'ibitabu vy'inkuru z'uruhimbo, catanguye kwiyumvira ku vy'impeke. Aba Physiocrats bagize uruhara muri urwo runani, basohora vy'ukuri amajana y'ibiganiro, udutabu n'ibitabu ku mwidegemvyo w'ubudandaji bw'impeke. Umwete ukomeye aba Physiocrats bahaye impaka z'ivy'ubutunzi ubonekera kandi ku gitigiri gitangaje c'ibikorwa n'udutabu vy'ivy'ubutunzi vyasohowe mu Bufaransa kuva mu 1760 gushika mu 1775. Nk'ikindi kimenyetso c'ugukwiragira kw'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi mu Bufaransa, twibuka ijambo rya M. de Vaublanc, twavuze mu cigwa cabanje, uwavuze i Metz mu 1774 ko abantu batavuga ikindi atari ivy'ubutunzi. "Vyari ivyakundwa na bose", yavuze. "Umuntu wese yari umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi".

Ukuburanira ivyiyumviro vyabo, mu bitabu, mu dutabu no mu kinyamakuru cabo *Les Éphémérides du Citoyen*, ntibitevye kugira ingaruka ku ngingo mfatirwako z'ubutunzi z'Ubufaransa. Mu 1763, ibwirizwa ry'umwami ryahaye umwidegemvyo ubudandaji bw'impeke, ico Quesnay na Mirabeau basaba n'umwete. Incuro nyinshi, ubutegetsi bwaragabanije kandi amabwirizwa agenga amashirahamwe y'imyuga kugira ngo hagire umwidegemvyo mwinshi wo kukora.

### Ububasha mu mahanga

Mu mahanga, uburoranirwa bwaje hakiri kare cane. Mu Budagi, Margrave wa Baden yaritwararitse ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy kandi yaguma yandikirana na Mirabeau na Dupont de Nemours.

![Charles Frederick, “Grand Duke of Baden”](assets/en/058.webp)

Yahaye umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi Johann August Schlettwein, uwari yemera Physiocracy, akazi ko gushira mu ngiro ihinduka ry'amakori no guha umwidegemvyo ubudandaji bw'impeke. Mu Ndamukiza 1770, igeragezo rya mbere ryabaye mu kigwati gitoyi ca Dietlingen. Abanyagihugu basa n'abakiriye izo ngingo n'umwete, mugabo abakozi babijejwe ntibabishigikira cane, ivyo vyatevye ugushirwa mu ngiro kwaguye.

Mu Burusiya, Catherine wa II yariko ategura ihinduka ry'amategeko kandi yasavye Diderot kumurungikira umunyabwenge yomufasha.

![Catherine wa II w'Uburusiya, Mercier de la Rivière, “The natural and essential order of political societies”](assets/en/059.webp)

Yakozwe ku mutima n'ugusoma igitabu [*The Natural and Essential Order of Political Societies*](https://archive.org/details/lordrenaturelete00mercuoft), casohowe mu 1767, yamurungikiye umwanditsi waco, umu Physiocrat Mercier de la Rivière.




Naho yavuye mu Bufaransa ahayagizwa, yakiriwe i Saint-Pétersbourg mu buryo bukanye (uretse ikirere), kandi Umwamikazi yatenguhwa na we.

Muri Suwedi, kuri Gustave wa III, no mu Butaliyano, kuri Leopold wa Toscane, aba Physiocrats baronse kandi abayoboke bari biteguye gushira mu ngiro ivyiyumviro vyabo.

Mu Bufaransa, aba Physiocrats baronse uburoranirwa butangaje igihe Turgot yashirwa mu kibanza c'Umugenzuzi Mukuru w'Ivy'Imari mu 1774.

![Ishusho ya Turgot](assets/en/060.webp)

Kubera yari azi ko izina ryabo ririko riragabanuka, Turgot ntiyigeze yiyerekana nk'umuyoboke w'aba Physiocrats w'umwizigirwa, canke, ku bijanye n'ivyo, nk'umwe mu banditse Encyclopédie, naho yari umwe muri bo, kuko abakuru b'idini babakengera. Amaze kuronka ubutegetsi, Turgot yasohoye amabwirizwa atandatu azwi cane yabaye intango yo gushira mu ngiro umugambi wa Physiocracy: umwidegemvyo w'ubudandaji, umwidegemvyo w'akazi, n'iherezo ry'ukwiharira amasoko.


![Ibwirizwa ry'Umwami](assets/en/061.webp)

Igihe c'Impinduramatwara y'Ubufaransa kigeze, aba Physiocrats bari bafise abayoboke bakeya. Marquis de Mirabeau yitavye Imana ku wa 13 Mukakaro 1789, ni ikimenyetso gikomeye. Abeille yari yavuyemwo, mugabo Dupont de Nemours yagumye umwizigirwa. Ashizwe mu Nama Nshingamateka, Dupont de Nemours yatwaye ijwi rya Physiocracy kandi asaba amahinduka mu vy'ubutunzi ashigikira ugutunga umutungo n'ubudandaji bwidegemvya.

![Ukwitaba kw'Inama Nkuru y'Igihugu i Versailles mu 1789](assets/en/062.webp)

Yararwanije kandi, ariko ntiyaroranirwa, assignats. Naho ivyo vyanse, ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy vyagumye biri mu mpaka z'abanyabwenge kandi vyagize ingaruka ku vyakozwe mu ntango z'Impinduramatwara. Nk'uko Joseph Rambaud yavuze, ivyo vyose Impinduramatwara yakoze mu kushigikira umwidegemvyo, twabikesha aba Physiocrats.

![Adam Smith, “Wealth of Nations”, Joseph Rambaud, “History of economic doctrines”](assets/en/063.webp)


### Aba Physiocrats na Adam Smith

Aba Physiocrats bagize kandi **ububasha bukomeye ku mateka y'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi**. Adam Smith, uwagendeye Ubufaransa kandi yahuye na benshi muri bo, yakozweko cane n'ibikorwa vyabo. Yiyumviriye mbere kwegurira Quesnay igitabu [*The Wealth of Nations*](https://planb.academy/resources/books/the-wealth-of-nations-c3e78eda-cc44-4cae-8460-f962148aa289).



Ikibabaje, Quesnay yitavye Imana imyaka ibiri imbere yuko igitabu ca Smith gisohoka, kandi Smith yakuye iryo jambo ryo kwegurira. Naho Smith ntiyemera iciyumviro cabo c'uko uburimyi bwonyene ari bwo bwimbura, yaremeye vyinshi mu vyiyumviro vyabo bishigikira amasoko yidegemvya kandi arabijana imbere mu bikorwa vyiwe bwite.


## Dupont de Nemours

<chapterId>6d585e0f-93b8-5b0a-a0a8-7f6e4a5bc68e</chapterId>




### Kuva ku guhura ku bwotoro gushika ku kuba uwukundwa cane na Quesnay

Muri Amerika, tuzi isosiyete DuPont, izwi kandi nka "E.I. du Pont de Nemours and Company", isosiyete ikorera mu bihugu vyinshi mu ndimiro ya shimi n'ubumenyi bw'ibinyabuzima.


![Isosiyete Dupont, Dupont de Nemours](assets/en/064.webp)

Uno musi, iradandaza ibirenga imiriyaridi 35 z'amadolari kandi ikoresha abantu bagera ku 65.000 kw'isi yose. Vyaragaragaye ko iyo sosiyete ifitaniye isano ikomeye n'ubuzima bwa Samuel-Pierre Dupont de Nemours, umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi w'Umufaransa w'ishure ry'ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy.




Yavutse mu 1739, Dupont de Nemours yegereye aba Physiocrats ku myaka 24. Ico gihe, yari aracarondera inzira yiwe mu buzima. Umusi umwe, yashikira agatabu gato cari The Wealth of the State. Yasanze ivyiyumviro vyaco vy'ivy'ubutunzi ari ubusa kandi yandika inyishu inegura yitwa Reflections on the Wealth of the State, yasohowe mu 1763. Iyo nyandiko yakiriwe neza, kandi abasoma barayihayagije, bavuga amajambo nk'aya: "Utegerezwa kuba umuyoboke wa Mirabeau!" Ikitwengeje, Dupont ntiyari mbere azi Mirabeau uwo ari we.


![Marquis de Mirabeau, Journal of agriculture, trade and finance](assets/en/065.webp)

Kubera yipfuza kumenya, yatanguye kusoma ibikorwa vya Mirabeau, *Friend of the People* na *Theory of Taxation*. Yahuye na Mirabeau na François Quesnay, hanyuma yinjira mu ishure ryabo.
Mu 1765, Dupont yahawe ikibanza c'umutunganya w'ikinyamakuru *Journal de l'agriculture, du commerce et des finances*, ikinyamakuru rya mbere ryo mu gihe caco mu ndimiro y'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi.




Hari imvo zibiri z'ivyo: Mirabeau na Quesnay bategerezwa guceceka, kandi Dupont yabonwa nk'inyenyeri yabo iriko iraduga.

Abagize ishure ry'ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy baremeranya ko Dupont de Nemours ntiyatevye kuba uwukundwa cane na Quesnay. Quesnay yavuze umusi umwe, ati: "Nimwitwararike uyu musore, ni we azovuga igihe tuzoba twapfuye". Umwe mu ba Physiocrats bagenzi biwe, Abeille, yagize mbere ishari ku bwitwararika Dupont yaronka kandi yiyonjorora ku ishure rya Quesnay.

### Umwubatsi w'ibisohokayandikiro vya Physiocracy

Dupont de Nemours yaguma yubaha cane Quesnay.

![Quesnay, Journal des Physiocrates](assets/en/066.webp)

Yahavuye avuga: "Nari umwana gusa igihe Quesnay yanyerekera amaboko; yangize umugabo." Quesnay ni we yamugize umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi akomeye mu ruhando rw'ubwanditsi rwo mu gihe ciwe.
Inyuma y'ikinyamakuru *Journal de l'agriculture, du commerce et des finances*, Dupont yashizwe mu kibanza c'umutunganya w'*Les Éphémérides du Citoyen*, ikinyamakuru cabaye ijwi ryemewe ry'aba Physiocrats.



Yagize ico koraniro c'ibinyamakuru ikibanza gikomeye c'inyigisho y'ivy'ubutunzi, akigumiriza mbere mu gihe aba Physiocrats bariko barahungabana, mu kwandika ubwiwe hafi ibitabu vyose vyaje inyuma. Ni Dupont de Nemours, ikirenzeko, yahimbye ijambo "physiocracy", rivuye mu majambo abiri y'ikigereki asobanura "ubutegetsi bwa kamere". Yakoresheje iryo jambo nk'izina ry'ikoraniro ry'ibiganiro vya Quesnay casohowe mu 1768, kandi iryo jambo amaherezo ryagumye mu mateka. Birazwi ko hagati yabo, aba Physiocrats bitana "abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi", kandi baguma bitwa gutyo no mu gihe c'Impinduramatwara.


![Turgot, Physiocracy, ikoraniro ry'inyandiko za Quesnay](assets/en/067.webp)

### Ubuzima bwuzuye ukwemera

Igihe Turgot yamara akanya gato ari umushikiranganji w'ivy'imari w'Ubufaransa, Dupont yabaye umuhanuzi wiwe wa hafi, umu Physiocrat wenyene yashobora kumushikira, kuko Turgot yaguma kure y'abandi.



Mu gihe c'Impinduramatwara, yatowe mu karere ka Nemours kandi yasanze ari mu Nama Nshingamateka, aho hari kandi uwundi Bwana Dupont. Baciye bamwita Dupont de Nemours, atari kubera yari umunyacubahiro, mugabo gusa kugira batandukanye abo babiri. Iryo zina, nk'uko bizwi, ryagumye.

Mu gihe c'Impinduramatwara, mu Myandagaro 1792, yafashe ibirwanisho kugira akingire Umwami mu ngoro ya Tuileries imbere y'ishengero ry'abantu.

![Joseph Schumpeter,” History of economic analysis”, Ukwigarurirwa kw'ingoro ya Tuileries mu 1792](assets/en/068.webp)

Umwami yamubwiye: "Bwana Dupont, tuguhora dusanga aho ukenewe!" Amaze kurokoka ku gitangaro igihe c'ubwicanyi bw'Impinduramatwara, aciriwe urubanza kandi ariko arindiriye guillotine mugabo yakizwa n'uguhirika kwa Robespierre, yasunikiwe mu bugarariji mu ngoma ya Napoléon kandi yaronse akanyamuneza muri Amerika, aho umwe mu bahungu biwe yashinze, afashijwe na se, isosiyete Dupont.




Naho yagize ubwo buzima bwuzuye ibintu, mu bwo yasohoye amajana y'ibiganiro, udutabu n'ibitabu, aracazwi bikeyi gushika uno musi. Ivyo birashoboka ko biva ku kuba yagumye umu Physiocrat ukomeye mu gihe iyo nyigisho yari itakundwa. Mu vy'ukuri, nk'uko Schumpeter yanditse, Dupont de Nemours yagumye umwizigirwa kuri Physiocracy "mu buzima bwiwe bwose bw'umwuga aho yaronse uburyo bwinshi bwo kuyihakana". **Yari umugabo w'ukwemera**.

# Ikiringo c'Ubwenge n'ubutunzi bwa politike

<partId>d1c885ad-7cb1-5f81-816c-be312719d9f8</partId>

## Voltaire n'abafilozofe

<chapterId>16bcf7bf-dad4-5113-8c64-2800f04ff07b</chapterId>

### Ivy'ubutunzi mu Encyclopédie

Ikinjana ca 18 mu Bufaransa carabonye ivy'ubutunzi bivuka nk'ubumenyi n'ishure rya mbere ry'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi: Physiocracy. Adam Smith yize ivy'ubutunzi muri ico kiringo, kandi abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'Abafaransa bamenyekanye kw'isi yose nk'abayobozi. Yamara, ico kinjana cibukwa kuruta nk'ikinjana ca filozofiya aho kuba c'ivy'ubutunzi. Naho urunani rwa Physiocracy rwahuje, mu ntumbero zarwo, na filozofiya y'Ikiringo c'Ubwenge, ingene abafilozofe, canecane Diderot na Voltaire, bafashe ibintu birakwiye kwihwezwa neza. Tuzobona ko abanyavyiyumviro b'Ikiringo c'Ubwenge bagize uruhara runini mu gukwiza iciyumviro ca laissez-faire mu Bufaransa.


![Adam Smith, Encyclopedia](assets/en/069.webp)

Ico Kiringo c'Ubwenge ca filozofiya cashitseko kizwi cane ni, ata gukeka, *Encyclopédie* ya Diderot na d'Alembert.




Kamere, ibiganiro vy'ivy'ubutunzi vyanditswe n'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi. Ku bitabu vya mbere, Diderot yitura Forbonnais, hanyuma yinjiza abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'umwidegemvyo: ubwa mbere François Quesnay (uwatanze ibiganiro nka "Grains", "Fermiers", "Hommes", ico ca nyuma ntikaba casohowe), hanyuma Turgot (uwanditse "Foires et marchés"). Izo nyandiko zifise akamaro kanini. Mu biganiro vyiwe, Quesnay yashinze ishimikiro ry'ico cahavuye kuba inyigisho ya Physiocracy. Hamwe na *Tableau économique* yiwe, izo ni zo nyandiko ziwe zizwi cane. Turgot, ico gihe yari acakiri muto, yateje imbere iciyumviro ca laissez-faire mu kiganiro ciwe, anegura ukwivanga kwa Leta mu gutunganya isoko.

Mu bindi biganiro vyinshi vy'*Encyclopédie*, abafilozofe, canecane Diderot, baburanira intumbero y'umwidegemvyo mu vyose: mu vy'idini, mu vya politike no mu vy'ubutunzi.

![Denis Diderot](assets/en/070.webp)

Umwidegemvyo w'akazi, canecane mu kurwanya uburyo bw'amashirahamwe y'abakozi, wari ingingo yagarukwako kenshi mu biganiro bitandukanye nka Arts, Métier, na Communauté.

### Isano igoye ya Diderot n'umwidegemvyo

Inzira Diderot yaciyemwo mu nyigisho y'ivy'ubutunzi iratangaje. Mu Encyclopédie, yari umuharanira ukomeye w'umwidegemvyo mu vy'ubutunzi kandi ni we yarondeye ugukorana kw'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'umwidegemvyo, nk'uko ikete rimwe ryerekana ibiganiro Turgot yari kumwandikira ribyemeza. Mu myaka ya mbere ya Physiocracy, yari uwushigikira kandi uwuteza imbere ishure rya Quesnay. Mu 1769 no mu 1770, yanditse mu *Les Ephémérides du Citoyen*, ivyo bikababaza cane abagenzi biwe b'abafilozofe barwanya umwidegemvyo, nka Melchior Grimm; yamara, ntiyatevye kwiyonjorora ku wo mugwi. Akwegwa n'ubwenge bukomeye bwa Abbé Galiani, yamufashije gusohora igitabu ciwe ku budandaji bw'impeke mu gifaransa, mu gihe Galiani yari hafi kugaruka mu Butaliyano.


![Dialogue on the wheat trade, ca Galiani, Citizen's ephemerides](assets/en/071.webp)



Ico gitabu cahavuye kuba igitero gikaze kuruta ibindi vyose vyakorewe ivyiyumviro vya Physiocracy, gitera igikumu gikomeye kuri urwo runani. Hanyuma, Diderot yaburaniye Galiani imbere ya Abbé Morellet, umugenzi wa hafi w'aba Physiocrats, mu gitabu ciwe *Apology of Galiani*. Nyuma y'imyaka mikeyi, mu gihe Turgot yari umushikiranganji, Diderot yabonywe ariko arakoma amashi ku kushingwa kw'umwidegemvyo w'akazi biciye ku gukurwaho kw'amashirahamwe y'abakozi. Ico gihe, Diderot, umuhungu w'umunyabukorikori, yasanze ahuje n'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'umwidegemvyo kandi yandika ikete rikaze kuri Galiani, uwavuga ko umwidegemvyo w'akazi wari guhonya inganda z'Abafaransa mu myaka mirongo ibiri canke mirongo itatu. Ku bijanye n'Impinduramatwara y'Inganda, amateka yerekana ukundi. Amaze gusubira ku ruhande rw'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'umwidegemvyo, Diderot ntiyaba agifise ukwizigirwa kwabo kandi yagumye wenyene. Ikintu cerekana neza ni ikete yarungikiye Dupont de Nemours mu 1774, aho yandika:

> Kera warankunda; ubu ntukunda, kubera ufise akazi kwinshi ku buryo utagifise umwanya wo gukunda umuntu.

![Voltaire](assets/en/072.webp)

### Voltaire hagati y'ukuhayagiza n'urwenya rukebura

Voltaire yaciye mu nzira nk'iyo, irangwa n'ukutahuza mu vyiyumviro vyiwe vy'ivy'ubutunzi. Yashima Vincent de Gournay, yandikirana n'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi (harimwo Dupont de Nemours na Turgot), kandi yaha agaciro aba Physiocrats-canecane kubera bahayagiza uburimyi. Yarabahayagije mu gitabu *Diatribe to the Author of the Ephémérides*. Yamara, yahavuye anegura iciyumviro cabo c'ikori rimwe ku matongo mu gitabu ciwe c'urwenya *The Man of Forty Crowns*, ico cavyuye kandi impaka nyinshi. Ubwa nyuma, nk'uko Diderot yagize, yanezerejwe n'igihe Turgot yari umushikiranganji, akita ico gihe igihe c'inzahabu kandi ahayagiza amabwirizwa yiwe abiri akomeye ku mwidegemvyo w'akazi n'ubudandaji bwidegemvya.

![Voltaire, “L'homme aux quarante écus”](assets/en/073.webp)

Amaherezo, isano y'abafilozofe n'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi ivanze. Naho bahayagiza kandi banegura ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi vy'umwidegemvyo, bagize uruhara mu kuzana ivyo vyiyumviro mu biganiro bininiya vy'abanyabwenge b'Ikiringo c'Ubwenge. Muri ubwo buryo, baba babishaka canke batabishaka, barafashije iciyumviro ca laissez-faire gutera imbere gushika ku Mpinduramatwara.

## Turgot, Umunyanyigisho

<chapterId>a8cd8185-8351-556b-8011-3a0c313e8a9d</chapterId>

### Ingene umuhinga mukuru mu vy'ubutunzi yavuye

Mu kigabane kizwi cane c'igitabu ciwe *History of Economic Thought*, umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi w'Umunyamerika Murray Rothbard yahayagije ico yita "ubuhinga budasanzwe" bwa Turgot.




amwerekana nk'umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi akomeye kuruta abandi bo mu kinjana ca 18, hamwe na Cantillon.

![Turgot, Montesquieu, “The Spirit of Laws”, Encyclopedia](assets/en/074.webp)

Turgot yashikira izina nk'iryo gute? Vyavuye ku bintu bitatu vy'ishimikiro bihurikiye hamwe. **Ubwa mbere, umuryango wiwe w'iteka**. Yavuye mu murongo murerure w'abayobozi b'umwami, benshi muri bo bafise amabanga makuru mu butegetsi. **Ubwa kabiri, igihe c'inzahabu yavukiyemwo**. Turgot yari afise imyaka 21 igihe Montesquieu asohora *L'Esprit des Lois*, kandi imyaka 24 igihe igitabu ca mbere c'*Encyclopédie* gisohoka.





Yari mu gihe kimwe n'aba Physiocrats, Voltaire, Diderot, d'Holbach, Adam Smith, Condorcet, n'abandi. **Ubwa gatatu, ubwenge bwiwe budasanzwe akiri muto**. Igihe yari umunyeshure kuri Sorbonne, yanditse ikete ku mahera y'impapuro ku myaka 22, yashikirije amajambo atangaje, kandi ku myaka 24 yanditse urutonde rw'ibikorwa 52 yipfuza kwandika.


Naho yari akiri muto, Turgot yagize uruhara mu *Encyclopédie*, yandika ibiganiro ku bintu nka "Etymology", "Existence", "Expansibility", "Fairs", na "Foundations". Ikiganiro kimwe gusa ni co cavuga ku vy'ubutunzi bwa nyene: ikiganiro ciwe ku "Fairs", ico casigura inkomoko y'imurikagurisha n'amasoko kandi kanegura ukwivanga kwa Leta kwariko kwiyongera kwabatengeza kandi kwabapfisha.


Muri iyo myaka ya mbere, yatojwe na Vincent de Gournay, uwamufashe akiri muto kandi bacudika. Gournay amaze kwitaba Imana mu 1759, Turgot yanditse ijambo ryo kumuhayagiza aho yatanze incamake nziza cane y'inyigisho ya laissez-faire. Canecane yanditse:

"Mu buryo bwose ubudandaji bushobora kugirira Leta ico buvuze, inyungu bwite y'umuntu irekuwe izohora yimbura iciza rusangi mu buryo bwizigiwe kuruta ibikorwa vy'ubutegetsi, ivyo bihora bifise agahaze kandi bikayoborwa n'inyigisho itomoye kandi itizigirwa".


![Turgot, “Praise for Gournay”, Turgot, “Reflections on the formation and distribution of wealth”](assets/en/075.webp)

### Incamake y'ivy'ubutunzi vy'umwidegemvyo

Mu 1767, igihe yari intendant, yanditse incamake y'ivy'ubutunzi ku zina rya [*Reflections on the Formation and Distribution of Wealth*](https://planb.academy/resources/books/turgot-oeuvres-completes-37fa0489-cabd-413c-9240-34d1663d0720).



Ugusaranganya akazi, ubukuru bw'abaguzi, umutungo bwite, uruhara rw'umutahe, hafi ingingo zose nkuru z'ivy'ubutunzi ziravugwa. Abahinga benshi mu mateka, uwa nyuma akaba umuhinga mu mateka Anne-Claire Hoyng, barerekanye ingene ico gikorwa ca Turgot gisa n'igitabu ca Adam Smith [*The Wealth of Nations*](https://planb.academy/resources/books/the-wealth-of-nations-c3e78eda-cc44-4cae-8460-f962148aa289), casohowe inyuma y'imyaka icenda.

![Turgot, “Letters on the freedom of grain trade”, Turgot na Smith,, ”A strange closeness”](assets/en/076.webp)

Turgot yaburaniye umwidegemvyo w'ubudandaji bw'impeke mu makete yandikiye Abbé Terray, ayahavuye ashikirizwa Umwami, mugabo igice cayo ubu cabuze. Yanditse:


> Bwana, nimba hariho ikintu kihutirwa, ntiyo kushiraho inzitiro nshasha ku budandaji bw'ishimikiro kuruta ubundi bwose, mugabo ni ugukuraho izo, mu buryo buteye agahinda, zarekewe kuguma. 
> Nimba hariho igihe umwidegemvyo wuzuye, utagira agahaze, utagira inzitiro n'imwe, wari ngombwa, ndashize ubutinyuka mvuge ko ari ubu, kandi ko ntibigeze biba bibi cane nk'ubu kwiyumvira gusohora ibwirizwa ku budandaji bw'impeke.




Mu 1769, Turgot yanditse ikiganiro Value and Money ku bwa Abbé Morellet ku gitabu *Dictionnaire de Commerce*, ico amaherezo ntikaba casohowe. Galiani yari yamaze kwerekana ko "umuntu ni ingero isanzwe y'ibintu vyose", abanjirije isesengura rishingiye ku ciyumviro c'umuntu Turgot yahavuye ateza imbere imyaka mirongo itatu inyuma muri ico kiganiro, aho arikwiragiza kandi arishinga.

Mu 1770, imbere cane ya Bentham, Turgot yanditse inyandiko iharanira umwidegemvyo w'ibipimo vy'inyungu n'ugutiza amahera ku nyungu nyinshi.


![Turgot, “Mémoire sur les prêts d'argent”](assets/en/077.webp)

"Ni ikosa kwiyumvira ko inyungu ku mahera mu budandaji ikwiye gushingwa n'amategeko y'abaganwa", avuga, "Ni igiciro c'ubu kirigenga ubwaco, nk'ic'ibindi bicuruzwa vyose", Mu kuburanira ico ciyumviro, ahirika ukurwanya kwa Aristote n'Abavyeyi b'Ekleziya.

### Ukudashoboka kw'ugutegekanya vyose bivuye hejuru

Incamake itangaje y'inyigisho ya laissez-faire ya Turgot iboneka mu kete ryibagiwe ryo mu 1773 ryandikiwe Abbé Terray ku bijanye n'ugushira ikimenyetso ku vyuma:


> Ico politike itegerezwa gukora ni ukwemera inzira ya kamere n'inzira y'ubudandaji, iyo itari ngombwa buke kandi itananiza buke nk'inzira ya kamere ubwayo, ata kugerageza kuyiyobora; kuko kugira uyiyobore utayitengeza kandi utiyonona, wokwiye kuba ushobora gukurikira impinduka zose z'ivyo abantu bakeneye, z'inyungu zabo n'iz'ibikorwa vyabo; wokwiye kuvyimenya mu buryo burambuye ku buryo bidashoboka ku mubiri kuvyironka, kandi aho mbere n'ubutegetsi bufise ubuhinga, umwete n'ubwitonzi kuruta ayandi buzohora bugeramiwe no kwibeshya, n'imiburiburi ku gice kimwe c'ibiri.

Ng'aha dusanga insiguro itomoye cane y'inyigisho ya laissez-faire, hamwe n'ukubanjiriza isesengura rya Friedrich Hayek ku kwiyita ko umuntu azi vyose, ni ukuvuga ukudashoboka kwa Leta kutahura neza ububasha bw'ivy'ubutunzi kugira ngo ibugenzure.




![Friedrich A. Hayek](assets/en/078.webp)

## Turgot, Umuhinduzi w'ibintu

<chapterId>9177429f-1679-51c4-bfd2-dd036d24a1cc</chapterId>

### Intendant wa Limousin atabishaka

Nk'uko twabikwiye muri make mu gice giheruka, Turgot yari umuhungu w'umuryango uzwi cane wari wiyerekanye mu nzego zo hejuru z'abakozi ba Leta y'Ubufaransa.


![Turgot](assets/en/079.webp)

Se yari yabaye umukuru w'abadandaza b'i Paris kandi sekuru yari intendant. Amaze kwiga neza cane, uwo muto w'umuryango wa Turgot yagomba kushika n'imiburiburi ku ntambwe nk'izo. Ubwa mbere yakoze igihe nk'umuyobozi ashikiriza amasaba, ni ukuvuga yari umuhuza w'aba intendant na Versailles. Cari ikibanza c'iteka, ico yategerezwa kuronkera uruhusha rudasanzwe ku bijanye n'imyaka, mugabo Turgot yipfuza ibirenze ivyo. Urupfu rw'umwigisha wiwe, Gournay, rwarushirije kumuremesha kwipfuza ibiri hejuru, kandi yasavye ko ashirwa mu kibanza c'intendant.


Mu 1759, ubwa mbere yasavye intendance ya Grenoble, ariko yaranse. Baciye bamuha ikibanza c'umukuru w'abadandaza b'i Lyon, ico yanse. Yasavye intendance ya Bretagne, mugabo na yo yaranse. Amaherezo, mu 1761, bamuhaye intendance ya Limousin kandi, atabishaka cane, yaremera. Yandikiye Voltaire: "Ndafise ivyago vyo kuba intendant", ashobora kuba yashaka kuvuga: Ndafise ivyago vyo kuba intendant muri Limousin.


Muri Limousin, abarimyi bari bakene kandi babayeho mu buzima buhavye, canecane ku bijanye n'amazu n'ibifungurwa. Urwego rusangi rw'ubumenyi rwari hasi cane. Amabarabara make yariho yari mu buryo buteye ubwoba.


![J.F. Millet, “The Gleaners”, 1857](assets/en/080.webp)

Kubera ko ico kibanza cari gikene cane, ntikari gifise ico kivuze ku bashikiranganji. Ivyo vyahaye Turgot umwidegemvyo wo kugerageza amahinduka. Muri Limousin, yakurikiranye imigambi itatu ikomeye:

- **Ugusubira kugabura taille**, ikori ry'umuntu ku giti ciwe (Turgot yagomba gushiramwo ubutungane bwinshi bushoboka mu kurihimba).
- **Corvée**, ubwoko bw'ikori ririhwa mu kazi, aho abarimyi bahatirwa kukorera mu kwubaka amabarabara. Amaze kuzunguruka intara, Turgot ntiyatevye kubona ko amabarabara ari mu buryo bubi. Yasubirije corvée n'ikori ririhwa mu mahera.
- **Ukwinjiza abasirikare b'imbonerakure**, ingabo z'abarimyi zatabazwa mu bihe vy'intambara.

![Ugutora ku bwotoro kw'abasirikare b'imbonerakure](assets/en/081.webp)

Ivyo vyakorwa mu gutora ku bwotoro, ivyo vyazana ubwoba n'urugomo kubera abahunga; Turgot yasubirije ayo makori y'agahato n'abitanga baronka impembo.

Ayo mahinduka yaroraniwe ata gukeka, kandi mu Mukakaro 1774, Turgot yashizwe mu kibanza c'umushikiranganji. Kubera atari afise uburambe, Umwami yabanje kumushira mu Bushikiranganji bw'Ivy'Amato. Iryo shirwaho ryatwenzwe na benshi. Turgot ubwiwe yemeye ati: "Nta co nzi ku bijanye n'ivy'amato", kandi Voltaire yavuze ati: "Sinibaza ko Turgot ari umunyamato kuruta jewe".


### Amabwirizwa atandatu yo guhindura Ubufaransa

Ukwezi kumwe gusa inyuma, Turgot yashizwe mu kibanza c'Umugenzuzi Mukuru w'Ivy'Imari, mu vy'ukuri aba umushikiranganji w'ivy'ubutunzi n'ivy'imari w'Ubufaransa.

Turazi ikete aho yashikirije Louis XVI ingingo ngenderwako ziwe: "Nta guhomba. Nta kwiyongereza amakori. Nta nguzanyo nshasha". Iyo filozofiya, iyo abanegura b'uno musi boshobora kwita "ubukazi" bw'ubusa, yagamije gukiza ingoma y'umwami.

![Igitabu c'ivyidodombera vy'akarere ka Nemours, Edit du Roy](assets/en/082.webp)

Turgot yateguye amabwirizwa atandatu y'umwami yo guhindura ubutunzi bw'Ubufaransa.



Ni ubwa mbere (kandi bumwe mu bwa nyuma cane) umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi yahawe umwidegemvyo wo guhindura ubutunzi bw'igihugu ciwe. Atatu mu mabwirizwa ya Turgot arihariye: rimwe ryakuyeho akazi k'agahato (corvées), irindi ryasenye amashirahamwe y'imyuga (corporations), kandi irya gatatu ryashinze ubudandaji bwidegemvya bw'impeke.

### Uguhirika kw'igihe c'inzahabu

Yinjiye mu bushikiranganji, Turgot yari azi ko yohura n'ukurwanywa n'imiryango ifise uburenganzira bwihariye. "Nzotinywa, mbere nzokwankwa, na benshi bo ku rugo rw'umwami n'abarondera ubutoni bwose", ni ko yabwiye Umwami. Abo bafise uburenganzira bwihariye ntibatevye kwifatanya kumurwanya kandi batuma yirukanwa ku kibanza c'Umugenzuzi Mukuru w'Ivy'Imari. Voltaire, uwashigikira rwose amahinduka ya Turgot, yanditse mu makete yiwe:

> Ah! Inkuru mbi ndumvise! Tuzobandukira he? Ndavunitse! Ntituzokwigera dusubira kwiyubaka inyuma y'ukwibonera ukuvuka n'urupfu kw'igihe c'inzahabu! Ico ndurumo cakubise umuzirikanyi wanje n'umutima wanje.

![Voltaire, Louis XVI](assets/en/083.webp)

Kuva ico gihe, benshi bavuze ko Ubufaransa ari igihugu bidashoboka guhindura. Ukunanirwa kwa Turgot vyasa n'ivyabyemeza: yari yategujwe n'ikinjana c'igice c'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi vy'umwidegemvyo; yari yashigikiwe n'abafilozofe b'Ikiringo c'Ubwenge; kandi Umwami ubwiwe, afise ubutegetsi bwose, yaramushigikira.


Amaherezo, parlements n'imiryango ifise uburenganzira bwihariye ni bo batsinze. Ico Umwami yashobora gukora conyene cari ukwinuba mu mpisho: "Ubu ndabona ko Bwana Turgot na jewe ni twebwe twitwararika abanyagihugu."

## Condillac

<chapterId>0ba8dbb5-dcd5-5981-bf85-6c185e0bf192</chapterId>

### Iciyumviro c'ubuhendanyi ca Montaigne n'ikinyoma c'uko ico umwe aronka ari ico uwundi ahomba

Nk'uko mercantilism, iyo ari yo yerekana ico ciyumviro mu ngiro mu buryo butomoye cane, iciyumviro c'ubuhendanyi ca Montaigne caramaze igihe kirekire kitazimira.

![Michel de Montaigne, Étienne Bonnot de Condillac](assets/en/084.webp)

Ico ciyumviro c'ubuhendanyi ni iciyumviro c'uko ubudandaji n'uguhanahana ari umukino aho ico umwe aronka ari ico uwundi ahomba. Ico uruhande rumwe ronse mu gikorwa c'ubudandaji, uruhande rwa kabiri rutegerezwa kuba rwabihombye. Abakishigikira bavuga ko ivyo ari ukuri hagati y'abantu ku giti cabo no hagati y'amakungu.

Abbé wa Condillac yagize **uruhara runini mu gusenya ico ciyumviro c'ikinyoma burundu**.



"Burundu" birashobora kurenza urugero, kuko mu mpaka za bose, ico ciyumviro c'ubuhendanyi kigaruka kenshi. Ivyo birashoboka ko ni co gituma, ikirenzeko, Condillac aguma ari umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi atazwi cane. Uretse abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi ba Otirishiya, ni bake batahuye akamaro k'inyigisho yiwe y'uguhanahana, kandi nta muntu agifise ico amuvuze.

Ku bahinga benshi mu mateka y'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi, umwaka wa 1776 urangwa n'ugusohorwa kw'igitabu [*The Wealth of Nations*](https://planb.academy/resources/books/the-wealth-of-nations-c3e78eda-cc44-4cae-8460-f962148aa289), ico cashinze ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi. Ivyigwa cumi na bitanu turangije, aho twize ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi bw'Abafaransa imbere ya Adam Smith, birahagije mu kwerekana ikosa ry'ico ciyumviro. Muri uwo mwaka wa 1776 nyene, ikirenzeko, ikindi gitabu gishobora kuba gikwiye guhayagizwa n'abahinga mu mateka kuruta igitabu ca Adam Smith: canditswe na Condillac kandi cimwe gusa [*Commerce and Government Considered in Their Mutual Relationship*](https://planb.academy/resources/books/condillac-le-commerce-et-le-gouvernement-5e397405-e066-43bc-82df-1017c1fb63ae).

![Condillac, Trade and Government](assets/en/085.webp)

### Uguhanahana nk'akamaro ku mpande zose

Kuri Condillac, nimba iciyumviro c'ubuhendanyi c'uguhanahana kubonwa nk'umukino aho ico umwe aronka ari ico uwundi ahomba kiguma kiriho, aba Physiocrats bafise igice c'iryo kosa, kuko bemeza ko uguhanahana ari isano y'ukungana. Nk'uko Condillac abivuga, ivyo ni ibinyoma: uguhanahana ni isano itangana, aho umuntu ahora atanga bike kugira aronke vyinshi.

Hagati y'ivyiyumviro vy'aba Physiocrats n'ivya Condillac, ntiwoshobora kwiyumvira ukudahuza kurenze ukwo. Umuhinga mu vy'ubutunzi w'umu Physiocrat Le Trosne yanditse:

> Uguhanahana ni, ku kamere yaco, amasezerano y'ukungana, akozwe ku gaciro kingana ku gaciro kingana.

Condillac, ku rundi ruhande, yanditse:

> Uruhande rwose rw'amasezerano ruhora rutanga ikintu gifise agaciro gito kugira ronke ikintu gifise agaciro kanini.

Yamara, ukudahuza kwabo ni kwa majambo cane. Bompi baremeranya ko igihe umuntu ahanahanye ifaranga rimwe n'igitabu, ico gitabu kingana n'ifaranga rimwe, canke igiciro c'igitabu ni ifaranga rimwe.



Itandukaniro riri mu kuba Condillac yemeza ko kubera duhanahana ifaranga rimwe n'igitabu, bisobanura ko, kuri twebwe, agaciro k'igitabu kirenze ak'igiceri cacu c'ifaranga rimwe. Duhitamwo igitabu aho kwitorera igiceri, kandi ni co gituma duhanahana.

Inyigisho ya Condillac ntihushanye rwose n'iy'aba Physiocrats, mugabo ntibavuga ururimi rumwe, tuvuge gutyo. Le Trosne avuga ku giciro mu gihe Condillac avuga ku gaciro, kandi ukundi nyene.

Aho Condillac afise ukuri ni uko nimba ibiciro bingana hagati y'ibicuruzwa bibiri bihanahanywa, agaciro ntikangana, kuko bitaba ukwo, nta guhanahana kwobaho.

### Ivyiyumviro bitatu vyahinduye ivy'ubutunzi

Kugira twegeranye inyigisho ya Condillac, ivyiyumviro bitatu birashobora kuvugwa:

Iciyumviro ca mbere: turondera ibicuruzwa kubera akamaro kavyo. Ivyo bisa n'ivyibonekeza, mugabo ni ingingo y'ishimikiro y'ubumenyi bw'ivy'ubutunzi ko abantu bahanahana kugira baronke akamaro, ingingo Jean-Baptiste Say na we yateje imbere neza.

![Jean-Baptiste Say](assets/en/086.webp)

Iciyumviro ca kabiri: agaciro kabaho imbere kandi katuma haba uguhanahana. Ico umuntu wese yiyumvira ku giti ciwe ku bicuruzwa n'ibikorwa gisobanura ko ivyo bicuruzwa n'ibikorwa nyene bifise agaciro kuri we, bifise agaciro kanini canke gito bivanye n'akamaro bisa n'ibitanga. Kamere, umuntu wese abibona ukwundi ku wundi, kandi agaciro karahinduka bivanye n'umuntu ku muntu.

Ubwa nyuma, iciyumviro ca gatatu: igiciro ni inkurikizi y'ibikorwa vy'uguhanahana. Ibicuruzwa ntibihanahanwa ku gaciro nzikwegera, kuko ingingo z'uguhanahana zivana kandi n'agaciro uwudandaza yiha ku bwiwe kuri ivyo bicuruzwa. Isano hagati y'umuguzi n'umudandaza, canke hagati y'ico umuguzi abona ku giti ciwe n'ico umudandaza abona ku giti ciwe, ni yo ishinga igiciro.

Ivyo vyiyumviro vya Condillac ni vy'ishimikiro. Bidufasha kutahura igituma uguhanahana kwose guhora gufitiye akamaro impande zose.

Ni co gituma bisenya rwose ukunegura ubudandaji bwidegemvya, kuko ugukingira ibicuruzwa vyo mu gihugu bibonwa gusa nk'uburyo bubuza abantu kugirirana akamaro. Laissez-faire ni na yo iciyumviro ca nyuma c'ibikorwa vya Condillac. Reka abantu bakore nk'uko bishakiye, kuko nimba ubutegetsi bwa rusangi bukingira umwidegemvyo n'umutungo, abantu bazohora batungisha bagenzi babo mu guhanahana hamwe.

## Condorcet

<chapterId>99e4aba6-da7f-5041-b02f-337158381515</chapterId>

### Umufilozofe yakwegwa n'ivy'ubutunzi

Condorcet ni, ata gukeka, **umufilozofe w'Ikiringo c'Ubwenge yashimikiye cane ku vy'ubutunzi**.


![Nicolas de Condorcet](assets/en/087.webp)

Mu gice kinini c'umwuga wiwe, yiyeguriye indimiro zitajanye n'ivy'ubutunzi na gato, kandi yari afise imvo, kuko impano ziwe zazanye iterambere rikomeye mu bumenyi yashimikiye cane. Mugabo ahagana mu 1770, yumvise ko akwegwa n'ibibazo vy'ivy'ubutunzi, birashoboka ko ari kubera abandi bafilozofe, canecane Voltaire na Diderot, ntibatanga umwanya wabo w'akaruhuko kuri vyo, kandi kubera yari yagiranye ubugenzi na Turgot.


Condorcet yagiye aremera buhorobuhoro ivyiyumviro vya laissez-faire n'umwidegemvyo, mu ntango mu ndimiro zitajanye n'ubutunzi bwa politike. Mu gitabu ciwe *Letter of a Theologian*, yatwenze mu buryo bwa filozofiya idini Gatolika kandi yerekana ko yipfuza kubona umwidegemvyo nyakuri w'ivyo umuntu yizera, harimwo n'umwidegemvyo wo kutizera. Mu rugamba rwiwe rwo guharanira ukwihanganirana no kurwanya ubuhuruduka bw'idini, Condorcet yarwaniye ko Chevalier de la Barre asubizwa iteka kandi ko D'Etallonde, uwari yacirwe urwo gupfa kubera yamennye umusaraba, asubira aburanishwa.


Yari rero yiteguye kuburanira umwidegemvyo igihe umugenzi wiwe Turgot yaduga ku kibanza c'Umugenzuzi Mukuru w'Ivy'Imari.


![Anne Robert Jacques Turgot](assets/en/088.webp)

### Uwaharaniye amahinduka ya Turgot

Kuva ico gihe, umwuga wa Condorcet warahindutse igihe yinjira mu mpaka z'ivy'ubutunzi, ashigikira ataruha amahinduka y'umwidegemvyo y'umushikiranganji mushasha. Ashigikira ihiganwa n'umwidegemvyo w'ubudandaji, Condorcet yasavye kandi ko corvées (akazi k'agahato) n'amashirahamwe y'abakozi bikurwaho, n'uko amakori aciwe mu butungane. Yavuze ivyo yemera ku mugaragaro igihe cose uburyo bwaboneka, abigira n'umwete ubonekera; yanditse cane kandi umusi umwe yavuze mu nyandiko ziwe: "Nimunyemerere kuvuga nanone ku mwidegemvyo w'ubudandaji; ndanezerwa no kuvuga kuri iyo ngingo."

Hagati ya 1774 na 1776, mu gihe Turgot yari umushikiranganji, Condorcet yagize ivyo avuga vyinshi, vyose birangwa n'ukwiyegurira laissez-faire. Ibikorwa vyiwe birimwo *Letters from a Farmer of Picardy to Mr. Necker*, ciburanira ubudandaji bwidegemvya; *Monopolies and Monopolists*, ciharanira ihiganwa ryidegemvya; *Reflections on Corvées*, gisaba ko zikurwaho; na *Reflections on the Grain Trade*, ico gisubira guhayagiza ubudandaji bwidegemvya kandi kinegura igitabu ca Necker kuri ico kintu nyene.

Mu gitabu ciwe [*Letters on the Grain Trade*](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_hg8jFw-y6bwC), Condorcet aravuga ingingo nkuru nyinshi.

![Galiani, “Letters on the grain trade”](assets/en/089.webp)

Ubwa mbere, ibiciro vyinshi vy'impeke vyo ico gihe ntivyavuye ku budandaji bwidegemvya, mugabo ku myimbu mibi, kandi Condorcet arabyerekana neza. Hanyuma asigura igituma laissez-faire itari nziza gusa mugabo ari yo ngingo mfatirwako yonyene ibereye, naho abantu bafise ivyiyumviro vy'ubuhumye. Yerekana ati: "Twamenyereye cane kubona ubutegetsi bwivanga mu budandaji bw'impeke ku buryo ukudakora ikintu bisa n'ikintu kidasanzwe kandi gishasha". Hanyuma yongerako ati: "Hafi bose, canecane abafise amabanga mu butegetsi, biyumvira ko nta kintu kiba ku bwako kandi ko vyose bihera nimba ubutegetsi butivanze muri vyose". Condorcet gutyo asubira kuzana ivyiyumviro vy'aba Physiocrats na Turgot, yerekana ko ubudandaji bwidegemvya buruta ubwoko bwose bw'ukwivanga.


Guteza imbere umugambi w'ivy'ubutunzi w'umwidegemvyo wa Turgot cari igikorwa c'ubutinyuka, kuko Condorcet yari azi ko ntibitevye kugerwako n'ugucungerwa kw'ibisohokayandikiro. Nkako, ivyo vyarabaye kandi agatabu kiwe ku gukurwaho kw'akazi k'agahato caratikijwe kandi cabujijwe mu 1776.


Turgot, ashimira Condorcet ko yaburaniye ivyiyumviro vyiwe n'ubwizigirwa, yamushize mu kibanza c'Umugenzuzi w'Ibiceri. Condorcet yeguye ubwo umugenzi wiwe ava mu bushikiranganji.


### Aho amahinduka yagarukira mu Bufaransa imbere y'Impinduramatwara

Naho yashize inguvu ziwe zose mu kuburanira Turgot, Condorcet ntiyihenda ku vyoheza igihe umugenzi wiwe yari ku butegetsi. Turgot ubwiwe yemeye mu mpera z'ubuzima bwiwe ko yabayeho n'ukwicuza, nk'uko yanditse mu kete, "kubera ntashoboye kugirira igihugu canje n'abantu bose iciza nabona ko coroshe cane". Condorcet, uwabona ibintu nk'uko biri, yerekanye abansi benshi Turgot yohuye na bo: abakundwa b'umwami, parlements, abakuru b'idini, abanyacubahiro, amashirahamwe y'abakozi, n'ibindi. Iyo mirwi yipfuza guhayagizwa, atari amahinduka. Condorcet yabwiye Turgot ati: "Ntiwari umuhendanyi namba, kandi ico ni agahaze, ku vyo ibintu biri kwa i Paris". Yari afise ukuri kwuzuye, kuko mu 1776, imyaka ibiri gusa inyuma y'ukuza kwiwe, Turgot yari yamaze guhatirwa kuva ku kibanza c'Umugenzuzi Mukuru.



## Ubwenge busanzwe bw'umwidegemvyo mu gihe c'Impinduramatwara

<chapterId>95e9a90d-e37a-58ff-b1ac-928b42e76ecf</chapterId>

### Mu kurwanya ukwiharira ivy'amabanki

Dupont de Nemours yari umuyoboke muto cane wa François Quesnay, uwavuze umusi umwe kuri we ati: "Dutegerezwa kwitwararika uyu musore, kuko azovuga igihe tuzoba twapfuye". Igihe Impinduramatwara y'Ubufaransa yatangura, hafi aba Physiocrats bakomeye bose, harimwo Marquis de Mirabeau, bari bamaze kwitaba Imana; Mirabeau ubwiwe yari yitavye Imana ku wa 13 Mukakaro 1789.
Dupont de Nemours yanditse urutonde rw'ivyidodombera vy'akarere ka Nemours.

![Ukwitaba kw'Inama Nkuru y'Igihugu i Versailles, ku wa 5 Rusama 1789, Champagney, “List of Grievances”](assets/en/090.webp)

Iyo nyandiko ikwega cane yarimwo ivyo vyose abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi b'umwidegemvyo bidodombera ku nzitiro ku budandaji, ku kwiharira amasoko, no ku bitero ku mutungo. Dupont de Nemours yatowe kandi mu Nama Nshingamateka.


Mu gihe c'Impinduramatwara, yabaye **ijwi ry'ubwenge busanzwe bw'umwidegemvyo**, ahagarara ashikamye imbere y'umuraba wagenda wiyongera w'ivyiyumviro vya populisme n'amajambo y'uburiganya asaba ukwivanga kwa Leta, ivyo amaherezo vyatsinze.

Kuva mu Munyonyo 1789, igihe bavuga ku kuha ukwiharira isoko Caisse d'Escompte (ivyo vyari gushobora kuyihindura banki ya Leta, Banki y'Ubufaransa), Dupont de Nemours yavuze aburanira ihiganwa.


![Dupont de Nemours, “Speech to the National Assembly”, 1789](assets/en/091.webp)

"Vyoba vyiza", avuga, "kureka ibikorwa vy'amabanki bigengwa n'amategeko y'ubudandaji bwidegemvya". Kandi yongerako:

> Ntahura ico umushikiranganji yashaka kuvuga igihe yavuga ku guha uburenganzira bwihariye Caisse d'Escompte. Nimba ubwo burenganzira bwihariye burimwo ukwiharira, mutegerezwa kubwanka, kuko mwaje ng'aha kugira musenye uburenganzira bwihariye bw'ukwiharira, atari ukurema ubushasha.

Naho yaburiye, Inama Nshingamateka yirengagije ivyo yavuze kandi ifata iyindi ntambwe igana ku kushinga banki nkuru n'ukwiharira ivy'amabanki.
Mu 1790, mu Nama Nshingiro, Dupont de Nemours yakurikiye umuco w'amahoro w'abagenzi biwe ba Physiocrats kandi aciye avuga ibwirizwa ribuza intambara zo gutera abandi. Ingingo ya mbere yavuga: "Igihugu c'Ubufaransa ntikizokwemerera kwinjira mu ntambara iyo yo kwigarurira ibihugu vy'abandi canke yo kurenga ku burenganzira canke ku mwidegemvyo w'igihugu ico ari co cose". 

Ico ciyumviro caranse.

### Urugamba rwo kurwanya assignats

Uwo mwaka nyene, 1790, impaka zatanguye ku gusohora amahera mashasha y'impapuro yo kuriha ivyo Leta ikoresha.
Dupont de Nemours yarabirwanije ashikamye kandi asohora agatabu kitwa [*Effects of Assignats on the Price of Bread*](https://archive.org/details/effetdesassignat00dupo). Izina ryari ryo, kuko yasigura ko ugusohora assignats vyari gushikana ata kabuza ku iyiduga ry'ibiciro, ni ukuvuga ukwiyongera kw'ibiciro vy'ibicuruzwa, harimwo n'umukate.


![Dupont de Nemours, “Effect of Assignats on the Price of Bread”](assets/en/092.webp)

Yashize ku gatabu ko kanditswe n'"umugenzi w'abanyagihugu". Iyo nyandiko yateye urwamo, kuko Inama Nshingamateka yabajije uwo yayanditse. Dupont de Nemours yaciye ahaguruka kandi yemera ko ari igikorwa ciwe, avuga ko atinyinya kwiyita "umugenzi w'abanyagihugu" kuko ukurwanya assignats vyari mu vy'ukuri ukukorera abanyagihugu.
Nanone, impanuro yiwe yirengagijwe, kandi assignats zarasohowe.


![Assignat y'ijana](assets/en/093.webp)

Twese tuzi ivyago vyakurikiye, uguhona kw'ivy'ubutunzi n'imibabaro abanyagihugu bashikiwe, benshi muri bo baratakaje vyose igihe assignats zataye agaciro kandi zitegerezwa guturirwa.
### Umuvugishwa atagira abamwumva

Nyuma y'imyaka mikeyi, Inama Nshingamateka yaraye yitwararika iciyumviro co kurema Banki y'Ubufaransa nyakuri, iyo yohawe ukwiharira ugukora amahera.

Dupont de Nemours, aracarwanya ashikamye iciyumviro c'ibanki ya Leta, abona ko ari ukwiharira gusa, yibukije Inama Nshingamateka ivyago vyaje ku assignats. Yababwiye ati:


> Ntimwiteguze kwicuza nk'ukwo kwababaje bagenzi banje bo mu Nama Nshingiro. Ico gihe, iciyumviro canje co kugabanya assignats zibe zikoreshwa gusa mu kuriha ibintu vy'igihugu kandi ntizigirwe amahera atembera, caranse. Uno musi, baravuga bati: 'Ah, iyo twumvira Dupont de Nemours!'

Mugabo nanone, nta muntu yamwumvise.

## Insozero: Ugutanga urumuri

<chapterId>ada8082f-db96-5e52-954f-719b47998153</chapterId>

### Imishinge yibagiwe

Ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi vy'Abafaransa vyo mu kinjana ca 18 kenshi bicirwa igicucu n'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi bazwi kuruta bo bo mu kinjana ca 19. Yamara, ku bijanye n'ukuburanira umwidegemvyo, ni mu kinjana ca 18 vyose bitangurira vy'ukuri. Ni ico kinjana aho ingingo ngenderwako ya laissez-faire, iyo yari ihindura vyose mu gihe cayo, yavuzwe bwa mbere mu buryo butomoye n'abanyavyiyumviro batari bake, kuva kuri Boisguilbert gushika kuri Dupont de Nemours.
Ni ukuri, abantu nka Jean-Baptiste Say, [Frédéric Bastiat](https://planb.academy/resources/books/bastiat-oeuvres-completes-765be39c-134a-4333-8b4b-e45a4fff7e73), [Gustave de Molinari](https://planb.academy/resources/books/molinari-oeuvres-completes-8a3dbdd8-2053-45bc-9203-dd3b7f3edfee), na Yves Guyot barasize ikimenyetso kiramba mu mateka y'ivyiyumviro vy'ivy'ubutunzi vy'Abafaransa. Mugabo nimba tubiga bonyene, hari ikintu c'ishimikiro tuhusha. Abo banyavyiyumviro bo mu kinjana ca 19 ntibavuye ubusa; bararazwe kandi bubatse ku mishinge yashizwe n'ababanjirije bo mu kinjana ca 18 mu gihe c'Ikiringo c'Ubwenge.

### Ingingo ngenderwako zitandatu zabumbye ivy'ubutunzi vya kino gihe

Boisguilbert yemeza ko abantu b'imitima myiza biyumvira ko bashobora gutorera umuti vyose mu kwinjiza ubutegetsi bahora bahindura ubutunzi bwo hejuru bwo hasi, kandi ko ni co gituma vyiza ni ukureka urutonde kamere rw'ibintu rugende.


![Pierre Le Pesant de Boisguilbert, Cantillon, “Essay on the nature of commerce in general”, Josiah Child, “A treatise on trade”](assets/en/094.webp)

Cantillon yashimikiye ko amahera ntakwiye na rimwe guhindagurwa ku ntumbero za politike. Kwemerera Leta gucungera amahera nk'uko yishakiye ni ukwugurura inzira y'akajagari kanini mu vy'imari no mu vy'ubutunzi.




Vincent de Gournay yemeza ko amabwirizwa arengeje urugero ku kazi aca intege abakozi n'abanyabukorikori, abasunikira mu bunebwe no mu kudakora. Twabikesha ihiganwa ryidegemvya ko ubutunzi bushobora gukura kandi igihugu gishobora kworoherwa.




Marquis d'Argenson yemeza ko nta butegetsi bushobora kubona hakiri kare kandi kugereranya vyose, kandi ko ni co gituma dutegerezwa kwizigira inyungu bwite y'umuntu wese.


![Quesnay, Condillac, D'Argenson](assets/en/095.webp)

Quesnay n'aba Physiocrats bemeza ko umutungo bwite ari ishimikiro ry'imibano y'abantu. Ata mutungo bwite, umuntu atakaza imvo yo kwihatira, yo kukora, kuko adashobora kunezererwa ivyavuye mu kazi kiwe canke kwegeranya ikintu.



Condillac yemeza ko uguhanahana guhora gufitiye akamaro impande zose kandi ko ni co gituma gutegerezwa guhora ari kudegemvya, ata gahaze. Igikorwa conyene ca Leta gitegerezwa kuba ni ugukingira umwidegemvyo n'umutungo.



### Ugutanga urumuri ku kinjana ca 19

Ivyo vyiyumviro vyose vyahavuye bifatwa n'abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi bo mu kinjana ca 19. Bamwe, nka Jean-Baptiste Say, bahaye ivyo vyiyumviro insiguro y'ubumenyi biciye ku gitabu gitorangura *Treatise on Political Economy*. Abandi, ahubwo riho, barondeye kumenyekanisha izo mpanuro z'ishimikiro mu kwinjira mu ndimiro y'inkuru, y'ibitabu vy'uruhimbo n'inkuru ngufi zitwengeje, nk'uko Frédéric Bastiat yabigize n'ubuhinga.
