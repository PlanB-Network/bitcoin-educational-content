Seedkeeper ni smartcard yakozwe na Satochip, ishirahamwe ry’Ababirigi ryitaho ivy’ugutorera umuti ibibazo vy’ubuhinga bwo gucunga no kurinda amabanga y’ubuhinga bwa none. Satochip izwi cane kubera urutonde rw’amakarata y’ubwenge y’ibidukikije vya Bitcoin, yahinguye Seedkeeper nk’ubundi buryo bwo kubika amajambo ya Mnemonic.



Mu majambo nyayo, Seedkeeper ifata uburyo bw'ikarita y'ubwenge ikora vyinshi, yemejwe na EAL6 ifise umurongo w'ubuhinga n'ubuhinga bwo kwibuka bushobora guhindurwa (i.e. "secure element*"). Nk’uko izina ryayo rivyerekana, uruhara rwayo ni ugubika amajambo n’amajambo y’ibanga ya Bitcoin Mnemonic mu buryo buziguye kandi burindwa. Na Seedkeeper, urashobora generate, kwinjiza, gutunganya no kubika amabanga yawe ataco uhinduye mu gice gitekanye c’ikarita.



Mu vyiyumviro vyanje, Seedkeeper ifise imikoreshereze ibiri nyamukuru, tuzoyitohoza mu nyigisho 2 zitandukanye:




- Bitcoin** Ububiko bw’amajambo Mnemonic: aho kwandika amajambo yawe 12 canke 24 ku mpapuro, urashobora kuyashira muri smartcard ukayakingira ukoresheje kode ya PIN.
- Ugucunga ijambobanga**: ushobora generate amajambobanga akomeye biciye ku rubuga rwa Seedkeeper maze ukayabika ataco uhinduye muri smartcard, bikaguha ubuhinga bwo gucunga ijambobanga ryoroshe kandi ryoroshe gukoresha utari mu nzira.



Mu buryo bw’ubuhinga, Seedkeeper ifise ubushobozi bwa bytes 8192, bikaba biyishoboza kubika amabanga 50 atandukanye (umubare nyawo uzovana n’ubunini bwayo be n’amakuru y’imbere ajanye na buri rimwe). Seedkeeper ishobora gushikwako [biciye ku gikoresho co gusoma ikarita y’ubwenge gihuye](https://satochip.io/accessories/) kuri mudasobwa, canke biciye ku gikoresho co kuri telefone ngendanwa gihuye na NFC. Ubuhinga bwose bukora mu buryo butajanye n’umurongo, ata n’imwe ikoreshwa kuri Internet, bikaba vyemeza ko ata gitero gishobora gutera.



![Image](assets/fr/001.webp)



Ikintu gishimishije cane ni ubushobozi bwo gusubiramwo ibiri muri Seedkeeper imwe mu yindi kugira ngo umuntu ashobore gukora backup. Muri iyi nyigisho, tuzokwereka ingene wobikora.



Seedkeeper nayo iraryoshe cane iyo ifatanijwe na Hardware Wallet idafise igihugu nka SeedSigner canke Spectre DIY. Muri ivyo, nta nkeka ko ukoresha mudasobwa ya Satochip canke umukiriya wa telefone ngendanwa. Igikoresho co gucungera imbuto kigumya seed muri secure element yaco kandi gishobora gukoreshwa ata guca ku ruhande n’igikoresho co gusinya, ivyo bikaba bituma udakenera gukoresha kode ya QR y’impapuro. Sinzotegura iyi nkuru y'ikoreshwa muri iyi nyigisho, kuko ari ikiganiro c'iyindi nyigisho yihariye :



https://planb.network/tutorials/wallet/hardware/seedkeeper-seedsigner-45cca4c4-1f22-46bb-87ae-9cddb68aa579

## 1. Ni igiki gikoreshwa ku Mucungezi w’Imbuto?



Muri iyi nyigisho, nzovuga gusa ku bijanye n’ikoreshwa ry’ibintu bijanye na Bitcoin, kuko nivyo iyi nyigisho ivuga. Ntituzoja mu mikorere yo gucunga ijambobanga, ivyo bizoba ari ivy’iyindi nyigisho.



Ugereranije n’ugushiramwo urupapuro rworoshe rw’ijambo Mnemonic, gukoresha Seedkeeper birafise ivyiza vyinshi:





- Ishobora guhangana n’ubusuma:** seed iri muri Wallet yawe ntishobora gushikwako mu nyandiko zitomoye. Kugira ngo uyikuremwo, ukeneye kumenya PIN y’Umucungezi w’Imbuto. Umusuma afata ico gikoresho ntaco azoshobora kugikoresha ataco akoresheje iyo kode.





- Gukwiragiza ingorane ku bintu bibiri:** ushobora kugabanya umutekano hagati y’ikintu ca digitale n’ikintu c’umubiri. Nk’akarorero, niwabika PIN ya Seedkeeper mu mucungerezi w’ijambobanga ryawe, uzokenera uburenganzira bwo gushika kuri uwo mucungerezi no kugira ikarita y’ubwenge kugira ngo uronke seed (ivyo bikaba bigabanya cane ubushobozi bwo guterwa).





- Uburongozi bushingiye ahantu hamwe:** Umucungezi w’imbuto afasha mu gucunga imbuto nyinshi zivuye mu bice bitandukanye.





- Ivyoroshe gucungera:** gusa gusubiramwo ibisubizo vy'ibanga ku bandi Bazigama b'Imbuto.



Ariko rero, hariho ingorane zitari nke ugereranije n’ugushiramwo urupapuro rworoshe rw’igitabu ca seed yawe:





- Igiciro:** naho ari gito (hafi €25), kiracari hejuru y’icy’urupapuro.





- Gushingira ku gikoresho c’ubuhinga rusangi:** Kwinjira no gucunga seed bisaba mudasobwa canke telefone ngendanwa, ivyo bisigura ko Mnemonic yawe ica mu mashini ifise ahantu hagari cane ho gutera kuruta Hardware Wallet. Ivyo birashobora guserura ingorane iyo ikibanza co gukoreramwo gishikiwe n’ingorane. Ni co gituma ntasaba gukoresha Seedkeeper kugira ubike seed ya Hardware Wallet (kiretse gukoresha ata gihugu ata mudasobwa, nk’uko biri kuri SeedSigner). Uruhara rwa Hardware Wallet ni rwo rwo kubika seed mu kibanza gifise umutekano muke cane. Mu kwinjiza seed yawe n’amaboko kuri mudasobwa yawe isanzwe, ntikiguma kuri Hardware Wallet gusa: irahera kandi ku mashini ikoreshwa muri rusangi, ishobora gushikirwa n’ibitero vyinshi. Rero ni vyiza gukoresha Seedkeeper ku Hot Wallet aho gukoresha Cold (kiretse SeedSigner / Hardware Wallet itagira igihugu).





- Ico kibazo co gutakaza gifitaniye isano na PIN:** ukudashobora gushikira seed ataco ushizeko, bitandukanye n’impapuro zo gukingira, biratanga vy’ukuri uburinzi ku busuma bw’umubiri. Ariko nk’uko vyama, umutekano ni igikorwa co gushiramwo uburinganire hagati y’akaga ko kwibwa n’akaga ko gutakaza. Nimba ububiko bwawe busaba PIN, gutakaza iyo kode bizokugirira akamaro ko udashobora kuronka ijambo ryawe rya Mnemonic, gutyo ushobore gushika ku bitcoins zawe.



Kubera ivyo vyiza n’ibibi, mbona ko uburyo bwiza bwo gukoresha Seedkeeper (uretse igikorwa cayo co gucunga ijambobanga) ari, ku ruhande rumwe, kubika imbuto zivuye mu **bitabo vyawe vy’amaporogarama**, kuko zisanzwe ziri kuri telefone yawe canke kuri mudasobwa yawe, canke zivuye mu bikoresho vyawe bitagira igicapo nka Hardware on the Sa Wallets, n’iyi Hardware Wallet itagira igihugu nka SeedSigner, aho vy’ukuri iza mu vyayo.



Ikindi kintu gishimishije cane co gukoresha Seedkeeper ni ubushobozi bwo gushigikira *Ibisobanuro* vy'ibitabo vyawe.



## 2. Noronka gute Umucungezi w’Imbuto?



Hari uburyo bubiri nyamukuru bwo kuronka Umucungezi wawe w’Imbuto. Ushobora [kuyigura ataco uhinduye mu iduka ryemewe rya Satochip](https://satochip.io/igicuruzwa/umucungezi w’imbuto/) canke ku mucuruzi yemerewe. Ariko kuko [applet ya Seedkeeper ari inkomoko yuguruye](https://github.com/Toporin/Seedkeeper-Applet), urafise kandi uburenganzira bwo kuyishiramwo wewe nyene ku [ikarita y’ubwenge idafise ikintu]



Niba wipfuza gukoresha ubuhinga bwo gucungera amakuru bwa Seedkeeper, biragaragara ko uzokenera kugura amakarita abiri y’ubwenge.



## 3. Gushiramwo umukiriya wa Seedkeeper



Muri iyi nyigisho, tugiye gusubiza inyuma igitabo cacu ca seed ku Seedkeeper yacu. Intambwe ya mbere ni ugushira iyo porogarama kuri mudasobwa yawe canke kuri telefone yawe. Kuri PC, uzokenera [gukuraho verisiyo nshasha ya Satochip-Utils](ibisohoka). Ku mbuga ngurukanabumenyi, porogarama ya Seedkeeper iraboneka ku [Iduka rya Google Play] (Iduka ry’Imbuto) be no kuri [Iduka ry’Imbuto rya Apple] (Iduka ry’Imbuto ry’Imbuto).

## 4. Gutangura umucungezi w'imbuto



Ufungure porogaramu maze ukande kuri buto ya "*Click & Scan*".



![Image](assets/fr/003.webp)



Uzosabwa PIN code y’Umucungezi w’Imbuto yawe. Kubera ko iyi ari ikarita nshasha, nta PIN irasobanurwa. Injira kode iyo ari yo yose kugira ngo usimbuke iyi ntambwe, hanyuma ukande "*Ibikurikira*".



![Image](assets/fr/004.webp)



Hanyuma ushire iyo karata inyuma ya telefone yawe. Iryo koraniro rizomenya ko Seedkeeper itaratangura, kandi rizogusaba gushinga kode ya PIN y’ikarita yawe y’ubwenge, ifise uburebure buri hagati y’inyuguti 4 na 16. Kugira ngo ubone umutekano mwiza, hitamwo ijambobanga rikomeye rirerire uko bishoboka kwose, ridasanzwe kandi rigizwe n’inyuguti zitandukanye. Iyi kode ya PIN ni yo yonyene ishobora gutuma udashobora gushika ku majambo yawe yo gukira.



**Ibuka kandi kubika iyi PIN ubu**, nk'akarorero mu mucungerezi w'ijambobanga, canke ku buryo butandukanye. Mu gihe ca nyuma, ntukigere ubika umurongo urimwo PIN ahantu hamwe n’Umucungezi wawe w’Imbuto, ahandi ho uwo mutekano uzoba ubusa. Ni vyiza kugira ububiko bwo kwizigirwa: ata PIN, ntuzoshobora gusubirana amabanga yabitswe kuri Seedkeeper yawe.



![Image](assets/fr/005.webp)



Wemeze kode yawe ya PIN ubugira kabiri.



![Image](assets/fr/006.webp)



Umucungezi w’Imbuto yawe ubu yaratanguje. Ushobora kuyifungura winjije kode ya PIN uherutse gushiramwo.



![Image](assets/fr/007.webp)



Ubu uzojanwa kuri paji y’uburongozi bw’ikarita yawe y’ubwenge.



![Image](assets/fr/008.webp)



## 5. Kwandika seed ku Mucungezi w'Imbuto



Igihe Seedkeeper yawe amaze gufungurwa, ukande kuri buto ya "*+*".



![Image](assets/fr/009.webp)



Hitamwo "Ibanga ry'imbere*". Ihitamwo rya "*Ibanga rya generate*" rigufasha kurema ijambo rishasha rya Mnemonic rivuye muri porogaramu.



![Image](assets/fr/010.webp)



Ku bijanye natwe, turashaka kubika seed mu vyo dufise. Fyonda kuri "*Mnemonic*".



![Image](assets/fr/011.webp)



Ushire "*Ikimenyetso*" kuri iri banga kugira ngo rishobore kumenyekana bitagoranye iyo ubitse amakuru menshi mu Mucungezi wawe w'Imbuto.



![Image](assets/fr/012.webp)



Hanyuma winjize ijambo ryawe ryo gukira mu kibanza catanzwe. Niba ubishaka, urashobora no kwongerako passphrase BIP39 canke *Ibidondora* vyawe. Hanyuma ukande kuri "Import*".



![Image](assets/fr/013.webp)



*Iyo Mnemonic yerekanwa muri iri shusho ni iy'ibinyoma kandi nta n'umwe ari iyo. Ni akarorero gusa. Ntukigere uhishurira umuntu uwo ari we wese Mnemonic yawe bwite, canke bitcoins zawe zizokwibwa

## 7. Gushigikira Umucungezi w'Imbuto



Ubu tugiye gukora backup y’Umucungezi wanje w’Imbuto ku Mucungezi w’Imbuto wa kabiri kugira ngo tugire kopi zibiri. Ivyo bishobora kuba igice c’ingene wokingira ama bitcoins yawe: nk’akarorero, kubika ijambo ryawe ahantu habiri hatandukanye kugira ngo ugabanye ingorane zo ku mubiri, canke kwemera kopi y’incuti wizigirwa nk’igice c’umugambi w’iragi.



Kugira ngo ubikore, jana n’Umucungezi wawe wa kabiri w’Imbuto (wibuke kumenya umwe muri abo babiri afise ikimenyetso kugira ngo ntihagire uwugutera urujijo). Tangana n’ugutanguza, nk’uko vyavuzwe mu ntambwe ya 4 y’iyi nyigisho. Hitamwo ijambobanga rikomeye kandi. Bivanye n’ingene ukoresha, woshobora guhitamwo ijambobanga ritandukanye canke ukaguma ukoresha iryo banga nyene.



![Image](assets/fr/023.webp)



Ugurure porogaramu, ukande kuri "*Click & Scan*", winjize ijambobanga ry'Umucungezi w'Imbuto yawe n°1 (inkomoko), hanyuma uyi scanner.



![Image](assets/fr/024.webp)



Ivyo bigushikana kuri paji y’intango irimwo urutonde rw’amabanga yawe. Fyonda ku tudodo dutoduto dutatu turi hejuru iburyo bwa Interface.



![Image](assets/fr/025.webp)



Hitamwo "*Kora ububiko*", hanyuma ukande "*Tangira*".



![Image](assets/fr/026.webp)



Injira muri PIN code y’ikarita yawe y’inyuma (Umucungezi w’imbuto n°2).



![Image](assets/fr/027.webp)



Hanyuma ushireko iyo karata.



![Image](assets/fr/028.webp)



Kora n'ikarata nyamukuru (Umucungezi w'imbuto n°1), hanyuma ukande kuri "*Kora ububiko*".



![Image](assets/fr/029.webp)



Umucungezi wawe w'Imbuto n°2 ubu arimwo amabanga yose abitswe ku Mucungezi w'Imbuto n°1.



![Image](assets/fr/030.webp)



Ushobora gucapura Seedkeeper yawe n°2 kugira ngo umenye ko amabanga yakopiwe.



![Image](assets/fr/031.webp)
