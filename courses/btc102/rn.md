# Urugendo rwo gushika kuma bitcoins yawe yambere 


Umuryango wa Bitcoin uriko urater'imbere ku rugero rutangaje kandi ritigeze rubaho. Impinduka zijanye n’ubuhinga, ubutunzi, hamwe n'imibano ziterwa n’igikorwa ca Satoshi Nakamoto ziriko zirakomera k'umusi k'umusi, kandi buhoro buhoro ziriko zirugurura amarembo y'isi nshasha, ubu nawe ushobora kuhungukira bimwe bikwiye.


Twinjire muri Bitcoin Rabbit Hole ni urugendo rw’ubwenge ruteye igomwe ruzotuma ushishoza neza kandi ruzoshira muri kabaza ivyo wahora wizera vyinshi. Runo rugendo ntiruzoguha gusa ugutahura kwimbitse ku buryo ama Bitcoins akora, ruzoguha kandi amahirwe ya nyayo y'uguharanira ubwigenge bwawe, gukingira ubuzima bw'ibanga  bwawe hamwe no gushikira ubwigenge nyakuri bw'ivyubutunzi.  


Mukukuyobora neza muri runo rugendo, narashizeho kino cigwa cose kubuntu. Hano, twibanda gusa kuri Bitcoin yonyene. Nta biganiro bitagira akamaro, nta kuvuga ayandi ma cryptocurrencies, ibitahuritse gusa, bifise ihangiro, hamwe n'inyandi zifise aho zigamije.


Kino cigwa cakozwe kugira kiguhe ububasha bwose ku kuntu wiga, kigufasha gutohoza Bitcoin ku rugero rwawe, ugakwirikira neza inzira ihuriranye n'imigambi yawe.

## Amayeri na magendu muvy'ubutunzi

Umuryango wa Bitcoin hamwe n'ibiwukikije biracaribito kandi ntibitunganijwe neza, bivanye n’igihugu. Mugihe bunobwigenge bwugurura amahirwe yagutse, burashiraho kandi urubuga rwoguhenderako abantu(amayeri) biciye muri magendu, amayeri, hamwe n'uburyo bwinshi bwoguhenda mvy'ubutunzi. Nico gituma ikigabane cambere gihambaye cane: gutahura imitego ihurirwako nabenshi bizokugirira akamaro mu kuyirinda. Umutekano wawe w'ivyubutunzi bitegezwa kuza imbere kubera ikintu kibi kigushikiye ntikigutera ingaruka gusa, kigira ingaruka ku muryango wose wa Bitcoin.


### Bitcoin vs cryptos: gutahura itandukaniro


Imbere yo kuja kure, Ningira akamaro gutahura itandukaniro riri hagati y'isi zibiri zitandukanye :


- **Umuryango wa Bitcoin** wubakiye ku ciyumviro c'amahera ya nyayo, cubatswe kubuhinga ataho bugenzurirwa bukomeye, ukudahungabana kw’igihe kirekire, ugukingira amakuru y'ibanga, n'ubwigenge bw'umuntu. Kuva, itangujwe mu 2009, Bitcoin yarakoreshejwe mu buryo bwizigirwa kandi butekanye, ihagarikiwe n'umuryango w’abahinguzi bo kw’isi yose kandi bavyiyeguriye. Si umugenzo ugenda urarengana, ahubwo ni umurongo washinze imizi kandi ushizweho neza wagenewe kuzigama agaciro mu gihe cose.



- **Uruganda rwa Cryptomonnaie**, kurundi ruhande, ni runini cane kandi rurimwo imigambi ibihumbi mirongo itandukanye, imwe yose ifise token yayo. Kino kibanza kenshi kiyobowe n'ubuhinga bwa none bunyaruka, gutera imbere, hamwe n'ugukorera kwihindagurika ry'ibiciro ry'igigihe gito. nyinshi muriyo migambi ifise igenzurirwa, ikingiye buhoro, kandi ntitanga agaciro kanyako-naho hari ukwizeza vyinshi n'isoko yirasa bwampi.

Nimba wipfuza kumenya neza aho Bitcoin yazananye hamwe n'ikiyituma vyukuri itandukana n'iyindi migambi, ndaguhamagariye kuraba kino cigwa cokubuntu gikwirikira kuri kahise ka Bitcoin mu nyuma:

### Amayeri nyamukuru yo kwirinda


Hano hari amayeri ahurirwako na benshi ashobora kwitambika murugendo rwawe:


#### Piramide ya Ponzi n'igishushanyo ca Ponzi

Ivyo ni bimwe mumayeri ahururwako na benshi mw'isi y;ama Crypto. Mugishushanyoca Ponzi, abaja mumugambi ubwambere barahembwa amafaranga yabashasha, ariko arikoatavuye mumitahe nyayocanke mubicuruzwa nyakuri.  Nta gaciro nyako kariko karashirwaho. Ubuhinga bukora gusa iyo abantu bashasha bagumye binjira. Iyo umurindi w'abinjira bashasha ugiye hasi, uwomubwose urasambuka.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/003.webp)


Ibiranga akenshi amayeri:



- Ukwemerera inyungu dashoboka kumusaruro (nk’akarorero inyungu kumusaruro wi 20%);
- Gucerwa canke ingorane iyo ugerageza gukuramwo umutahe washizemwo;
- Uguhimiriza vya cane abanywanyi bashasha kugira ubuhinga bugume bukora;
- Ibura ry'umuco kuvyerekeye ukuri kunkomoko y'inyungu y'usuraruro wemerewe.


Amaherezo, ama piramide hamwe n'ibishushanyo vya Ponzi vyaremewe gutakaza. Intege nke vyubakiyeko nukokuntu haguma hakenerwa kuzana abanyamitahe bashasha kuriha inyungu kuri bashasha bagiyemwo. Ukwo gukenera birateba bikaba ibidashoboka ubiharuye uko igihe gihera kuko igitigiri c'abakenewe bashasha gikenera kuguma cigwiza uko umugambi ukura. Iyo hashikiriwe urugero rudashimishije, abajamwo baca batangura kugira amakenga, icizere kikagenda, hama piramide yose igasambuka. Kuri runo rugero, abantu binjiye ubwanyuma, kenshi bafise amakuru make, baratakaza imitahe yabo yose ataniyindi nzira yokuyigarukana, mugihe abiteguye canke abashizemwo imitahe yabo baba bavuye mumugambi.


Mw'isi y'ama cryptomonnaies, igicapo ca Ponzi gishobora gufata imforo nyinshi, akenshi ikaba igenewe guhisha kamere yayo yamagendu inyuma y’igipfukisho c'ivy'ubuhinga canke c'ivy'ubutunzi. Anomayeri ashobora kuboneka nka Token nshasha itangwa canke Initial Coin Offerings(ICOs), ibisanzwe ari ibikorwa vy'ugusaba amafaranga aho cryptomonnaie nshasha idandarizwa kumugaragaro. Inyuma y'imvugo z'ubuhinga nka "Blockchain", "Smart Contract", or "Staking" Imigambi imwimwe iranyegeje ibicapo vya piramide biyobeye. Abandi bavuga ko batanga inyungu z'umurengera mugushira hamwe imitungo y'ama crypto iteye amakenga hamwe n'ubuhinga bwukuriha busanzwe bwubakiye vyose ku kwinjira kw’abanyamitahe bashasha.


Vuba cane, igicapo ca Ponzi naco nyene carakwiragijwe m'isi y'ubutunzi itagenzurwa(DeFi). Mugihe DeFi yerekeza gutanga ubufasha muvy'ubutunzi, imigambi imwimwe irayikoresha kugira ngo itange imbono y’ikinyoma y’uko amayeri yayo ibereye. imbuga zimwe zimwe za DeFi zizeza inyungu ninshi, zishingiye ku vyemezo mugukabisha itanga ryama Cryptomonnaie munyubako munyubako atawuzikoresha. Kuno kuryosharyosha kenshi na kenshi bikwegera abantu akenshi bishigikirwa n’uburyo butaboneka kandi butashobora kugenzurwa, harimwo ibimenyetso vyaremewe canecane ubuhendanyi. Muvy'ukuri, buno buryo  ntandinganizo irama y'ubudandaji bufise—inyungu murimake irihwa nabashasha bayikoresha, nkakurya kw'igicapo cakera ca Ponzi. Igihe icizere gitanguye guhera canke uruja n'uruza rw'abantu bashasha rugabanutse, buno buhinga burasenyuka atagisivya, bukerekeza kw'iterambere riboneka kubanyamitahe.


Tubasabe mufate mumutwe ko inyandiko zakino cigwa zifise ihangiro ry'ukwigisha gusa ntibisiguyeko bifatwa nk'ipanuro. Umutekano w'ivyubutunzi bwawe bivana n’ubushobozi ufise bwo kuguma wiyubara, ugira amakenga kandi ufise amakuru meza ku ngingo yose ufata muvy’amahera.


Umutekano mwiza nikwama ubaza imisi yose kinokibazo : Mu vy’ukuri, nihehe inyungu batwemereye iva ? Nimba inyishu idatahuritse, nuhunge ubwo nyene.


#### Ipompa n'Ikinogo


Buno bwoko bw'amayeri bujanye n’ugutuma igiciro c’ikintu kiduga mu buryo bw’ubukorano—kenshi cryptomonnaie ya Token ifise agaciro gatoyi-biciye muruganda rwo kumenyekanisha riyobowe, kenshi rurongowe n'umurwi w'abanyamitahe. Igishushanyo c'Ipompa n'Ikinogo gikurikira iyi nzira:




- Umugwi w’abantu b'indani canke abantu bafise ababayoboka, begeranya batekanye amafaranga menshi y'umutungo bahisemwo.
- Hama bagatanguza imenyekanisha ryihuta kugira baronke igiciro kinini, bigatuma igiciro kija hejuru.
- Abantu bose murirusangi, bayobowe na FOMO (Ubwoba bwo Kubura), baca batangura kugura uwo mutungo ku bwinshi, ivyo bikaba bituma igiciro kigenda kiraduga.
- Ku rwego rwo hejuru rw’ivyo bimenyetso, abari indani baragurisha ivyo bafise vyose icarimwe.
- Igiciro kirasenyuka, bigatuma abinjiye ubwanyuma batakaza cane.




![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/004.webp)


Ni vyiza kumenya ko amayeri ya Pump & Dump atemewe n’amategeko mu bihugu vyinshi kandi afatwa nk’uburyo bwo gukoresha nabi isoko. Nubwo bimeze uko, mwene iyo migambi irabandanya gutera imbere cane cane mu kibanza c’amahera y’ivy’ubuhinga bwa none, aho amategeko atarashobora kuhaboneka.


Ube maso cane cane ku migwi y'abantu ku giti cabo "y'ibimenyetso" ku mbuga nka Telegram, Discord, canke izindi nzira z'ubuhinga ngurukana bumenyi. Ivyo akenshi birongorwa n’abantu bafise ababayoboka canke biyita abahinga, bamwe muri bo mbere bagasaba amahera yokugira ushobore kwinjiramwo. Naho iyo migwi ivuga ko itanga amahirwe yo gushiramwo amahera yihariye, ukuri ni ukugira uruhande rumwe: abayitegura ni bo bonyene baronka inyungu, mu gihe benshi mu bari muri iyo migwi baheza bagatakaza amafaranga yabo.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/005.webp)


Wanashima nivyo yuko bamwe mu bari muri iyo migwi bashobora kuronka inyungu mu gihe gitoyi muri ubwo bwoko bw’ugukoresha nabi isoko, ariko ukuroranirwa kwabo akenshi ntigushingiye ku kindi kirenze amahirwe n’igihe canyaco. Mu gihe kirekire, iyo migambi ntishobora kuramba. Bisaba ko umuntu ahora yifatanya n’ibintu bishobora gutera ingorane nyinshi kandi akaguma agira uruhara mu bikorwa vy’amayeri bishobora gusenyuka ata kabuza.


N’ikibi kuruta, barafungura mu kwihenda guteye akaga: ukwizera ko bishoboka ko umuntu aronka amahera yihuta kandi yoroshe ataco atahura ingene mu vy’ukuri uburyo bwo gutanga amahera bukora. Ivyo vyiyumviro ntibishira abantu ku giti cabo mu kaga gusa, ariko kandi birahungabanya icizigiro c’ibidukikije vyose vy’amahera y’ibanga .


Kubera izo mpamvu zose, ubuhinga bwiza ni ugukomera ku buryo bukomeye, bwiyumviriwe neza bwo gushiramwo amahera, uburyo bushingiye ku nyigisho z’ivy’amahera, ugutahura gukomeye kw’ivy’ishimikiro, n’ukubona ibintu mu gihe kirekire.

Mu kwubaka ubumenyi bwawe wihanganye, uzoba udashobora gushikirwa cane n’ugukoreshwa nabi mu mutima be n’amasezerano atari yo; kandi bafise ibikoresho vyiza cane vyo kwirinda ubwoko bw’imitego y’amahera ishobora gutuma umuntu atakaza vy’ukuri.


#### Impano, Loteri, n'Impano z'Ibinyoma


Ubwo bwoko bw’ubuhendanyi burasezerana Bitcoin ku buntu canke izindi ngororano muri Exchange kubera wewe ubanje kohereza amahera makeyi. Ni ngombwa kwibuka: nta muntu canke ishirahamwe ryemewe rizokwigera rigusaba ko worungika amafaranga y’ibanga imbere y’igihe n’isezerano ryo kugurungikira ibindi mu gusubiza.


Abahendanyi kenshi bigira nk’abantu bazwi cane(nka Elon Musk canke abandi bantu bazwi cane) kugira ngo bakwegere abahohotewe biciye ku mbuga ngurukanabumenyi. Imisi yose nusuzume kabiri ko amakonti n’imbuga ngurukanabumenyi vyemewe imbere y’uko ukorana na vyo, kandi ntukigere wizigira ivyo utanga bisa n’ibitanga cane canke vyiza cane ku buryo ata co bimaze.


Rimwe na rimwe, ivyo bihendahenda bigaragara nk'ibinyoma vy'"amahera y'imbere". Usezeranirwa igihembo canke impembo(amahera, igicuruzwa, canke igikorwa) ariko ubanza usabwa kwishura amahera, bivugwa ko azokwishura ibintu nk’ivyo gutwara, imisoro, canke amafaranga y’ugucuruza. Iyo umuntu amaze kwishura, uwo mubeshi arazimangana, kandi impembo yasezeranywe ntiyigera ishika.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/006.webp)


#### Shitcoins n'amafaranga y'ibanga biriko biratangwa


Imigambi y’amahera y’imbere mu gihugu rimwe na rimwe itanga ibimenyetso vy’ubuntu ("*airdrops*") kugira ngo bikwegere ababikoresha. Niwiyubare cane ku bijanye n’ubwo bwoko bw’ibintu vyo guteza imbere; akenshi baba bariko baratanga imitego aho gutanga amahirwe nyayo.


#### Ubusuma bw'akaranga n'ubuhendanyi


Abatera kenshi bakoresha imbuga z’ibinyoma, amakonti yo ku mbuga ngurukanabumenyi, canke imeyili zihenda kugira ngo bagerageze kwiba amafranga yawe. Ivyo bihendahenda bishobora kuza biciye ku nzira iyo ari yo yose yo guhanahana amakuru: imeyili, imbuga ngurukanabumenyi, guhamagara kuri telefone, canke mbere ubutumwa bwa kera...


Imbere yo gufyonda ku nzira canke gukora ikintu ico ari co cose, wama usuzuma kabiri akaranga k’uwarungitse. Igihe ushidikanya, genda kuri uru rubuga n’amaboko aho gukoresha uruja n’uruza rwatanzwe. Ikiruta vyose, ntukigere usangira n’umuntu uwo ari we wese imfunguruzo zawe canke amajambo y’ibanga yawe.


#### Bitcoin Amafork akomeye


Mu myaka iheze, Bitcoin yarabonye amafork akomeye (hard forks), ayo mahinduka yabonye ingaruka zo gutuma havuka ubundi buryo bwo gukoresha amafaranga ya nyayo. Mu majambo yoroshe, Hard Fork ni ugucapura mu rubuga bijana ku nzira zibiri zitandukanye, zose zisangiye amateka amwe gushika ku gihe co gucapura. Ivyo bimenyetso biboneka iyo igice c’umuryango c’abahindura porogarama canke abantu bafata imigambi ku bijanye na Bitcoin bashaka gushiramwo amahinduka akomeye mu masezerano y’intango ariko ntibishobora kuzana amahinduka ku masezerano yose. Aho guheba ivyiyumviro vyabo, bafata ingingo yo gutangura ubundi bwoko bushasha bwa Bitcoin (ifise amategeko yahinduwe), bizeye ko abakoresha n’abacukuzi bazohitamwo gukurikira Fork yabo aho gukurikira.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/007.webp)


Si *amaforok ya Hard* yose ari ay’ayibinyoma, kuko amwe akomoka ku kutumvikana mu vy’ubuhinga canke mu vy’ingengabitekerezo mu kibano. Ariko rero, abandi babitumwe n’ivy’ubudandaji canke mbere n’imvo z’ubuhemu. Ingero zizwi cane z’izo nkoko zikomeye ni **Amahera ya Bitcoin (BCH)** na **Iyerekwa rya Bitcoin Satoshi (BSV)**. Yatangujwe mu 2017 na 2018, hakurikijwe, ayo mafaranga atari yo akenshi avuga ko ari "amafaranga meza" y'umwimerere Bitcoin. Batera imbere inyungu zivugwa nk’amahera make yo gucuruza canke gucuruza vyihuta kubera ubunini bw’inkuta bwongerekana. Ariko rero, ayo mahinduka mu vy’ubuhinga azana n’uguhinduranya gukomeye mu bijanye n’umutekano, ukwegereza ubutegetsi abaturage, n’ugukomera; ibintu bishobora guhushanya ingingo ngenderwako z’ishimikiro za Bitcoin.


Uretse itandukaniro ry’ubuhinga, ayo mafaranga atari yo akenshi akoresha urujijo kugira ngo akwegere abanyamitahe badafise amakuru akwiye. Bashobora gukoresha ubuhinga bwo kwamamaza bugenewe kuzimiza n’ibigirankana abashasha bizera ko bariko bagura Bitcoin (BTC) y’ukuri.


Kugira wirinde kugwa muri uwo mutego, wama ugenzura amafaranga uriko uragura. Bitcoin y’umwimerere ikoresha ikimenyetso **BTC**, mu gihe Bitcoin Cash n’ibindi biva kuri yo bikoresha amajambo biyongereyemwo atandukanye, nka BCH canke BSV.


#### Abanyabinyoma n'abahinga b'ibinyoma


Uko amahera y'mahinguriro agenda aramamara cane, ubuhinga bwo guhanahana amakuru ku mbuga ngurukanabumeni bwarabonye uruja n'uruza rw'abagira ico bakoze, abahinga biyita, n'abitwa "*crypto gurus*". Naho bakeyi bashobora gutanga ubumenyi nyakuri bw’inyigisho, abandi benshi barakoresha akaryo ko kugaragara kwabo kugira ngo bateze imbere imigambi iteye amakenga(y'ibinyoma) canke intambara z’ubudandaji ziteye akaga (kandi rimwe na rimwe zikaba ari ububeshi).


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/008.webp)


Abo bantu bagira ico bakoze akenshi bizera ubuhinga bugaragara kugira ngo bakwegere abaje bashahsa gutangura muri ino si ya bitcoin: berekana ingaruka zitangaje z’ivy’ubutunzi (akenshi zikaba ari iz’ibinyoma canke zidashobora gusuzumwa canke kugendurwa), bashira ahabona ubuzima bw’abo nk'abantu bafise amafaranga nk’ikimenyetse c’uko bamaze kuronka, kandi bagatera imbere intambara zo gushiramwo amahera “z’igitangaza”. Intumbero ni ugutuma FOMO — ubwoba bwo guhomba amahirwe — no gusunika ababumviriza mu ngingo zidasanzwe no mu gushiramwo amafaranga atabanje kwiyumvira.


Ni vyiza gutahura ko impanuro nyinshi "z'ubuntu" zivuye kuri abo bantu zitazokwigera zigira ukwizigirwa vy'ukuri. Inyuma y’ubugwaneza akenshi hariho ubuhinga buharuriwe bwo kuyobora abantu ku kugura ibintu biteye amakenga. Kandi naho abagira ico bakoze bamwebamwe boba ari abavugakuri, gusubiramwo ivyo bavuyemwo vyoba hafi bidashoboka; ukuroranirwa kwabo akenshi kuvana n’igihe kinaka, ubumenyi bwo mu mutima canke ibintu bidasanzwe udashobora gusa kuronka.


Hari abaguhamagarira kugira ngo winjire mu mirwi yigenga, yishurwa aho bavuga ko basangira ibimenyetso vy’ubudandaji vyihariye canke impanuro z’imbere mu gihugu zibemerera inyungu zihuta kandi ziyoroshe. Mu vy’ukuri, iyo migwi ahanini irahezagira abayitegura ubwabo. Akenshi bakoresha abanywanyi babo nk’isoko y’amahera, ahanini bagakura ivy’ubutunzi kugira ngo bironke inyungu zabo bwite. Abanyamuryango kenshi baheza bagatakaza amahera, kuko badashobora gukora vyihuse nk’abanyamuryango b’imbere mu gihugu bategura ubudandaji (raba igice kivuga ku migambi ya Pump & Dump).


Kubera ivyo bintu vy’ukuri, ng’ibi inyifato nziza twogira kugira ngo ntitugwe mu mitego y’abantu b’abababeshi:



- Ube maso cane ku vyiyumviro vyose vyo kudandaza muri crypto.**

Umuntu wo kwizigirwa kandi afise ubumenyi ntazokwigera akuhatira kugura amafaranga y’ivy’ubuhinga bwa none atagutera intege ngo ubanza kuvyiga muburyo bwimbitse kuruhande rwawe bwite.


- Amashure y'ubudandaji canke y'igushora imitahe yishurwa ntabwo yama ari ikimenyetso c'uburyo.**

Benshi muri ivyo vyirwa bararemesha intambara zishobora gutera akaga canke ziyoroshe cane; kenshi ayo makuru nyene ushobora kuyaronka kuri Internet ku buntu.


- Nta kintu nk'ico co gukopa ubucuruzi bw'umuntu maze ukaronka ibisubizo bisa.**

Intambara zose zo gushiramwo amahera zivana n’ivyo umuntu abayemwo, igihe afise, ubumenyi afise, n’ibindi bintu bidashobora kwisubirwamwo neza na neza.


- Cane cane urabe maso ku mpanuro zisa n’aho ari nziza cane mugutahura kwawe.**

Amasezerano atari yo canke inyungu zishimikijwe hafi y’igihe cose ni ibimenyetso vy’ugukoreshwa nabi. Ibuka: kuba umuntu avuze ikintu yizigiye ntibituma kiba ukuri.


Imisi yose wibuke, umuntu wese uhura na we muri Bitcoin canke muri crypto ecosystem yagutse afise umurongo w’ibikorwa vyiwe bwite agenderako; yaba ivuzwe ataco ihinduye canke ihishijwe hagati y’imirongo. Nta kintu nk’ico c’amakuru atagira aho ashimikiye gusa. Mbere n'iki cirwa ca, BTC102, gifise intumbero itomoye, *gutuma abantu barushiriza gutahura Bitcoin.*

Ni co gituma ari ngombwa ko uguma umenya imvo nyakuri zituma ukoresha ikintu cose ukoresha. Kandi ntimwigere mwibagirwa iri tegeko ry’inzahabu rya internet:

*Nimba igicuruzwa canke igikorwa kimeze nkaho ar'ica gusa, hari amahirwe menshi ko ari wewe igicuruzwa.*


Ariko ukamenya ko ingingo nziza yo kwikingira ni indero yawe bwite, ukugira amakenga gufise ikimazi, kandi ikiruta vyose, ukagira akamenyero ko gusuzuma ibikorwa vyawe kenshi.


### Uko wokwirinda ubusuma


**Kugira ngo ugire umutekano kuri internet, cane cane mw'isi ya crypto, uzirikane izi ngingo ngenderwako zingirakamaro:**



- Ntuze wigere wizigira atacemeza ubona: "*Ntukizere, suzuma*" ;
- Gukekeranya ku bijanye n’inyungu zemewe canke ziri hejuru cane;
- Ntukigere usangira n’umuntu numwe imfunguruzo zawe z’ibanga;
- Nturungike Bitcoin (canke crypto iyo ari yo yose) ku ma aderesi atazwi;
- Nimba ukekeranya, nturungike. Rindira wiyumvire ufate umwanya imbere y’uko ugira ico ukoze. FOMO ni umwansi wawe mubi cane;
- Irinde imigwi y’ubudandaji canke imiryango y’ubudandaji yishurwa itanga amasezerano atari yo;
- Ivyinshi mu bihembo canke amatora "y'ubuntu" yo kuri internet ni ubuhendanyi canke ni ubuhendanyi bukomeye;
- Uzokwama wungka vyinshi mu gihe kirekire mu kwiga kuruta mu gukina.


Kwikingira ubusuma ni intambwe ya mbere ihambaye cane mu kurinda itunga ryawe; ariko ntibikwiye. Kubungabunga isuku rikomeye ry’ubuhinga bwa none ni ikintu gihambaye nk’uko nyene. Ni co gituma, mu kigabane gikurikira, tuzoca irya n’ino ingene wokomeza umutekano wawe wo kuri internet n’ingene wokora neza kugira ngo wikingire mw’isi y’ubuhinga bwa none.

### Igituma umutekano wo kuri Interineti ari kirumara


Bitcoin iguha ubushobozi bwo gufata amafaranga yawe yose muminwe(ata mabanki), ata bahuza. Ariko ubwo bwoko bw’ubwigenge bw’ivy’ubutunzi buzana n’amabanga akomeye: iyo BTC yawe yibwe kubera ukubura umutekano, nta buryo nabumwe buhari bwo gusubira inyuma ngo uyigarukane. Urumva bitandukanye n'aya ma banki tumenyereye, nta nakimwe kigufasha kuyagarura, ntanaho woshobora gushinga urubanza iyo wibwe nta n’ubwishingizi bwo kugukiza.


None kandi, inyungu z’ukuri z’Ibinii Bitcoin ziratuma uba icari c’abantu binkozi zikibi: umu hacker yokwiba BTC zawe ashobora kuzimurira ako kanya ku konti yiwe ya Bitcoin, ata buryo na bumwe bwo kuzigarura. Muri ivyo bihe, isuku nziza yo kuri interinete si ikintu co kwitega gusa: ni umurongo wa mbere wo kurinda ko ivyakozwe hamwe n’amafaranga yawe bitava mu maboko yawe.


### Inyabwonko isukuye kandi iri ku gihe


Kimwe mu bintu vyirengagizwa cane mu bijanye n’umutekano wawe wo kuri internet ni ugushira kugihe iminsi yose ubuhinga ukoresha. Ivyiyumviro vyinshi bishasha birakuramwo utunenge tumenyewe nkudushobora gukoreshwa ninkozi zikibi. Naho bamwe bamwe birinda gushira munyabwonko zabo ibintu bishasha mugutinya ko inyabwonko zabo bica zikora bukebuke, ingorane nyayo iri mu gukoresha porogarama za kera canke ubuhinga bwa kera abasuma baza baramaze kumenyera no kumenya utunenge twazo baba bazi ahari inzira yoroshe yo kukwiba.


Ni vyiza cane kandi gukoresha antivirus yizewe. Kuri Windows, Windows Defender yubatswemwo muri rusangi irikwije ku bakoresha benshi. Niba ushaka ibindi bikoresho canke amahoro yo mu mutima, amahitamwo antivirus idandazwa nka Kaspersky kandi araboneka. Ku macOS, iterabwoba rya malware ni ryinshi cane mu mateka **ariko riguma ryoshoboka** rero nivyiza cane kwama wiyubara ukikingira.

### Umuti w'inzozi mbi z'umwidondoro


Imve mu mvo nyinshi zituma abantu binjirirwa ni ugukoresha amajambo kabanga(password) agoyagoya. igitigiri kinini c'abanywanyi baracakoresha ijambo kabanga rimwe kuma conti menshi, canke bagahitamwo gukoresha ibintu(indome ibiharuro canke utumenyetso) vyoroshe kwiyumvira canke gutora. ## Impanuro ku bashasha


<chapterId>33134b3f-92c1-5185-afb6-88599e47e801</chapterId>

Kwinjira muri Bitcoin birashobora kuba ibiteye umunezero; ariko bizana impanuka nyinshi. Isi ya crypto ntabwo isa n’izindi zose; ibiciro vy’ibintu bihindagurika burikanya, n'ubuhinga butamenyerewe, n’ubushobozi nyabwo bwo gutakaza amahera yawe kubera amakosa yoroshe canke amahitamwo mabi y’ugushiramwo imitahe, vyose ni imitego abinjira bashasha bategerezwa kumenya kandi bakirinda.


Muri iki gice, nzobabwira impanuro ngirakamaro n’ubuyobozi rusangi ku muntu wese ariko aratera intambwe za mbere, cane cane abariko baragura ubwa mbere canke bariko baratohoza isi yagutse y’ugushiramwo ry’amahera.


Ehe ingingo nyamukuru tuzokwihweza hamwe:



- Iyubare **Shitcoins** n’izindi crypto zitagira akamaro;
- Ntimushiremwo **amahera ushobora gutakaza**;
- Menya itandukaniro riri hagati ya **ugucuruza** n'**ugushiramwo amahera**;
- Menya **ingaruka z’imisoro** k'umafaranga ushiramwo;
- Rinda neza **ijambo ryawe ryo kugarukana ijambobanga**;
- Gumana wicishije bugufi kandi wihoreye **low profile**(ukwihorera canke ukwinyegeza ni kimwe mu bigize umutekano);
- Wiyumvire kugihe kirekire**, zoom out kandi wihangane(wiyumvire nk’urugendo rwo kwiruka, atari urugendo rw’amaguru).

### Amakosa asanzwe yo kwirinda


Bitcoin yugururiwe umuntu wese, ariko ivyo ntibisigura ko ukwiye kuvyitamwo utavyiteguye. Akira amwe mu makosa ya kera akunda gukorwa n’abashasha:


**Amakosa y'ubuhinga:**



- **Gutakaza ijambo ryawe ryo kugarukana ijambobanga(Losing your seed phrase):** Ijambo ryawe ryo kugarukana (kenshi ni amajambo 12 canke 24) ni bwo buryo bwonyene bwo gushika ku Bitcoin yawe iyo hari ikintu kibaye ku ngodo(Wallet) yawe. Iyo uyitakaje, amahera yawe arahera ntusubira kuyabona ubundi;
  
-**Kubika ama bitcoins yawe ku rubuga rwa gatatu:** Nimba amafaranga yawe ari ku rubuga rwo rushobora kugenzurwa, ntabwo ari wewe ufise vy’ukuri. Ushobora gushikirwa n'ingorane nk'ubusuma, urubuga rwo kunanirwa, canke mbere no gufata amafaranga;
  
- **kwanjanjwa k'ubuzima bwawe bw'ibanga:** Gukingira ubuzima bwawe bw'ibanga ni igice nyamukuru co gukingira itunga ryawe. Guhishura ku mugaragaro ingene Bitcoin ufise vyoshobora gutuma utakaza vyose;
  
-**Umutekano udahagije wo kuri interinete:** Kudakingira ibikoresho vyawe n’uburinzi bw’ishimikiro (nk’ugshira kugihe, amajambo banga akomeye, canke 2FA) bituma uba ikimenyetso coroshe ku batera; kandi vyoshobora kugutwara vyose.

- **Gugwa mu mutego mugutinya gutakaza ayo mahigwe**: kugura utabanje kwiyumvira kubera ubwoba bwo gutakaza amahirwe akenshi bituma umuntu agira amahitamwo mabi kubera ingingo mbi yafashe. Ukwihangana ukarindira niyo ngingo nziz kurusha izindi zose.

### Gusigura ubuhinga bwo gushora amahera


Imbere y'uko wiyumvira kugura satoshi yawe yambere, birakenewe cane ko utahura igituma uriko urashiramwo amahera muri Bitcoin n’uburyo ubikoramwo. Ivyo bisigura kugira umugambi utomoye w’ivy’amahera yawe uhuye n’ingene umeze be n’imigambi yawe y’igihe kirekire.


Tangurana no kumenya **umutahe(amafaranga)** ufise atagukekeranya. Ntufate igiharuro cose kikuje mumutwe muruwo mwanya. Fata umwanya ubanze umenye amafaranga winjiza ku kwezi, ukuremwo amahera ukoresha (nkay'inzu, ingurane, amakori, amafaranga y'ama fagiture(amazi, amatara n'ibindi)), hamwe n’amahera usohora ku munsi ku munsi (ayo gufungura, ayo kwiyunguruza, ayo kwinezereza, n’ibindi). gukora ivyo bituma ushobora kugira umutekano ukwiye muvy'ubutunzi bwawe cane cane mugihe amasoko agabanutse, umugambi canke ubuhinga bwiyumviriwe neza nibwo buryo bwiza kandi buramba bwo kwirinda canke kurwana no kwihangana igihe kirekire.




![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/020.webp)


Igihe uhejeje kumenya amafaranga canke umutahe uzokoresha, wiyumvire ingene ushaka gushiramwo wa mutahe wawe. Uburyo bumwe buboneka, bufasha bashasha kandi banahamagarirwa cane ni Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA), ugura umubare udahinduka w’ama Bitcoin mu bihe bitandukanye (ku ndwi, ku kwezi, n’ibindi). Iyi nzira ifasha gutuma igiciro cawe co kwinjira kigenda neza mu gihe c’igihe kandi igabanura ingaruka z’inyiyumvo z’uguhinduka kw’igiciro. Ni uburyo bwiza ku bantu benshi cane cane abashasha.


Hanyuma, wibaze uti: Igihe canje ni ikihe?

Woba uriko urarondera kwinjira no gusohoka(kigenda no kugaruka) vyihuse mw’isoko (ugucuruza)? Canke woba uha agaciro cane imbono yo ku gihe kirekire, Mu kubika Bitcoin imyaka myinshi(hodler)? Nimba uri umunyaruyeri, bisigura ko udatewe ikibazo n'ihindagurika ry'ibiciro rya burimunsi, ahubwo uhagaze cane kukurinda umutekano wawe hamwe no kurinda ubutunzi bwawe. Nimba uriko uracuruza, uzorushiriza gushikirwa n’urusaku canke uguhindagurika kw’isoko ry’igihe gito, ingorane nyinshi, n’uguta umutwe akenshi bizanana n'ugufata ingingo vyihuse kandi utiyumviye. Nta n’inyishu nimw ihari yoshimisha bose, ariko kumenya uburyo cane uburyo ukoreramwo bizotuma umenya gufata ingingo.


Ahubwo, ntuze wigere ufata ingingo ushingiye kumarangamutima canke k'ubwoba. Iyumvire ubuhinga bwawe bwite imbere y’igihe, uvyandike(ubwo buhinga( strategy)) kandi ubwubahirize ntukore nakimwe hanze y'ivyo wateguye.


Niba ugikekeranya, **tangura wiga.**

Fata amasaha makeya uyahebere Bitcoin, urabe ivyirwa vyo ku buntu biri kuri Plan ₿ Network, usome ibitabu bimwebimwe, shiramwo amayero atanu gusa kugira ngo wimenyereze ubwo buhinga, kandi urabe ibintu vyiza kuri Interinete. Guma ufise ishaka ryo kumenya. Uko uzorushiriza kwiyumva neza, ni ko bizokworohera gusubira gusuzuma uburyo canke ubuhinga bwawe ukoreramwo zawe, guhindura uburyo ukoresha, no gutera imbere ushikamye.

Bitcoin izwi cane kubera uguhinduka kw’ibiciro bikomeye. Kwimuka kw’ibice 10%, 20%, canke mbere 50% mu misi mikeyi gusa si ikintu kidasanzwe. Ku bashasha, ubwo bwoko bw’uguhinduka burashobora kubatera ubwoba. Biroroshe gutwarwa n’ivyo mugihe co kuduga kwa bitcoin canke gutera umutima uhagaze(uruhagarara, guta umutwe) mu gihe co gukoroka ca bitcoin ; ivyo vyose akenshi bituma umuntu afata ingingo mbi, nk’uguhitamwo kudandariza mu ruhombo.

### Itandukaniro riri hagati y'ugucuruza, gushiramwo amahera, gutunga canke gufita


Bitcoin akenshi ikikujwe n’ivyiyumviro bitari vyo vy’abantu benshi; kimwe mu bikunze gukoreshwa cane ni iciyumviro c’uko ari inzira yihuta yo gutunga biciye mu bucuruzi. Ariko ni ngombwa gutahura itandukaniro rigaragara hagati y’ugucuruza, gushiramwo amahera, n’ugutunga canke gufita muri Bitcoin, kuko uburyo bumwe bumwe buzana n’umuzirikanyi wabwo, ubuhinga bwabwo, n’urugero rw’ingorane.



- Ubucuruzi :**


Reka tuvuge ukuri:**kumbure ntukwiye kwisuka muvyo kudandaza.**

Kuja muvyo kudandaza(trading) bizana kuvuga amajambo menshi mukiringo c'igihe gito, biba birondera gufatira canke bikoresheje uguhindagurika kw'igiciro ca bitcoin. Naho ivyo bisa nkaho ari ibishobora kuguhenda, Kuroranirwa muvyo kudandaza(trading) bisaba ubumenyi bukarishe(nko kumenya gusoma canke kwihweza nea ibiharuro vy'isoko, kumenya kwirinda impanuka), kumenya kwifata wewe nyene kandi ukama amaso yawe ahanganye n'isoko iminsi yose. ni igikorwa gikoresha kandi kiruhisha umutwe cane, kandi ikintu kiriho kigoye no kwemra nuko abashasha benshi baratakaza amahera cane kuko benshi baragaya ivyo bisabwa babifata nk'ibisanzwe. 

Nk’uko Warren Buffett yabivuze neza:

"**Nimba udakomereye kubika imigabane ku gikorwa imyaka cumi, ntukiyumvire no kuyibika iminota cumi**."

Bitcoin si umugambi canke uburyo bwo gutunga vuba.



- **Gushiramwo imitahe:**


Abanamitahe bafata ivyiyumviro vy’igihe giciye hagati n’igihe kirekire, bagura ama BTC bizeye ko igiciro cayo kizoduga uko igihe kigenda kirahera(amezi, imyaka, canke mbere imyaka mirongo). ingaruka zo zama zihari, birashoboka, kuko igiciro ca Bitcoin gishobora guhinduka cane. Ariko iyo nzira muri rusangi iratuza kandi irakora cane ku bantu benshi, cane cane abadashaka kumara amasaha menshi bihweza ibiharuro kenshi.



- Gufita(gutunga) (HODL) :**


"HODL" yatanguye nk'ikosa ryo kwandika "hold" maze yihuta kuba igice c'umuco wa Bitcoin. Uyu musi, ni ikimenyetso c'ubudasa.

Hodlers bakoresha imitahe yabo nko kugura bakarindira igihe kirekiere cane rimwe na rimwe kugeza no kumyaka cumi canke iranga. Babika Bitcoin yabo atakibazo kandi bagategerezwa gusa, bavyurwa n’ukwemera gukomeye ko Bitcoin ishobora gukora igihe kirekire. Ntibababazwa n’uguhinduka kw’ibiciro ku musi ku musi canke amasoko. ### Inkomoko: Umugambi wavutse bivuye ku bushakashatsi bw'iminsi myinshi


#### Cypherpunks n'ukurema uburyo udashobora kugendurwa n'ama banki


Bitcoin ntiyavutse ukobukeye uko bwije. Ivyo ni ingaruka y’ubushakashatsi n’igerageza ry’imyaka mirongo mu vy’ubuhinga bwo gukingira amakuru, ubuhinga bw'inyabwonko be n’ivy’amahera. Imbere y’uko itanguzwa mu 2009, imigambi myinshi(nka eCash, b-money, Bit Gold, na RPOW) yaragerageje gukora amahera y'ubuhinga bwa none. Bose bari bahuriye ku kibazo nyamukuru: ingene bokwirinda gukoresha amahera incuro zibiri mu buryo budacungewe abantu ata kwizigira abategetsi bakuru.


Ico kibazo nticatorewe umuti kugeza mu mpera z’umwaka wa 2008, igihe umuntu akoresha izina Satoshi Nakamoto yasohora igitabu cera Bitcoin. Haciye amezi makeyi, verisiyo ya mbere y’ubuhinga bwa none bwa Bitcoin bwaratanguye gukoreshwa, buca butangura ubuhinga bwo gukora ata mabanki canke intwaro zikora.


Uwo mugambi wararisunze cane inyigisho runtu Cypherpunk; umuryango w’abahinguzi n’abanyabwenge bemera gukoresha ubuhinga bwo gukingira umwidegemvyo w’umuntu ku giti ciwe kuri Interinete. Kuri bo, ubuzima bwite n’ugusenyura ubutegetsi ntivyari ivy’ubuhinga, ahubwo vyari ivy’ngirakamaro vy’ingengabitekerezo. Bitcoin yaserutse nk’inzira y’ivyo vyiyumviro yaroraniwe kuruta izindi zose: uruja n’uruza rw’amahera rw’urunganwe umuntu wese ashobora gukoresha, ata n’umwe ashobora kurugenzura, kandi umuntu wese ashobora kurugenzura.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/035.webp)


Aba Cypherpunks ni umuryango w’abantu udasanzwe, mpuzamakungu, baharanira gukoresha ubuhinga bwo gukingira kugira bahagararire ubwigenge bw'umuntu wese kuri interinete. Bemera cane uburenganzira umuntu afise kubuzima bwiwe bw'ibanga; cane cane mwiyi si iriko irangwa n'ugucungera cane gukorwa n'ama reta canke ugukoresha nabi amakuru y'abantu bikorwa n'ama shirahamwe.


Imizi y’umuhari Cypherpunk isubira mu ntango z’imyaka ya 1990, igihe imigwi y’abahinga mu vy’amabanga  no guhisha amakuru, abahinga mu bijanye n’amaporogarama, n’abaharanira umwidegemvyo batangura gutohoza ingaruka za politike z’amabanga y’amabanga mu gihe c’amakoraniro yo muri Silicon Valley. Umwe mu majwi azwi cane muri uwo murwi yari Tim May, yanditse igitabu citwa Manifesto y’abanyagitugu b’abanyagihugu mu 1988; igisomwa c’ishimikiro kigaragaza iyerekwa ry’isi aho ugushiramwo amakuru y’ibanga vyoha ubushobozi abantu ubwabo gukora birenze ubushobozi bwa leta n’ubugenzuzi bushingiye ku rwego rwo hejuru.

Igihambaye cane muri uwo muhari caje mu 1992 aho hashingwa urutonde rw’abarungika ubutumwa rwa Cypherpunks, ihuriro aho ivyiyumviro, imigambi, n’ibiganiro vya politike ku bijanye n’ubuzima bw'ibanga n’ubuhinga bwo gukingira amakuru y’ibanga vyari gushobora gutera imbere. Hanyuma mu 1993, Eric Hughes yarasohoye igitabu citwa Manifesto ca Cypherpunk, kikaba cari itangazo rigufi ariko rikomeye ryerekana neza ubutumwa n’ivyo abo bantu bemera kandi baharanira.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/032.webp)


Iciyumviro c’amafaranga y’ubuhinga bwa none akora yigenga ku butegetsi bw'abashaka kugendura (nka Bitcoin)cashinze imizi cane mu myumvire ya Cypherpunk.

Isoko ry’amazu ryo muri Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika ryasenyutse be n’ingorane y’ingurane z’agaciro gatoyi vyatumye amabanki akomeye ananirwa kandi bituma abantu bacika intege ku bijanye n’ivy’ubutunzi vyose.


Muri ivyo bihe vy’ubwoba n’ugukekeranya ni ho Bitcoin yavukiye. Uwo yayiremye, azwi kw’izina rya Satoshi Nakamoto, yashize ubutumwa bw’ikigereranyo cane mu gice ca mbere nyene c’ico gice ca Bitcoin Blockchain, kizwi kw’izina rya Genesis. Ubutumwa bwari:

### Urunani rutagira ubuyobozi rwo gutanga agaciro

Ivyo bisigura ko umuntu wese ashobora kwinjira muri urwo rubuga akoresheje pororgarama ibigenewe(node ​​ya Bitcoin) maze agatangura guhanahana amakuru canke amahera n'abandi banywayi, ata kwizigira server(urusenge) nyamukuru. Intumbero y’ukwo kwegereza ubutegetsi abanyagihugu ni ukubuza ikigo na kimwe (nk’ibankire, Leta canke ishirahamwe rinini) kugenzura, gucengera canke guhagarika iyo nzira. Iyo habaye igikorwa gishasha co guhanahana amahera, kiramenyeshwa ku rubuga. Abacukuzi(Miners) baca bemeza ivyo bikorwa mu kubishira mu bice bica vyongerwa ku mpera y'urukuta (ni co gituma izina rya "Blockchain").

#### Blockchain: igitabu c'uharura ivy'ubutunzi


Iyumvire Blockchain nk’igitabu kinini cane c’uguharura ubutunzi, aho umurongo wose ugereranya igikorwa. Mu buryo bwa kera ama banki akoreramwo, urutonde rw’amakuru rubikwa ku nyabwonko zabo ashobora guhinduka akanya kose babikeneye. Ku rundi ruhande, muri Bitcoin, **amahinduka yose aremezwa ku rubuga rwose**: iyo igice gishasha c'ibikorwa congerewe kuri Blockchain, biragoye cane kugihindura mu nyuma ntikinahinduka. Ukwo kwemeza gushizwe ahantu hamwe gutuma ububiko bwa Bitcoin butekanye kandi bugaragara.

### Uruhara rw'abacukuzi(Miners) n'agahembo kabo(Proof-of-Work)


#### Uko barema uruhome: gucukura(Mining)


Gucukura ni uburyo inyabwonko (canke amatongo manini ya Mining) atanga **ubushobozi bwo guharura** kugira ngo akingire ivyamaze gukorwa vya Bitcoin no guhingura amabuye mashasha. Abacukuzi b’amabuye y’agaciro barahiganwa kugira ngo batorere umuti agapfindo k'ibiharuro ukagitorerera inyishu, bararondera igice ca Hash yagonze. Ivyo bisaba inguvu n’ubutunzi vyinshi. Iyo umucukuzi amaze kuronka umuti ubereye, baratangaza iyo block ku rubuga, na rwo rukagenzura kandi rugakemera ko rubereye.

Nk’impera, Miner ironka ama bitcoins mashasha yaremwe (yitwa block subsidy) hamwe n’amahera y’ugucuruza ava ku bikorwa vyose biri muri iyo block.


#### Halving: Kugabanya agahembo ku ruhome 


Kugira ngo Bitcoin yame ikenye, agahembo kuri buri ruhome bitunganijwe mu buryo bw'uko kicamwo kabiri impome 210.000 zose; nk’inyuma y’imyaka ine. Ico kintu kizwi nka "Halving." Igihe Bitcoin yatangura, abacukuzi b'ama BTC baronka 50 BTC ku ruhome rumwe. Mu mwaka w’2025, iyo ngororano yaragabanutse igera kuri 3.125 BTC kandi izobandanya kugabanuka uko igihe kigenda kirarenga.

Amaherezo, nko mu mwaka w’2140, iyo nkunga izoshika kuri zero, kuko Bitcoin yose hamwe izoba itanzwe, izoshika ku biceri miliyoni 21. Iyi nzira y’ugusohoka ishobora gutegekanirwa yigana ubukene bw’ibicuruzwa vy’umubiri nk’inzahabu; nivyo bituma abebshi bayita **inzahabu ya digitale**.

### Bitcoin Ivy'amahera


#### Ukubura na politike y'amahera ishitse


Kimwe mu vyiza ngenderwako vya Bitcoin ni *politike y'amahera ishobora idetegekanwa kandi idahinduka*. bitandukanye n'amahera tumenyereye (nk’idolari, euro, canke yen), amabanki ashobora gukora canke gucapura uko ashaka(akenshi bituma amafaranga atakaza agaciro canke haba ihungabana ry'ubutunzi)Bitcoin ikora hakurikijwe amategeko ari hamwe kandi agaragara kuri bose yanditswe mu mategeko ayigenga.

Ntihazokwigera haba igitigiri c'ama bitcoins kirenga imiliyoni 21, kandi urugero amafaranga mashasha asohorwako ruramenyekana imbere y’igihe n’umuntu wese ari muri iyo nzira.


Nta leta, ikigo canke umuntu ashobora guhindura ku ruhande rumwe rushobora guhindura ivyo vyashinzwe canke amategeko agenga ivy’ugutanga. Uburyo bwonyene bwo guhindura ivyashinze muri bitcoins nuguhindura amategeko yose kuva mu mizi; kandi mbere ivyo bisaba kwemezwa n'igitigiri kinini c'abari muri urwo runani.


Uko gukene bushizwemwo ni ikintu ciza cane gikwegera abashaka guhitamwo kuva mu ngingo ngenderwako z’amahera zihindagurika canke kwirinda gusenyuka buhoro buhoro kw’ubushobozi bwabo bwo kugura biciye mu gutera imbere kw’ibiciro. haciye igihe, ivyo vyoshobora guserukira uguhinduka mu vyiyumviro vy’ivy’ubutunzi, aho kuzigama mu mutungo ushobora gutera imbere nk’uwa Bitcoin bica bikwegera abantu kuruta kwizigira amafaranga asanzwe, ashobora gutera guta agaciro.

Kimwe bintu ngirakamaro kandi abantu benshi birengagiza canke bagaya ni ukuntu bitcoin igabaguye. Bitcoin imwe yose irashobora kugaburwamwo ibice imiliyoni 100, ivyo bice bikaba vyitwa izina satoshis (canke Sats muri make). Ivyo bikaba bisigurako atari ngombwa ko ukoresha ibhumbi vyinshi vy'amayero canke amadolari kugira ngo utangure; urashobora kugura bitcoin nkeya kumayero makeya cane, Gushika kuduce dutoduto cane.

### Kugururira bose hamwe n'umuco


#### Itegeko rya bose, rigenzurwa na bose


Bitcoin ikoresha uburyo bwa bose, **uburyo bufunguye(isoko ipfunguye)** (cane cane biciye ku [Bitcoin Core]). Ivyo bisigura ko kode yayo iboneka ku buntu kugira ngo umuntu wese ayisuzume, ayigenzure, kandi ayisubiremwo canke ayongeremwo ibindi. Nta buryo bwihishije canke uburyo bupfutse; vyose ku bijanye n’ingene Bitcoin ikora biri hanze.

Nta buhinga bunyegeje canke ubuhinga bwugaranye; uko bitcoin ikora buri muntu wese arashobora kubibona. Biragoye kwiba canke kongeramwo no guhindura mwibanga ntibiboneke. Umuntu wese afise ubuhinga bwo gukora node arashobora gukoresha amanode, guterera agacumu k'ubumwe mu guteza imbere, canke kwubaka ibikoresho bihuye. Muri Bitcoin, ukwizigira kuronswa biciye mu mategeko no mu kwumvikana, atari biciye mu kugenzura hamwe.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/030.webp)


Uwo muco ni kimwe mu bintu nyamukuru bituma abantu bizigira amasezerano ya Bitcoin; birabuza abahinga mu vy'ingurukana bumenyi benshi canke bakeya gukoresha urubuga bihigira inyungu zabo. Bitcoin ikoresha ingingo ngenderwako yoroshe ariko ikomeye: iyo utemeranya n’amahinduka ategekanijwe, urafise umwidegemvyo wo kudahindura porogarama yawe. Mu bihe bimwebimwe, ivyo ntibizotuma habaho uguhungabana; uzoguma ukorana n’ibindi bice vy’urubuga. Ariko mu bindi bihe, ivyo bishobora gutuma haba ico bita Hard Fork, aho uruja n’uruza ruca mu bice bibiri, hanyuma hakabaho verisiyo nshasha ya Bitcoin. Ivyo nyene ni vyo vyabaye mu mwaka w’2017 n’ugucapura hagati y’amahera y’amafaranga ya (BTC) n’amafaranga y’amafaranga ya (BCH).


Naho ubwo bwoko bw'ubutegetsi bushobora kuba bwikwega kandi butera akajagari, ni ngirakamaro; biratuma ata kigo na kimwe gishobora kwiha ububasha bwo kugenzura ico gisata, kigafasha Bitcoin kuguma ihagaze neza kandi mumutekano, itagira aho yegamiye, kandi ishobora guhangana n'abashaka kuyigenzura.


#### Kwemeza vy'umuntu ku giti ciwe: amanode


Bitcoin iremesha umuntu wese kugenzura ukuri kwa Blockchain mu gukoresha "node" kuri nyabwonko yiwe canke kuri server yiwe. Ivyo bisigura gukuraho porogarama ya Bitcoin Core (canke iyindi verisiyo y’umurongo wa Bitcoin) no kugenzura amafaranga yose n’inkuta kuva mu 2009. Iyo node yawe imaze gushirwaho no gukorana, iba kopi yuzuye ya Blockchain kandi igafasha gushigikira urubuga.


Naho ubu buryo ari ubinga cane, buraha abakoresha batanezerewe canke basaba vyinshi uburyo bwo kuronka amahitamwo yandi. Gukoresha node bituma abakoresha bashobora kugira uruhara mu nzira y’uguhurizako, bikaba bifasha ata guca ku ruhande umutekano n’ugusabikanya rubuga ntihagire uwurwihagira ngo arugenzure.

Uretse ugushora imitahe, Bitcoin itanga uburyo bwo kurinda ubwigenge bw'ubutunzi. Imigambi iramaze gutangura mu bihugu nka Suède, EU, Uburusiya, n’Ubushinwa.


Abanyabwenge b’ubuhinga bwa none na bo nyene baraje muri iryo higanwa ryo kwiruka. Facebook (ubu yitwa Meta) yatanguje umugambi wayo wo gukora amafaranga y’ubuhinga bwa none, Libra, afise intumbero yo guhingura amafaranga y’ubuhinga bwa none ashigikiwe n’igiseke c’amahera y’ubuhinga bwa none. Bitcoin, kubera kamere yayo, ikora hanze y’imirongo y’imigenzo ya kera. Nta ubutegetsi na bumwe buyigenzura kandi ntishobora kugenzurwa canke guhindurwa n’ikigo na kimwe. ### Aho amabanki ahagaze muri bitcoin


Nk’amabuye y’imfuruka y’ivyubutunzi vya kera, amabanki abona ko Bitcoin ari igikoresho giteye ubwoba uburyio bwabo bakoreramwo, kikaba gishingiye ku guhuza no kugenzura uruja n’uruza rw’ivy’ubutunzi. Ivyo bisigura igituma amabanki menshi kw’isi yose yashizeho amategeko ku bucuruzi no ku bantu ku giti cabo bakoresha Bitcoin. Bamwe bashika no ku kugara amakonti canke guhagarika uburenganzira bwo kuronka ibikorwa ku masosiyete akora mu rwego rw’amahera y’amahinguriro(cryptocurrencies), akenshi bavuga ivy’ukurwanya ubusuma bw’amahera (AML) n’ugutanga amahera yo kurwanya iterabwoba (CTF).


Ariko naho Bitcoin ibonwa nk’iyihiganwa, benshi muri ayo mabanki nyene bariko barashiramwo amahera menshi mu bushakashatsi no mu gutegura Blockchain, bagerageza gukoresha ubuhinga bushasha bwa Bitcoin batabanje guheba ububasha bwabo. Baratahura ko Blockchain itanga ubushobozi buhambaye, ariko bafise intumbero yo kugenzura ingene yinjijwe mu bikorwa remezo vyabo biriho, aho kwakira uburyo bwa Bitcoin bushizwe ahantu hamwe, bwuguruye.

### Ingodo(wellet), gucukura(mining) n’iterambere: inkingi z'igisata


#### Ingodo ya Bitcoin


Mu mutima wo gufita Bitcoin kuri wallet yawe: igikoresho kidasanzwe kibika neza imfunguruzo z’ibanga zikenewe kugira ngo ushike ku kuma bitcoin yawe. Wallet ishobora gufata isura nyinshi: igikoresho cihariye, porogarama yo kuri telefone ngendanwa canke yo ku mashine nyabwonko, canke mbere urupapuro rwanditsweko urufunguzo. Ivyo bikoresho bifatanya ubutunzi bwawe bwa digitale n’isi nyakuri, bituma Bitcoin ikoreshwa mu buzima bwa misi yose.


Ubwoko bwose bwa Wallet butanga uburinganire butandukanye bwa:


- Ubuzima bw'ibanga
- Umutekano
- Biroroshe gukoresha
- Igiciro


Igisata ca Bitcoin Wallet zigizwe n’ibice vyinshi, kimwe cose kikaba gifise ivyo gikeneye bitandukanye be n’ingero z’ubuhinga butandukanye:



- **Abahinguzi ba Wallet ushobora gufata mu minwe**: Izo sosiyete zikora ibikoresho vyagenewe kubika imfunguruzo ata nkomanzi. Bimwe bifise inkomoko yuguruye, mu gihe ibindi bitanga inyishu zitandukanye zifise ibintu bitandukanye be n’ingero z’umutekano. Amazina azwi cane ni Ledger, Trezor, Coinkite, Umushinge, na Shiftcrypto.
  
- **abahinga bakora wallet zo ku buhinga bwa none**: Abo bava ku masosiyete gushika ku bahinga bigenga bakora porogarama zo kuri telefone ngendanwa n’izo kuri biro. Ivyo batanga biratandukanye n’ubumenyi bw’abakoresha, umutekano be n’ibintu birimwo. Ingero ni Sparrow, Sardine y’Umupfumu, Galoy, Ijambo rimwe, n’Igikoko c’Igihugu.
  
- **DIY (*Do It Yourself*) Wallets**:Izo nzira zifunguye zigenewe abakoresha bateye imbere bashaka kugenzura vyose no kwizigira cane abandi bantu. Kubaka Wallet yawe bwite bigabanya ukwizigira kandi birashobora kwongera umutekano wawe. Amahitamwo azwi cane yo gukora ni Seedsigner na Spectre gukora.


Ama wallets arafise uruhara runini muri Bitcoin kandi azokwihwezwa mu buryo burambuye mu nyuma muri iki cigwa.


#### Ugucukura Bitcoin


 Gucukura ni igikorwa nyamukuru c’urubuga rwa Bitcoin. Iratuma iyo sisitemu itekanye kandi igatuma Blockchain iguma ikora. Abacukuzi b’amabuye y’agaciro bemeza amafaranga kandi bagakingira urubuga mu gukora ubuhinga bwo guharura bukoresha inguvu nyinshi buzwi nka Proof of Work(agahembo). Buri bubiko bushasha bucukuwe bwongerako umugwi w’ibikorwa kuri Blockchain maze bugasohora amafaranga mashasha y’ama bitcoins hakurikijwe urutonde rw’ugusohora rw’amasezerano.


Mu misi ya mbere ya Bitcoin, gucukura vyoshobora gukorwa umuntu akoresheje inyabwonko y’umuntu ku giti ciwe. Ubu, ni uruganda rufise amahiganwa, rwo kw’isi yose ruganzwa n’amashirahamwe afise amahera menshi n’ubuhinga bwinshi. Ugushaka inkomoko z’inguvu zidahenda vyacitse ikintu nyamukuru, kuko abacukuzi b’amabuye y’agaciro bafise intumbero yo gutuma amafaranga y’ibikorwa n’inyungu bigenda neza. Ibikorwa vya Mining ubu biva ku bibanza binini vy'inganda gushika ku bibanza bitobito bikoreshwa mu ngo canke mu magaraje.


Igisata co gucukura kigizwe n'ibahinga benshi bafasha:



- **Abahinguzi b'ibikoresho**:Amashirahamwe nka Bitmain arahingura kandi agahingura ASICs (Ibikoresho vy'Ibikoresho vy'Ibikoresho), ibikoresho vy'ubuhinga vy'ubuhinga bwa none vyakozwe gusa ku bwo gucukura Bitcoin.
  
- **Mining pools**:Ivyo ni amashirahamwe y’abacukuzi bashira hamwe ubushobozi bwabo bwo gukoresha ubuhinga bwa none kugira ngo bongere amahirwe yo guhabwa impembo. Kubera ingorane zigenda zirushirizaho zo gucukura, ama pools atanga inyungu zishobora gutegekanirwa mu gutanga impembo z’ama block (ama bitcoins mashasha yacukuwe n’amahera y’ugucuruza) mu bari muri iyo nama bishingiye ku ntererano yabo. Ingero ni Foundry USA, AntPool, F2Pool, MARA, n’Ikiyaga ca Braiins.
  
- **Abacukuzi**:Abo ni abantu canke amashirahamwe akoresha ibikoresho n’amaporogarama ya Mining. Ku ruhande rumwe, hariho abacukuzi batobato bakoresha imashini nka Antminer S9, ku rundi ruhande, ibikorwa vy’inganda nka Galaxy Digital, bicungera ibikorwa binini cane vyerekeye gucukura.


Gucukura ni isi yiwe bwite, ifise ibice vyinshi vyo gutohoza; ingorane z’ubuhinga, inkurikizi z’ubutunzi, n’ivy’inguvu vyose biraza. Ku bashaka gutohoza cane aka karere no gutahura vy’ukuri ingene gakora, inyigisho yacu ya MIN201 iragutwara mu vyo ukeneye kumenya vyose.

Bitcoin ni uburyo bufunguye bwagenewe kuba buke, bukomeye kandi butekanye kuva mu ntango. Kugira ngo umuntu yongereko ibikorwa ataco ahinduye ku mishinge yavyo, muri rusangi amahinduka akorwa mu kwongerako **ibice vy’amasezerano** n’ibikorwa vy’inyongera bitunganya ibidukikije ataco bihinduye ku kwegereza ubutegetsi n’ugukomera kw’ibidukikije nyamukuru. Ukwo guhinduranya kwatumye amashirahamwe menshi n’abahinga mu vy’ubuhinga bwigenga bubaka ibikorwa vy'igisata ca Bitcoin, bongerako ubuhinga bushasha bujanye n’ikoreshwa ritandukanye.


### Bitcoin ukwaguka n'ibindi bice


Uburyo bwo gutera imbere butuma Bitcoin ishobora gutera imbere ata guhindura umurongo wayo nyamukuru, bikaba vyemeza ko ubuhinga nyamukuru buguma buhagaze kandi nuri mu mutekano. Ubwo buryo busa n’ingene Intereneti ikora, aho amasezerano menshi akorakorana kugira ngo atange ibikorwa bitandukanye mu gihe aguma akorana neza.


Mu mice nyamukuru igerekeranye ituma habaho gutera imbere cane kw'ico gisata ca Bitcoin harimwo:



- Lightning Network**:


Lightning Network, yaremwe na Thaddeus Dryja na Joseph Poon mu 2016, ni umuti wa kabiri wagenewe gutuma umuntu ashobora kuriha ubwo nyene kandi bagakata amahera make make cane. Abakoresha babiri barashobora barashobora kugurura umurongo kugira bahanahane amahera atankomanzi atan'uwubaraba ubutunzi burashirwa kugihe kuri blockchain mugihe uwo muhora wuguruwe canke wugaye. Ibikorwa vyo mu muhora bibera off-chain, bisobanura ko bidakeneye kwandikwa ku giti cabo kuri Bitcoin Blockchain. Uwo mubumbe utuma umuntu ashobora gukoresha amafaranga ako kanya kandi bakamukata amfaranga make cane, ivyo bikaba bituma akuriha amahera make asaba kwemezwa mumwanya muto.

### Kubera iki wobanza gushiramwo umwidondoro wawe?


Nta buryo bumwe bwo kugura no gucunga BTC. Uwukoresha wese afise ikibanza ciwe, gishingiye ku mibereho yiwe y’amahera, ubumenyi bwiwe, n’ivyo arindiriye canke yiyumvira kuronka muri bitcoin. Ni co gituma bihambaye guhitamwo ubuhinga bujanye n’ivyo wiyumvamwo. Kumenya uburyo ukoresha biragufasha gufata ingingo nziza no kwirinda amakosa asanzwe aterwa n'ukuttagereranya neza ivyo ukeneye.


Ngibi bimwe mu bintu vyo kwitwararika igihe urondera uburyo bwiza bwo kugura no kubika ama bitcoins yawe:



- **Amahera ushaka gushiramwo**: Woba ushaka gushotamwo umutahe mutoya kugira uranbe wongere wige utishize mu ngorane nyinshi? woba utegekanya gushiramwo umutahe munini, utegekanya kubikayo igihe kirekire

- **Incuro ushora amahera**: Woba ushka kugura rimwe, hama uce ukingira ayo mahera yawe mu maguru masha, canke woshima gukwiragiza ivyo ugura mu gihe ukoresheje ubuhinga nk’ubwo bwa Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) kugira ngo ushireho uguhinduka kw’ibiciro?
  
- **Ivyo ushaka gukora n’amahera yawe ya bitcoins**: Woba uyafata ataco ukora nk’amahera uziganije igihe kirekire? Uyikoresha mukuriha mu buzima bwa minsi yose? canke kimwe muri ivyo?
 
- **Ivyo uhagaze ku bijanye n’ubuzima bwite**: Woba uryohewe no gutanga amakuru y’ibanga no gukoresha ama ku mbuga ahuriweko kugira ngo ugure Bitcoin? Canke woba ukunda uburyo bwo gushira imbere ubuzima bwite nk’uguhanahana amakuru hagati y’abantu, ata KYC?
  
- **Ivyerekeye wewe bwite, ivy’amategeko, n’ivy’aho uba**: Bivanye n’aho uba, ushobora gushika ku bintu bimwebimwe vyo guhanahana amakuru bishobora kuba bibujijwe. Amategeko yo mu karere n’amategeko agenga amakori na vyo nyene birashobora kugira ico bikoze ku kuntu ubika no gukoresha Bitcoin yawe.


Ico kintu cose muri ivyo kirafise uruhara mu guhingura imikenyuro ibereye kuri wewe. Abakoresha bamwebamwe bazoha agaciro ukworohereza n’ukworoha gukoresha biciye mu bikorwa bitegekanijwe, mu gihe abandi bazohitamwo uburyo bwigenga kandi bwibanda ku buzima bwite.

#### Ivyago n'iterabwoba


Guhitamwo ubuhinga bwiza bwo kuronka no gukingira amafaranga yawe ya bitcoins bitangura n’ugusuzuma ata buryarya ivyawe ufise. Hari ibintu vyinshi bigira ico bikoze kuri izo ngingo, harimwo ingorane woshobora guhura nazo, uko ubayeho, n’ingene utegura gukoresha amahera yawe ya bitcoins:



- Ibitero vyo kuri Interinete**: Nimba utazi neza ivy’umutekano wo kuri Interinete canke ukoresha ibikoresho bishobora gushikirwa n’ingorane, woshobora guterwa n’abantu bo kuri Interinete. Muri ivyo, kubika ama bitcoins yawe ata murongo (ububiko bwa Cold) vyoshobora kuba uburyo butekanye.
  
- Iterabwoba kuei wewe**: Ikibabaje ni uko ibitero kuri wewe bishika; ubusuma, ubusuma, mbere n’ukunyurza abantu vyarabaye kubafise Bitcoin. Naho ivyo bintu bikiri bike kandi ahanini bitera abantu b’abanyacubahiro, birabereye ko ubizirikana kuva mu ntango igihe uriko urategura umutekano wawe.



- Ivyago vy'ihindagurika ry'ibihe**: Nimba uba ahantu hashobora guterwa n’umuriro w’ishamba, umwuzure canke ibindi bihe bikomeye cane, birahambaye cane ko ufise ubuhinga bwo gukingira imfunguruzo zawe z’ibanga.



- Kwaka amahera yawe na leta**: Nimba igihugu cawe gifise amategeko agenga ivy’ubutunzi abuza canke adashikamye, ushobora guhura n’imipaka mu kugura canke gukoresha Bitcoin. Mu bihe nk’ivyo, uzoshaka gushigikira inyishu zo kubika ibanga.



- **kwibagira canke gutakaza amakuru**: Gucungera imfunguruzo z’ibanga bisaba ko ubika no kugarura amakuru y’agaciro. Niba uhangayikishijwe n’ukwibagirwa canke ingorane z’amagara zishobora kubaho, ni vyiza gushiramwo uburyo bukomeye bwo kugarura ivyagiye; canke mbere ushiremwo umuntu wizigira mu mugambi wawe wo gucungera umutekano.


Kugira ngo utahure neza aho ushobora gushikirwa n’ingorane, nushire urugero kuri kimwe cose muri ivyo bibazo ku rugero ruva kuri 0 (ruto) gushika kuri 10 (ruhambaye). Ukwo kwisuzuma kuzogufasha gushiramwo imbere uburinzi bubereye mu gutera imbere.

#### Gukoresha ama bitcoins yawe


Ingamba zawe zo kubika no kuzicungera zikwiye kwerekana ingene ushaka gukoresha Bitcoin yawe. Kumenya neza ivyo kuva mu ntango ni vyo bizokuyobora mu ngingo ufata.



- **Ivyo uziganya mu gihe kirekire**: Ugura Bitcoin nk’ishoramari kandi ntutegekanya kuyikoresha vuba. Muri ivyo, umutekano mwinshi n’ingorane zo gushika ku bintu bike ni vyo bihambaye.
  
- **Kwirundanira buhoro buhoro**: Uba uriko uragura amahera makeyi ubudasiba. Ingamba nk’iyo gupima igiciro c’amadolari (DCA), ifatanijwe n’iyi Wallet itekanye, irakora neza.
  
- **Amafaranga y’umusi ku musi**: ou ukoresha Bitcoin nk’amahera. Koresha Wallet igendagenda ku bihembo bitobito vy’umusi (kumbure ukoresheje Lightning), kandi ubike igice kinini ca Bitcoin yawe ahantu hatekanye cane.
  
- **Gutegura gufata ikiruhuko c’izabukuru canke gutanga ubutunzi bwawe**: Ushaka gutanga Bitcoin yawe ku muntu mu nyuma. Koresha ibikoresho bituma vyoroha kandi ataco bihungabanya gutanga amahera igihe kigeze. Umaze kwiyumvira ingorane ingorane wafashe, ubuzima bwawe n’imigambi yawe, uzoba witeguriye guhitamwo neza urugendo rwawe rwa Bitcoin.

### Ama wallet n'imikenyuro yo gucungera umutekano


Gukingira imfunguruzo zawe z'ibanga (izo ziguha uruhusha rwo gushikira bitcoins zawe) zifise uruhara ruhambaye cane co kugira no gukoresha Bitcoin. Bitandukanye cane na konti yo muri banki, aho hari uyundi muntu agenzura amahera, Bitcoin iraguhereza uburenganzira bwawe bwose. Ariko uwo mwidegemvyo uzanana n'ivyo utegerezwa nawe gukora: iyo utakaje imfunguruzo zawe, Bitcoin yawe(zawe) ziratakara burundu. Hari ubwoko bwinshi bw’ama wallet ushobora gukoresha. Imwimwe yose irafise ibibi n'ivyiza bivanye nivyo ukeneye canke ivyo ushoboye.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/067.webp)


#### Hot Wallet


Ama wallet ya Hot ni ama apirikasiyo canke porogarama zihujwe na internet. Bibika imfunguruzo zawe z’ibanga ku gikoresho kimwe aho zishizwe. Ivyo bikoresho ni vyiza cane mwi koreshwa rya buri musi canke mu kubika amafaranga makeyi ya Bitcoin.


Akarorero: Blue Wallet, Green-Wallet, Sparrow-wallet

izikorana na lightining: Phoenix, Wallet ya Satoshi, BitKit


**Ivyiza** :



- Biroroshe gukoresha kandi ushobora gushika ku mahera yawe ningoga.
- Ni vyiza ku kuriha amahera makeya no gukoresha ku musi ku musi.
- Hari abashigikiye Lightning Network kugira ngo umuntu ashobore gukoresha amafaranga yihuta kandi atari menshi.


**Ibibi** :



- Mutekano mutoyi: imfunguruzo zawe ziri ku gikoresho gifatanye na internet, ivyo bikaba vyongera ingorane zo kwinjirirwa.
- Ntibibereye kubika amahera menshi ikiringo kinini.


Ni ngirakamaro kuri: abashasha , amafaranga make, n’ukurungika canke kwakira umwanya wose.


#### Ama wallet y'ibikoresho


Hardware wallets(ziri kugikoresho ushobora gufata mu minwe) ni ibikoresho bibika imfunguruzo zawe z’ibanga hanzi y'imbuga ngurukana bumenyi. ni nziza cane zifise umutekano cane kuruta ama wallet ya Hot kuko zigabanya ingorane zo guterwa kuri internet.


Ingero: Ledger, Igikoresho co gutera, Ikarata y'ubukonje, Jade, Isanduku y'ibice


**Ivyiza** :



- Imfunguruzo ziri inyuma y'imbuga na interinete = biragoye cane abasuma kuzironka.
- Yagenewe canecane umutekano.


**Ibibi** :



- zirateba cane uriko urakoresha; ukeneye gufatanya igikoresho(online) no kwemeza kuri ca gikoresho cawe amafaranga(offline).
- Uzokenera kugura ico gikoresho, gishobora kugutwara amahera meshi.


Ni ngirakamaro kuri: Abagura kugira babike igihe kirekire cane, n'abantu bose bashaka kugura bitcoins nyinshi cane.


#### Ama wallet afise imikono myinshi


Ama wellet y'imikono myinshi(Afise imikono menshi) asaba urufunguzo rurenze rumwe kugira ngo umuntu yemerere kwakira canke kurungika. Iyumvire nk’inzu y’ububiko ikeneye imigwi ibiri canke itatu(amajambo y'ibanga) kugira ngo yuguruke. Ivyo bikoresho ni vyiza ku bakoresha bateye imbere, abacuruzi, canke umuntu wese arondera umutekano wo ku rwego rwo hejuru.


Ingero: Liana, Casa, Specter, Sparrow


**Ivyiza** :



- Uburinzi bukomeye cane; iyo urufunguruzo rumwe rwibwe, amahera yawe aguma mu mutekano.
- Bishobora kugabanya ingorane ziterwa n’ubusuma, gutakaza canke kwinjirirwa kunguvu.
- Ushobora kugabanganya impfunguruzo n'abantu benshi canke mu bibanza vyinshi.


**Ibibi** :



- Biragoye cane kuyikoresha no gucungera.
- Bishobora gusaba uguhuza hagati y’abantu/ibikoresho vyinshi.
- Icoba co gutakaza amahera iyo Multisig utayiravye neza;
- Igiciro kinini co kugura mu ntango, bishobora gusaba kugura ama wellet menshi cane mu gutangura


Ni ngirakamaro kurikuri: Abakoresha basaba ibintu vyinshi, imiryango, amashirahamwe, n’ububiko bw’igihe kirekire busaba umutekano mwinshi.


#### Ibikorwa vyo kubungabunga hamwe na ETFs


Abantu bamwe bamwe bahitamwo kubika Bitcoin n’ibikorwa vy’abandi nk’uguhinduranya, ababikira, canke ETFs. Ivyo bituma uronka ubumenyi kuri Bitcoin utacungera imfunguruzo z’ibanga wewe nyene. Ariko rero, biza n’ibintu bikomeye bishobora gutuma umuntu agira ivyo akora.


Akarorero: Imbuga z'ubudandaji nka Coinbase canke Binance, ibintu vy’ivy’ubutunzi nka Bitcoin ETF ya BlackRock.


**Ivyiza** :



- Biroroshe gukoresha; ntibikenewe ko ufata ukoresha wewe nyene impfunguruzo zawe canke wallet yawe.
- Ni ngirakamaro ku bantu bashaka gusa gushirwa ahabona kw’ugushora imitahe, batiriwe barakoresha bitcoin.


**Ibibi** :



- Mu vy’ukuri nturi nyene bitcoin yawe; uwurinda arabigira. Iyo ahomvye, n’amahera yawe yoshobora guhomba nayo (baza gusa abakoresha Mt. Gox canke FTX).
  
- Amafaranga ashobora kuba menshi kandi ata mutekanao ukwiye.
  
- Kwizerera ku muntu agira gatatu, bisobanura kwiyaha uburenganzira bwawe, kimwe mu bintu nyamukuru vyiza kuri bitcoin;
  
- Nturonka inyungu zose za Bitcoin mu bijanye n’ubuzima bw'ibanga hamwe n'umutekano n'uburenganzira bwose ku mafaranga yawe.


**Itegeko ry'inzahabu: Si imfunguruzo zawe, si ibiceri vyawe.**


Ntiduhimiriza ibikorwa vyo kubika. Ni vyiza cane gufata umwanya wo kwiga ingene wokingira neza Bitcoin yawe kurusha kwama uraho ushobora kuzitakaza umwanya wose bivuye ku gufata ibintu minenerwe. Ariko nimba uri hano, usoma ivyerekeye ama wallet n’ugucungera urufunguzo, birashoboka ko umaze gutahura igituma iyo ngingo ngenderwako ihambaye.

### Uburyo bwo kuronka n'ubunini bw'amafaranga


Hari uburyo butandukanye bwo kuronka ama bitcoins, bivanye n’ingene ukeneye ubuzima bwite, amategeko yo mu gihugu cawe, n’ingene ushaka kuronka. Hari uburyo bubiri nyamukuru:


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/070.webp)


#### Kuronka n'Igenzura ry'Akaranga (KYC)


Imbuga ategekanijwe akenshi asaba ko usuzuma akaranga kawe imbere yo kugura Bitcoin. Ivyo bisigura ko uzokenera gutanga ivyangombwa vyemewe (pasiporo, karangamuntu, ikimenyetso ca Address, n’ibindi) kugira ngo ukore konti no kuronka ibikorwa vyo kugura no kugurisha.


**Ivyiza** :



- Uburyo bwo kugura bworoshe, kenshi bubereye abashasha;
- Gushobora kugura amahera menshi icarimwe;
- Ugushika ku bindi bikorwa (DCA, guhindura ubwavyo, gukura fiat vyoroshe) ;
- Ivy’amahera menshi bifise itandukaniro ritoyi hagati y’ibiciro vyo kugura n’ivyo kugurisha.


**Ibibi** :



- Amafaranga bakata mu kuvunjisha no kubikura amahera;
- Hariho imbuga zigoye kandi ashobora kugurengera n’ibintu vy’ubudandaji biteye imbere canke altcoins;
- Ibibazo bikomeye vy’ubuzima bw'ibanga.


Ingaruka mbi nyamukuru c'imbuga zisaba KYC ni uko ushobora guhura nibibazo vyo gutegwa mu buzima bwawe bw'ibanga, bishobora kandi gutuma umutekano wawe bwite uhungabana. KYC iremeza ko abahinduranya amakuru bashobora guhuza umwidondoro wawe nyakuri n’amaderesi yawe yo kubikura Bitcoin. Aya makuru aba intango nziza yo gusesangura Blockchain. Igihe Address imenyekanye, birashoboka gukurikirana amafaranga yose ajanye n'iyo aderese, ivyo bikaba bishobora gutuma ubuzima bw'ibanga bwawe On-Chain buhungabana. Ata ngingo z’ukwikingira zindi, igikorwa cawe cose ca Bitcoin gishobora gukurikiranwa kugeza kuri wewe.


Ikindi kibazo nyamukuru kiri mukuntu reta ishobora, ifise uburenganzira bwo gushikira mu rutonde rw'amakuru atunganijwe, vyumvikana y'uko leta ishobora kumenya neza abantu baguze bitcoin. Naho ivyo biboneka nk'aho vyoroshe mur'iyi minsi, vyoshobora kuba ikibazo iyo hashizweho amategeko agenga ubutunzi canke politike. Ikindi kandi, amashirahamwe ategekanijwe ategerezwa gutanga raporo y’ibikorwa vyose biteye amakenga, ivyo bikaba bituma habaho amahirwe menshi yo kugenzurwa na reta.


Kandi, imbuga za KYC abika amakuru menshi y’agacamutwe, harimwo n'amakarata karangamuntu hamwe n'ama aderese y'abantu. Aya makuru akenshi atangazwa n’abandi bajejwe kugenzura umwidondoro. Iyo urubuga rwinjiriwe canke rukananirwa, ayo makuru arashobora gushirwa ahabona maze agakoreshwa mu guhenda abantu, mu kugerageza kwiba canke guhenda(ubupfungakiwani). Mu buryo butandukanye n’ibindi bimenyetso vy’uguhungabanya amakuru, amakuru ajanye na Bitcoin arateye ikibazo cane cane kuko arashobora kwerekana ko afise ikintu c'agaciro.


Rero, birakenewe cane gutahura ko guca muri KYC atari intambwe isanzwe utoha agaciro; **birashobora kugira ingaruka mbi ku mutekano wawe**. Intwaro zisaba ivyo vyangombwa vy'umwidondoro ntiziriko zirahungabanya gusa uburenganzira bwawe bwa kavukire, ariko kandi ziriko ziragushira mu kaga. Naho gukoresha izo nzira bishobora kugufasha gutangura gukoresha Bitcoin, ukwiye kumenya ingorane zirimwo kandi ukiyumvira gutohoza uburyo butajanye n’ubuhinga bwa KYC kugira ngo uronke Bitcoin udakoze KYC.


#### Kuronka BTC utabanje gukora KYC (No-KYC)


Kugura Bitcoin udatanze umwidondoro wawe ni uburyo bwo guhitamwo ku bashira imbere ubuzima bwite. Ubwo buryo burashobora gukoresha uburyo bwinshi:



- **Uguhanahana hagati y'abantu babiri (P2P) hagati y’abantu atawubagiye hagati**: Kugura ubwonyene n'amafaranga ku muntu wizeye (abagenzi, umuryango, urubuga rwo mu karere, amateraniro...) biririnda amakuru yose yemewe y’ibikorwa vy’ubudandaji bikurinda abagukurikirana kandi ikujana kure ya sisteme zama banki. Naho ubu ari bwo buryo bwiza bwo kugura canke kugurisha Bitcoin, izana n'ingorane zo kugirirwa nabi mu buryo bw'umubiri kuko ari uguhanahana murikumwe.
  
- **Imbuga zo guhanahana hagati y'abantu babiri**: Ivyuma nka Bisq, RoboSats na Peach bifasha mu guhanahana amakuru hagati y’abantu atawubagiye hagati ataco bakeneye kugenzura akaranga, hakoreshejwe uburyo bwo gukingira amafaranga. Izo nzira ziracari zifise ingorane zo kwibwa iyo uwundi adakoranye, ariko ingorane zigabanywa n’ibikorwa vy’ububiko.
  
- **Ivyuma bitanga amahera y'ama bitcoins (ATM)**: Amamashini amwe amwe (kiosks) araguha uburenganzira bwo kugura amahera makeya y'ama Bitcoin, naho amafaranga akatwa aba menshi, kandi bisaba umwidondoro ukwiye kugira ukureko amafaranga menshi cane.
  
- **Imbuga zo guhanahana zitagira KYC**: Ivyuma bimwebimwe, cane cane mu Busuwisi, biguha uburenganzira bwo kugura amahera makeyi ya Bitcoin udasavye gutnga indangamuntu.
  
- **Mining(gucukura)**: Abantu basanzwe barashobora gucukura Bitcoin. Amamashini ya kera canke zidakomeye cane zirahari, agatanga uburyo butamenyerewe bwo kuronka Bitcoin ata KYC. Mu kwifatanya na Mining pool, urashobora kuronka amahera yinjira nurimunsi, mbere n’ibikoresho vy’ishimikiro. Ushaka kumenya vyinshi, [raba inyigisho zacu za Mining](https://planb.network/inyigisho/Mining).
  
- **Guhembwa / Umushahara mu bitcoins**: Niba ukora ubucuruzi, urashobora kwemera Bitcoin nk'amafaranga urihwa. Niba ukora, bivanye n'ububasha bwawe, ushobora gusaba ko baguhembwa muri Bitcoin. Ubu buryo buratuma uronka Bitcoin ata KYC.


**Ingaruka nziza** :



- Bivanye n’uburyo bukoreshejwe, birafasha kurinda ubuzima bw'ibanga bwawe mu kugabanya amasano ari hagati y’akaranga kawe n’igikorwa cawe ca Bitcoin;
- Bigabanya ingorane zo gucengera, guhagarika amafaranga, canke kwivanga kwa reta muvyo ukora.
- Igufasha gushika ku Bitcoin no mu bibanza bigendurwa cane.


**Ingaruka mbi** :



- Kenshi na kenshi biragoye kuruta imbuga ya KYC;
- Imahera abona ni make kandi uguhanahana ni guke;
- Iyo abantu babiri bahanahanye bikunda kujana nutwo barenzako;
- Ivyago vyinshi vy’uguhanahana hagati y'abantu babiri (ububeshi, ugucisha ku ruhande, kugirirwa nabi, n'ibindi).


### Ubwoko 4 bw'abakoresha Bitcoin


Dushingiye kubintu twavuzeko, turashobora kumenya imirwi myinshi y’abakoresha Bitcoin. Ivyo bimenyetso birahinduka kandi birashobora guhinduka, ariko birafasha gusobanura imikenyuro ibereye ubwoko bumwe bumwe no kukuyobora mu guhitamwo ibikoresho bikwiye.


#### 1. Umunya hodler


Uwo hodler ni umunyamitahe agura Bitcoin afise iciyumviro c’igihe kirekire kandi ntategura kuyakorako hadaciye imyaka myinshi. Atandukanye n'umu Stacker, agura ubudasiba uko igihe, Hodler akenshi agura nyinshi, hanyuma akabika vyonyene ama bitcoins yiwe.




Imikenyuro ikoreshwa cane :



- Kuguera ku mbuga zisaba kugira KYC canke kugura ku mbuga zidakoresha KYC bivanye n'ivyo akaneye ku bijanye n'ubuzima bwiwe bw'ibanga;
- Ububiko kuri wallet canke gukoresha umuti uburyo bw'imikono myinshi kugira ngo umuntu agire umutekano mwinshi;
- Guhanahana bidakunda kuba, bikagabanya ugukorana n'igisata ca vya Bitcoin.


Uwufise Bitcoin afata iyo bitcoin yiwe nk'ububiko bw'agaciro, busa n’ugushora imitahe w’inzahabu y’umubiri. Ingingo yiwe nyamukuru ni ukubika bitcoin mu mutekano mwinshi no gutegura neza uko azoziraga.


#### 2. Uwurundanya


Uwurundanya ni umuntu afata uburyo buhoro buhoro, kandi ubudasiba. Bagura Bitcoin ubudasiba, kenshi biciye mu nzira y’ugupima igiciro c’amadolari (DCA). yabo ni ugukwiragiza igiciro c’ukugura kwabo mu gihe c’igihe no kugabanya ingaruka z’uguhinduka kw'ibiciro kwa Bitcoin.


Imikenyuro ikoreshwa cane:



- Koresha ukugura vyikora biciye ku rubuga rwa KYC rutanga DCA;
- Kurungika amahera umwanya wose mu ngodo yawe ngendanwa kugira ngo abikwe neza ;
- Igira ivyiyumviro bibereye ku bijanye n’ubuzima bwibanga ariko ishira imbere umutekano n’ukworohereza kwirundanira.


Aba Stacker ntibashimika ku kurindira igihe gito canke ngo bakoreshe cane ama bitcoin yabo. Babona ko Bitcoin ari umugambi wo kuziganya vyo ku gihe kirekire, ukamara imyaka canke mbere imyaka mirongo. ico nico gitandukanya Stacker na Hodler ni uburyo akoreramwo hamwe n'uburinganire bw'uko ahanahana amahera.


#### 3. Umukoresha akora cane


Uwukoresha akora cane abona Bitcoin ahanini nk’uburyo bwo guhanahana aho kubona ububiko bw'agaciro. Bayikoresha akenshi mu gucuruza, mu kuriha,canke guhanahana canke mu gukora ubutunzi buzunguruka.


Imikenyuro ikoreshwa cane:



- Koresha Hot Wallet kuri ngendanwa yawe canke ku nyabwonko yawe kugira uze urarungika canke ukoreshe mu kuriha mu buzima busanzwe;
- Koresha Lightning Network kugira ushobore kuriha kansi, uriha vyihuse kandi bagukata amafaranga make cane;
- Gukomeza uburyo bwo gukorana n’ibindi, ukoresheje Wallet ngendanwa kugira ngo ukoreshe amahera, ukoresheje wallet yo mu minwe kugira ngo uzigame;
- Ukubangamirwa kw'ubuzima bw'ibanga bivanye n'ivyo ukeneye.


Uwukoresha ashobora kuba ari uwiyungunganya canke umudandaza, akora ku giti ciwe, canke umuntu aba aba mu karere aho bitcoin ikoreshwa cane nk'uburyo bwo kuriha bwa minsi yose. icihutigwa ni ikoreshwa ryoroshe hamwe n'umutekano, hamwe n'ukunganisha hagati y'umutekano n'umuco.


#### 4. Afise ubwoba bw’abantu


Uwu ni uwukoresha ashira imbera ya vyose umutekano w'ubuzima bw'ibanga. Babona ko Bitcoin ari igikoresho co kwidegemvya gusa ku muntu ubwiwe. ariko kandi kugabanya cane bishoboka uko umuntu abonwe ku ruhande rw'ukwizerana hamwe no mu mategeko .


Imikenyuro ikoreshwa cane:



- Kuronka Bitcoin atahandi biciye atari mu nzira ya KYC;
- Koresha ama wallets yongeweko amajambo y’ibanga BIP39 n’inyishu y’imikono myinshi;
- Bika Bitcoin mu bikoresho vyinshi bitandukanye mu bibanza bitandukanye;
- Irinde ibikorwa hamwe n'ama serevisi agenzurwa bishobora guhungabanya ubuzima bwawe bw'ibanga.


Ubwo bwoko bw'umukoresha ni bwo bwoba bukenenwe mu burere bitcoin yankwa cane, aho gutunga canke gukoresha bitcoin bishobora kugukwegera ibihano. Ni uburyo bugoranye cane, busaba ubuhinga bumwe bumwe hamwe n'indero igoye cane kugira ntutakaze uruhusha ufise rwo gushikira amahera.

### Ni ubuhe bwoko bw'umukoresha buhuye na wewe?


Zirikana ko ubwo bwoko bw'abakoresha atari gungere ku muntu umwe ahubwo harashobora kubaho umuntu umwe ari hose muri ubwo bwoko, kandi arashobora guhindura uburyo bwiwe uko igihe kigenda. Bivuzeko, woshobora gutangura nk’umuteranya ibintu maze buhorobuhoro ugahinduka uwukoresha cane canke mbere ugafata umuzirikanyi w’umutekano w’agahomerabunwa. Ushobora gutangura uri umuhodler w’igihe kirekire mu gihe ukiriko urakoresha Bitcoin mu buzima bwawe bwa misi yose.


**Igihambaye cane n'uko womenyereza imikenyuro yawe ushingiye ku buzima ubayemwo hamwe n'imbono yawe yo ku gihe kirekire.**


Mu bice bikurikira, tuzokwihweza mu buryo bwimbitse ubwoko bwose bw'umukoresha kugira ushobore gutahura no kumenya ni ubuhe bwoko bukubereye wokoresha; kandi ukoreshe uburyo bufise insiguro kuruta izindi ku migambi yawe. ivyo bizogufasha kubaka umukoenyuro bwite kandi mwiza wo kugira hamwe n'umutekano.

Mu muryango wa Bitcoin, ijambo "hodler" ryerekeza ku muntu afata uburyo bwo gushiramwo imitahe ku gihe kirekire. Ijambo ryakomotse ku makosa azwi cane yo kwandika ngo "hold" ku nama y'ibiganiro ya Bitcoin mu myaka iheze kandi kuva ico gihe ryacitse ikimenyetso c'iciyumviro co kubika Bitcoin yawe uko vyogenda kwose utitaye ku kuntu isoko ihindagurika ku gihe gitoya
.
![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/071.webp)


Aba Hodlers bemera iciyumviro c’agaciro ca Bitcoin. Kubera ko bitcoin zihari zitazorenga imiliyoni 21, babona ko Bitcoin ari ububiko bw’agaciro bw’igihe kirekire(inzahabu ya digitale).


### Ni kuki wohitamwo ubuhinga bwo kubika(hodling)?


Mu mateka, hodling ni ni umukenyuro winjiza amahera menshi ku banyamitahe benshi batabimenyereye. Bikuraho ivy’ugukenera gufata umwanya mw’isoko, ico naco ni igikorwa tuzi ko kigoye kandi gitesha umutwe. Mu kugura no kubika gusa, umu hodler yirinze uguteshwa umutwe n'amasoko, kandi yirinze gufata ingingo mbi, kand akenshi zituma uhomba.

Kuva kera, Bitcoin ikunda kugendagenda mu nzinguzingu bigizw ahanini n'ibihe vyo kuduga cane, hanyuma hakaba ibihe birebire vy’uguceceka. Hagati y’umwaka wa 2013 n’uwa 2025, Bitcoin yarashikiwe n’ivyo bihe vyo kuduga vyinshi, rimwe na rimwe igatakaza gushika ku bice 80% vy’agaciro kayo.. Ivyo bice birashobora kugerageza ukwihangana kw’umu Hodler, mugabo abaguma muri iyo nzira muri rusangi barunguka uko igihe kigenda kirarenga.


Gutahura izo nzinguzingu birafasha kumenya uko witwara ntutwagwe n'ibigumbagumba. ### Ni kuki wohitamwo ubuhinga bwo kurundanya?

Kurundanya ni ubuhinga bwiza cane bwo gushiramwo amahera ku bantu bashaka kugabanya ukwinjira kwabo ku masoko kandi bakaguma bagura buhoro buhoro ama bitcoins. Mu buryo butandukanye n’ugushiramwo amahera y’igihe kimwe canke igihe c’isoko., Uwurundanya akoresha cane bwitwa Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA); gushiramwo amahera atari make mu bihe bitandukanye (ku musi, ku ndwi, ku kwezi...), bivanye n'agaciro ka Bitcoin kiriho muri uwo mwanya.


Inyungu zo kurundanya :



- **Kugabanya ivyo ahitamwo**: Kugura mu buryo buhoro buhoro, uwurundanya agabanya ingaruka zo guhitamwo ku mutahe wiwe muri rusangi, kuberako agura ama sats menshi iyo ibiciro biri hasi kansi akagura bike mugihe ibiciro biri hejuru. agabanya igiciro ciwe kandi ababanya guta umutwe bifatiye ku gutakara kw'agaciro.
  
- **Ugushikira**: Ntukeneye umutungo munini w’intango. Ushobora gutangura n’amahera makeyi nk’amayero 10 ku kwezi maze ukongera amahera yawe ya DCA uko igihe kigenda kirarenga.
  
- **Intonde n’ukwikoresha** : Abarundanya baririnda gufata ingingo zishingiye bafatiye ku kuntu biyumva. Ivyo ugura bisubiramwo bikoresheje ubuhinga bwa none biratuma haba uguhuza kandi bikagabanya ingorane zo kwimuka ata co wiyumviriye.
  
- ***Imikenyuro y’Igihe Kirekire**: Gushiramwo amahera buhoro buhoro birongereza amahirwe yo kwungukira ku masoko y’ibimasa mu gihe bigabanya ingorane mu gihe c’ugusubira inyuma kw’ubutunzi.


Ubwo buryo cane cane **bubereye neza abashasha**; birakuraho umukazo wo gushinga igihe isoko kandi bikaba bituma habaho ukwirundanira gukomeye, ataco bimaze.

## Uwukoresha Bitcoin: Guhitamwo gukoresha Bitcoin mu buzima bwa misi yose


<chapterId>e0a022ab-207c-571f-b4ad-c432214a756c</chapterId>


:::id ya videwo=5f6a7b8c-9d0e-1f2a-3b4c-5d6e7f8a9b0c::


Kuba umukoresha w’umunyamwete wa Bitcoin, ni uguhitamwo kuja mu bakoresha aya mahera mu buzima bwa minsi yose, Naho bija kure yo gushiramwo imitahe vy’igihe kirekire. Udakunze hodler canke stacker, baziganya amahera bayasohora, uwukoresha w'umwete afata Bitcoin nk'uburyo nyabwo bwo gukoresha mu guhanahana canke kuriha**. Bayikoresha mu kuriha ibintu n’ibikorwa kandi bakagira uruhara n’umwete mu bikorwa vyo kurwanya ubutunzi.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/083.webp)


### Ni kuki wohitamwo umukenyuro w'uwo mukoresha w'umunyamwete?


Gukoresha Bitcoin ku musi ku musi biratanga inyungu iboneka ku muntu ku giti ciwe no ku bantu bose:



- **Guterea agacumu k'ubumwe umuryango wa bitcoin mu gufasha muri iryo koreshwa ryayo intambwe kuyindi** :


Ukuriha canke kurungika bikozwe bikomeza ikoresha rya bitcoin nk'amahera kandi bituma abadandaza canke abatanga ama serevisi bayemera. Kandi ni ngirakamaro cane gutahura ko gukoresha bitcoin ni nk'uburyo bwo gutora. Amahera abona agaciro kayo mu bushobozi afise bwo kworohereza ubudandaji; si uguhinduranya. Iyo ayo mahera atemewe n'abadandaza, nta gaciro afise. Uko urushiriza kugura ibintu n’ibikorwa vyinshi, ni ko urushiriza kugira akamaro. Mu guhitamwo kuriha hakoreshejwe Bitcoin, uba uriko uratuma n'abandi bayikunda. Ivyo bituma bongera inyungu zabo kandi bishobora, kugabanya abo muhangana. Mu kibano cose, iyo nzira irashobora gutuma abantu benshi bemera Bitcoin kandi igatuma habaho uruzitiro rwiza rwo kwemerwa.



- **Ukoreshe neza ibintu vyihariye kuri Bitcoin** :


Bitcoin itanga ibintu vyinshi bikomeye, nk’uguhanahana binyaruka cane biciye ku Lightning Network, n’ukwidegemvya ku bijanye n’ugucengera, gucungera ivy’amahera, n’ugukora seigniorage. Agaciro kayo nyamukuru kari mu gukuraho ububasha bwa Leta ku mafaranga no gutuma havaho gucungera amafaranga atembera ahanahanwa. Nimba izo nyungu zihuye n’ivyo ukeneye be n’agaciro kawe, woshobora gusanga urutonde rw’Umukoresha Akora rugubereye neza.



- **Tahura neza ubuhinga bwa none** :


 Gukoresha cane Bitcoin biragufasha gutera imbere mu bumenyi bushingiye ku bikorwa. Uzomenya ubwoko bwa Wallet, ubumutekano wo kuriha, n’uburongozi bwa UTXO. Gukoresha ku musi ku musi bica bihinduka ikibanza co kwigiramwo ngirakamaro.

Mugabo, ni ngirakamaro gufata mu minwe ibibazo bimwe bimwe: Ubuhinga bugoye, ubushobozi bw'uko ibiciro bigira ibiharuro vy'ivyasohotse, hamwe no kumenya neza gutunganya neza ivy'umutekano hamwe no kuronka ubumenyi bushasha.

### Gusuzuma umwidondoro wawe w'ukoresha


Woba ufise umwidondoro kugira ngo ube umukoresha wa Bitcoin akora cane? Akira ibiranga nyamukuru bisobanura iyi nkuru:



- **Ukwiyemeza n'ugufata ingingo** Urafise umwete wo kwinjiza Bitcoin mu buzima bwawe bwa misi yose no kugira uruhara runini mu gukura kw’ibidukikije vyayo. Urazi ivyiza n’ingorane bizanana n’ivyo.
  
- **Ubuhinga bw’ubuhinga:** Ushobora kuba usanzwe ufise ugutahura gukomeye kw’ingene ama wallets ya Bitcoin na Lightning Network akora; canke ufise umushasharo wo kwiga. Uraryoherwa no kugendera mu vyiyumviro biteye imbere nk’uburongozi bwa UTXO.

- **Guhinduranya no Guhinduranya:** Urafunguye kugira ngo ugerageze ibikoresho bishasha, uhindure hagati y’imiti, no guhindura ingendo yawe uko ibidukikije vya Bitcoin bigenda biratera imbere kandi ivyo ukeneye bihinduka.
  
- **Inyifato nziza(indero) mu vy'ubutunzi:** Ucungera neza amahera ukoresha n’ayo uzigamye mu Bitcoin. Uratahura akamaro ko gutandukanya neza amahera ukoresha ku musi ku musi n’ayo uzigama mu kiringo kirekire.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/082.webp)


Mu gusozera, uwukoresha cane arafise uruhara rudasanzwe mu gutuma igisata ca Bitcoin gitera imbere. Mu gushiramwo Bitcoin mu ngeso zabo za misi yose, ntibashigikira gusa ukuyifata ariko kandi barakura mu buhinga bwabo bwite.


Mu kigabane gikurikira, tuzoca irya n’ino urutonde rw’abakoresha Bitcoin rwa nyuma kandi kumbure ruteye umunezero kuruta abandi bose: umukoresha yama yibaza ko bamurwanya.


## Umuntu Yama yibaza ko bamurwanya: Ubuzima bwite hejuru ya vyose

Ku ba Bitcoiner bafise ubwoba, ubuzima bwabo bw'ibanga si ubw’uguhitamwo; ivy’ingenzi vyayo. Mu isi aho hafi ingendo yose y’ivy’ubutunzi ikurikiranywa, babona ko kurinda akaranga kabo n’amateka y’ibikorwa vyabo ari ikintu kidashobora gusubirwamwo mu gukoresha Bitcoin. Ubusanzwe barazi neza ingorane ziri mu gushikiriza amakuru y’umuntu ku giti ciwe (na cane cane iyo bakoresheje ubuhinga bwo guhindura amakuru bushingiye kuri KYC busaba ko umuntu asuzuma karangamuntu).


Kuri bo, igikorwa cose kirahambaye. Igihe cose umuntu akora, igihe cose akora Wallet, n’igihe cose umuntu akorana n’urubuga, birasuzumwa neza. Gunyerera rimwe gusa vyoshobora guhishura amakuru y’agaciro ahuza akaranga kabo n’ivyo bafise Bitcoin. Intumbero yabo nyamukuru? Koresha Bitcoin mu buryo buzotuma akaranga kabo, aho bari be n’ingene amahera yabo bimeze bitamenyekana rwose.

### Ni kubera iki wofata ingingo nk’iyo?


Guhitamwo umutima wo gushira imbere ubuzima bw'ibanga biratanga inyungu nyinshi :



- **Kugumana umwidegemvyo wawe w'ivy'ubutunzi:** kwirinda ugucungerwa n'amabanki, intwaro n’amashirahamwe akora, urakingira ukugendurwa na reta canke imbuga zigendurwa.
  
- **Ukugabanya gushobora gushikirwa n'ingorane yo kw'akwa ama BTC yawe:** Iyo ubutunzi busubira inyuma canke budahagaze neza bivuye kuri politike, biragoye cane ko abandi bantu bakurikirana canke bafata Bitcoin y'umukoresha ashira imbere umutekano wiwe.
  
- **Kugabanya ingorane zo kuboneka cane hamwe n'abashobora kukugirira nabi:** Abantu bazwi ko bafise ibigwanisho vyinshi vya Bitcoin barashobora kuba abafatwa. Gukomeza guhisha akaranga kawe biragabanya ingorane zo gufatwa n’abantu.


- **Ukugabanya ivangura mu vy'ubutunzi**: Hari ibikorwa canke intwaro zishobora guhagarika amafaranga canke amakonti ashingiye ku gikorwa cawe ca kera canke uwo uri we. Ubuzima bw'ibanga buratuma ubwo bubasha buguma bugenzurwa.
  
- **Gukomeza kuba intahemuka ku mizi ya Bitcoin:** Bitcoin yari yateguwe nk’uburyo bw’amahera butagira uruhusha kandi butagira uruhusha. Gukingira ubuzima bwite bwawe ni kimwe mu vyo gutera iteka iyo ntumbero y’intango.


Ubwo buryo buri kura cane yo kuba nk'iyi nyifato isanzwe, iharuwe ku bibazo vy’ukuri biriho mu bijanye n’ubuhinga bwa none n’ivy’amahera. Egome, iyo nzira isaba utwigoro twinshi. Bisaba umwanya, ubumenyi bw’ubuhinga n’indero. Ariko ku bahisemwo iyo nzira, si ugukoresha Bitcoin gusa; ni ukugira ngo basubire kugenzura amahera yabo, akaranga kabo, n’umwidegemvyo wabo.

Si abashoramari canke abakoresha gusa; bariko barahitamwo kuva mu nzira isaba kumenya vyose ku bijanye na bo.

#### Ivyiyumviro bitari vyo bikunze gufatwa ku bijanye n'ugutegura iragi


Abantu benshi baracerezwa n’iyo ntambwe kubera ivyiyumviro bitari vyo bibabuza kugira ico bakoze. Aha hariho inkuru z’imigani zisanzwe, zavuzwe na Pamela Morgan mu _Itegeko ry’Iragi ry’Ivy’Imitungo_ (urupapuro rwa 18):



- **"*Ndakeneye gukoresha umushingwa manza.*"** → **Ikinyoma**. Naho umuvoka yoshobora gufasha mu bibazo vy’amategeko, umugambi w’iragi w’ubuhinga Bitcoin urashobora gushirwaho ata n’umwe. Igihambaye cane ni ukugira amabwirizwa atomoye kandi ashobora gushikirwa;
  
- **"*Ndakeneye kwizigira uwundi muntu.*"** → **Ikinyoma**. Itegeko ryawe rishobora gutegurwa mu buryo bugabanya ivy’ukwizigira, nk’ugukwiragiza amakuru ku bantu benshi canke gukoresha inyishu z’imikono myinshi, harimwo canke hatagira igihe co gufunga;
  
- **"*Gutegura bizotuma ivy'umutungo wanje vyoroha kwibwa.*"** → **Ikinyoma**.Itegeko ryiyumviriwe neza rikingira ubusuma mu gihe nyene rituma abazungu bawe bashobora kugarura amahera yawe ata nkomanzi. Ariko rero, birahambaye kumenya ko ata muti udashobora guhera: umugambi w’iragi uratuma haba ingorane zo kwiba, bivanye n’amakuru asangiye. Ivyo tuzobivuga mu buryo burambuye mu nyuma;
  
- **"*Agaciro k'ama bitcoins yanje ni gato cane ku buryo ntashobora gutegura.*"** → **Ikinyoma**.  ni vyiza kwama utegura imbere y’igihe. Abakunzi bawe birashoboka ko batazi umubare nyawo wa Bitcoin ufise, kandi ivyo ni vyiza. Ariko iyo hari ikintu kigushikiye, ntibokwipfuza kukigarura, naho boba batazi igitigiri nyaco? Agaciro ka Bitcoin gashobora gukura cane uko igihe kigenda kirarenga, ni vyiza rero ko boroherwa no kuronka amahera yawe, no kwirinda kubaha icizigiro c’ikinyoma ku bijanye n’agaciro canke kubasiga barondera ikintu coba kitariho.

- **"*Ba samurarwa banje bazovyiyumvamwo bonyene.*"** → **Ikinyoma**. Bitcoin si nk’ikonti ya banki isanzwe. Ata mabwirizwa atomoye, abakunzi bawe boshobora kutazokwigera bashobora kuronka amahera yawe, canke bakaronka igice gusa. Udakunze amakonti ya banki, aho ibigo vy'imari canke aba notari bavugana n'abagize umuryango iyo upfuye, nta muntu azomenyesha umuryango wawe ivyerekeye Bitcoin Wallet yawe. Ni wewe rero ubwirizwa kubishira mu buryo butomoye mu mugambi wawe w’itongo;
  
- **"*Smart contract ishobora gutwara vyose.*"** → **Ikinyoma**. Smart contract, nka multi-sig Wallet ifise igihe co gufunga, irashobora kuba igice c’umuti, ariko ntizokwigera isubirira umugambi utunganijwe neza, cane cane ku bantu batamenyereye Bitcoin. Ivyo bisubizo vyose bibiri birahuza.

Naho woba utarabikora vyose ubu nyene, ariko wiyemeje kubikora vuba, uba umaze gushika imbere y’ivyo vyose. Uwo mwiyemezo wo kwiga no kugira ico ukoze ni wo mu vy’ukuri uhambaye. Ubu uratahura igituma Bitcoin iserukira ihinduka rihambaye cane ku bijanye n’akazoza kacu ku giti cacu no muri rusangi.


Kuva natanguye kwigisha abantu ivyerekeye Bitcoin, jewe n’ibidukikije twarateye imbere cane. Iryo shure ryarahinduwe ryose kugira ngo ryerekane ivyiza kuruta ibindi vyose Bitcoin ifise muri iki gihe. Haciye urugendo rwiza cane turi kumwe!

### Ni igiki gikurikira kuri Bitcoin?


Turavye umurongo w’igiciro c’ibintu bishingiye ku logarithme, ikintu kimwe kiratomoka: ntitwigera tumenya neza aho turi mu nzinguzingu. Kumbure uriko urasoma iri shure nk’uko nyene igice gishasha c’iterambere kiriko kiratangura, canke kumbure uri hagati mw’isoko ry’idubu. Uko vyogenda kwose, izo nzinguzingu zirabandanya, ata kabuza zigaca ziterwa n’ibintu vyiza bitangaje be n’ugukosorwa kw’ubukazi rimwe na rimwe.


Ariko birenze igiciro, ukura kwa Bitcoin kuratangaje. Ico catanguye nk’umugambi utazwi neza mu ba cypherpunk, caracitse umuhari w’isi yose. Kuva ku nzira ya Silk Road gushika ku kwemerwa n’inzego, Bitcoin yarashinze imizi mu vy’ubutunzi kw’isi yose. Ntibikibonwa nk’igerageza ry’inyuma, ahubwo nk’umugwi w’ubutunzi bukomeye bwakiriwe n’inzego n’abantu amamiliyoni kw’isi yose.


Ukwo guhinduka kw’ukuntu abantu babona ibintu kwaje kumwe n’ugutera imbere kw’ubuhinga gukomeza. Amasakoshi n’ibikorwa remezo biguma bitera imbere. Buri mwaka, harasohoka inyishu nshasha zituma Bitcoin irushiriza gutekana, gukomera no gukoreshwa neza. Muri iki gihe, Bitcoin irakoreshwa cane, irashikira abantu benshi kandi iratahurwa neza kuruta uko vyari bimeze kera.


Ego ni ko, ingorane ziracariho: amategeko, ubuzima bwite, kwemerwa mu buryo bwagutse, no guteza imbere ubutunzi buzunguruka. Ariko igihe cose abanyabubasha bazoguma bakora, bashaka kumenya vyinshi, kandi bagira uruhara, Bitcoin izoguma ari uburyo bukomeye bwo gusubirira uburyo bwa kera bwo gutanga amafaranga.


Ikintu kimwe nta gukeka: Bitcoin izobandanya kubaho; gusohora igice gishasha hafi buri minota 10, ataco arushe, umusi ku wundi, umwaka ku wundi. Tuzobona abakoresha benshi, ama wallet, amahinduka, n’amabwirizwa menshi, intambamyi z’ubuhinga, ingorane, n’ibintu bishasha.


![BTC102-Bitcoin](assets/fr/098.webp)


Uko ingorane zo kw’isi yose zikomeza kandi n’ugutera imbere kw’ibiciro bikaba bigabanya ubushobozi bwo kugura amafaranga y’amahera, Bitcoin izokwegera abantu benshi, abarondera umwidegemvyo w’ivy’amahera n’ubusegaba bw’umuntu ku giti ciwe. Ivyo bizoguma bikomeza Bitcoin no gukwegera abahinga bashasha kugira ngo batere imbere mu bijanye n’imirongo ngenderwako yayo, ivyicaro vyayo vyo gupima, n’ikoreshwa ryayo rya misi yose mu bijanye n’umutekano, ubuzima bwite, n’ubuhinga.


### ni ikihe gikurikira?


Igiteye ubwoba ni uko ihinduka rinini rizoba ari Bitcoin; bizoba ari wewe.

Uzotera imbere mu buryo ukoresha, utahura, no gufatanya na Bitcoin. Kumbure uzogira urukundo nyakuri rwo kubikunda, bihagije kugira ngo utangure kwigisha abandi, winjire mu kibano, canke gusa uzigame ubutunzi bwawe igihe kirekire. Uko inzira yawe yoba iri kwose, Bitcoin izoba yahinduye ukuntu ubona isi; kandi birashoboka ko ari ubucuti ufitaniye n’umwidegemvyo be n’inshingano z’amahera.
