# Exploring the Evolution of Bitcoin
<partId>590acb46-88a6-4c61-8aaa-2afbca3425f6</partId>

This course takes learners on a journey through Bitcoin’s transformation from a payment system to a global financial and technological force. Participants will examine Bitcoin’s institutional adoption, its role as an investment and strategic reserve, and the diverse ways it integrates into the global economy. Technical aspects such as blockchain scalability, transaction costs, and the trilemma of security, decentralization, and scalability are also explored. By the end, students will gain a well-rounded perspective of Bitcoin’s ecosystem, its challenges, and the innovations shaping its future.
