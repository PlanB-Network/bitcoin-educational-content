#### Chapter 6: Banks' Stance on Bitcoin [ORIGINAL]
- Banks see Bitcoin as threat
- Account restrictions

#### Chapter 7: Cryptocurrency Exchanges & Custody [ORIGINAL]
- Exchange selection criteria
- KYC requirements and privacy trade-offs
- Alternatives to KYC (P2P, ATMs, earning BTC)
- Platform types:
  - P2P exchanges (Bisq, RoboSats, Peach, etc.)
  - Bitcoin-only platforms (Relai, Bull Bitcoin, StackinSat)
  - Trading platforms (Bitfinex, Kraken, Bitstamp)
- Exchange security warnings (hacking, seizure, bankruptcy)
- "Not your keys, not your coins"

#### Chapter 8: Wallets, Mining & Development [ORIGINAL]
- **Bitcoin Wallets**:
  - Hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor, Coinkite, etc.)
  - Software wallets (Sparrow, Galoy, etc.)
  - DIY wallets (Seedsigner, Specter)
- **Bitcoin Mining**:
  - How mining works
  - Hardware manufacturers (Bitmain)
  - Mining pools
  - Small vs industrial miners
- **Development**:
  - Bitcoin Core and maintainers (5 in 2025)
  - Layered protocol developers
  - Independent developers
  - BIP process

### Part 4: Bitcoin's Layered Architecture

#### Chapter 9: Extension Layers [ORIGINAL]
- Why layered approach works
- **Lightning Network**:
  - Instant payments
  - Off-chain transactions
  - Coffee shop example
- **Sidechains**:
  - Liquid and RSK
  - Two-way peg concept
- **RGB**:
  - Smart contracts on Bitcoin
  - Single-use Seals
  - Client-side validation
- Layered design philosophy

#### Chapter 10: Merchant Tools [ORIGINAL]
- Options for accepting Bitcoin:
  - Hot wallets for small businesses
  - Advanced payment processors for larger businesses
- **OpenNode**: Custodial, fiat conversion
- **BTCPay Server**: Open-source, self-hosted
- **Swiss Bitcoin Pay**: Balanced approach
- Benefits of accepting Bitcoin:
  - No chargebacks
  - Lower fees
  - Financial autonomy
  - Fixed supply hedge
  - Marketing opportunity

#### Chapter 11: Going Further [ORIGINAL + NEW]
