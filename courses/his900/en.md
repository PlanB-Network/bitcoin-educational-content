# Governance in Bitcoin and its difficulties
<partId>22af11a0-b5dc-4f25-bef3-c44992cbc050</partId>

This course provides a comprehensive overview of Bitcoin and Blockchain technology, exploring their origins, the principles of decentralization and security, and the milestones that shaped their development — including the creation of Ethereum, the ICO bubble, and governance challenges. It also delves into technical mechanisms such as mining, staking, and consensus systems, while analyzing economic and financial phenomena such as Bitcoin halving, its intrinsic value in today’s society, and its potential as a strategic tool in investment portfolios and global reserves.
